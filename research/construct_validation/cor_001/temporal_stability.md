# Temporal Stability

## Purpose

Evaluate whether COR-001 behaves consistently across time as a descriptive market-correlation state construct.

No predictive or economic interpretation is made.

## Yearly Behavior

COR-001 produced valid observations in every year from 2010 through 2025.

Yearly mean correlation varied from 0.1497 in 2017 to 0.4600 in 2010.

High yearly mean correlation periods included:

- 2010: 0.4600
- 2011: 0.4548
- 2020: 0.4043
- 2022: 0.4103

Low yearly mean correlation periods included:

- 2017: 0.1497
- 2024: 0.1966
- 2021: 0.2370

## Coverage Stability

Coverage improved over time as more securities had sufficient historical data.

Mean valid-date coverage:

```text
93.15%
```

Minimum valid-date coverage:

```text
83.30%
```

## Interpretation

Supported by evidence:

COR-001 is temporally stable enough for construct validation and varies across historical periods in a way consistent with a market co-movement construct.

Not evaluated:

Whether temporal variation has predictive or economic value.
