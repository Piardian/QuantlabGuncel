# Cross-Period Consistency

## Purpose

Evaluate whether COR-001 produces usable observations and interpretable state variation across calendar years.

## Summary

COR-001 generated valid raw observations in every evaluated calendar year from 2010 through 2025.

The construct remained within valid correlation bounds in every period.

The construct produced meaningful cross-period variation:

- Lowest yearly mean: 0.1497 in 2017
- Highest yearly mean: 0.4600 in 2010
- 2020 yearly mean: 0.4043
- 2022 yearly mean: 0.4103
