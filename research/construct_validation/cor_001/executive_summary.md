# Executive Summary

COR-001 / CV-001 evaluated whether the implemented Market Correlation construct behaves like a valid construct.

Result:

```text
Supported by evidence
```

The construct generated:

- 4,023 total rows
- 3,963 valid raw observations
- 3,712 normalized observations
- mean raw correlation of 0.3140
- median raw correlation of 0.2983
- raw correlation range from 0.0674 to 0.7301
- mean coverage of 93.15%

COR-001 produced bounded, interpretable, non-constant, temporally varying market co-movement values. Coverage diagnostics were sufficient for construct validation.

Known limitations remain, especially current-universe survivorship risk, Yahoo data limitations, and Pearson correlation's inability to capture nonlinear or tail dependence.

No predictive, trading, alpha, profitability, economic value, or production-deployment claim is made.

COR-001 is ready for MI-001 after human approval.
