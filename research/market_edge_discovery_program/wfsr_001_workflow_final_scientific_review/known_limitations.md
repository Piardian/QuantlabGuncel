# Known Limitations

- The reference universe is current-constituent based and not survivorship-free.
- OOS coverage is short relative to the full reference sample.
- Economic validation used fixed equal-weight workflow abstractions.
- Transaction costs, slippage, taxes, capacity and liquidity constraints were not modeled.
- Conflict-region evidence is unavailable because CSM_HIGH x TSM_LOW was absent or insufficient.
- No paper trading or live trading validation has been performed.
- The workflow remains a research workflow, not a production system.
