# WFSR-001: Workflow Final Scientific Review

## Scope

WFSR-001 synthesizes the complete scientific evidence chain for the CSM-001 x TSM-001 nested composite workflow.

Included studies:

- CIP-001: Construct Interaction Study
- CWP-001: Composite Workflow Protocol Registration
- CWS-001: Composite Workflow Scientific Evaluation
- CWF-001: Final Composite Workflow Review
- WOR-001: Workflow OOS Reproducibility Protocol
- WOR-002: Workflow OOS Reproducibility Audit
- WEV-001: Workflow Economic Validation Protocol
- WEV-002: Workflow Economic Validation

No new empirical analysis was performed.

## Final Classification

**Scientifically Supported, OOS-Reproduced, Partially Economically Supported Nested Composite Workflow**

## Supported Claims

- CSM_HIGH is nested inside TSM_HIGH in the evaluated reference sample.
- The nested relationship reproduced out of sample after 2025-12-30.
- TSM_HIGH is materially broader than CSM_HIGH.
- CSM-001 and TSM-001 are not fully redundant.
- CSM-001 and TSM-001 are not independent equivalents.
- UC-3 is economically supported under the fixed WEV-001 workflow definition: CSM leadership subset inside TSM_HIGH outperformed the broader TSM-positive non-CSM region in the evaluated research workflow.

## Partially Supported Claims

- The workflow has economic utility.

Reason:

Economic support is concentrated in UC-3. UC-1 and UC-2 were not supported because CSM_HIGH was already nested inside TSM_HIGH, so gating or weighting did not change the selected set. UC-4 was inconclusive because the conflict region was absent or insufficient.

## Not Supported Claims

- The workflow is a new construct.
- The workflow is production-ready.
- The workflow authorizes deployment.
- The workflow establishes unrestricted alpha.
- The workflow has been validated with execution costs, capacity, slippage, taxes or live trading.

## Inconclusive Claims

- Conflict-region economic utility.
- Real portfolio implementation value.
- Capacity-sensitive performance.
- Cost-adjusted deployability.
- Live or paper trading behavior.

## Final Assessment

The CSM-001 x TSM-001 workflow is scientifically meaningful as a nested research workflow. The strongest evidence supports using CSM-001 as a leadership subset inside the broader TSM-positive state.

The workflow is eligible for future preregistered execution-realism research.

It is not eligible for production deployment based on the current evidence.
