# Construct Limitations

- CD-001 does not evaluate whether CSM-001 predicts future returns.
- CD-001 does not evaluate economic value or implementation costs.
- 252 and 21 trading-day conventions are approximations of 12 months and 1 month.
- Adjusted close data quality can affect reproducibility.
- Universe selection can materially affect cross-sectional ranks.
- The top-decile flag is a construct state, not a trading signal.
- Long-only and long-short applications are not defined here.
- Sector-neutral, residual, volatility-adjusted and industry momentum variants are excluded from CSM-001.
