# Construct Assumptions

- Adjusted close is the required price input.
- Trading-day offsets are row-based within the provided daily price panel.
- The universe is predefined before empirical work.
- The construct is cross-sectional, not time-series.
- The construct measures relative prior performance, not absolute market direction.
- The one-month skip period is represented as 21 trading days.
- The twelve-month formation anchor is represented as 252 trading days.
- Ranking is performed independently for each valid date.
- Missing or non-positive prices make a security ineligible for that date.
- CSM-001 does not include sector, industry, volatility, liquidity, fundamental or risk-adjusted inputs.
