# Alternative Definitions Review

## Rejected For CSM-001

### 6-1 Momentum

Rejected because CSM-001 is intended to begin with the more canonical 12-1 academic baseline.

### 3-1 Momentum

Rejected because it may capture shorter-term continuation and higher-turnover behavior rather than the canonical intermediate-horizon construct.

### Industry Momentum

Rejected because it requires industry classification data and represents a different construct family.

### Residual Momentum

Rejected because it requires factor-model residualization and introduces additional modeling choices.

### Volatility-Adjusted Momentum

Rejected because it blends momentum with risk normalization and is less suitable as the first baseline construct.

## Important Boundary

Rejected alternatives are not judged inferior. They are simply not selected for CSM-001. They may become future constructs if preregistered separately.
