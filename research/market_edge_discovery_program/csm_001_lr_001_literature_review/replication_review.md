# Replication Review

## Strong Replication Themes

- Momentum has been replicated across multiple samples, periods and markets.
- Momentum is included in standard factor datasets and empirical asset-pricing tests.
- Broad research infrastructure exists for testing momentum variants.

## Weaknesses and Replication Risks

- Returns can decline out-of-sample and post-publication.
- Implementation details materially affect results.
- Microcap inclusion, weighting scheme, rebalancing and costs can alter conclusions.
- Momentum can experience large crash episodes.
- Multiple-testing concerns require stricter statistical standards.

## Factor-Zoo Context

Harvey, Liu and Zhu argue that many claimed factors are likely false under conventional thresholds. Hou, Xue and Zhang and Open Asset Pricing-style replication projects reinforce the need for robust testing.

## LR-001 Conclusion

Momentum is not dismissed by replication critiques, but it must be validated under preregistered definitions, fixed universes, out-of-sample tests and economic-cost assumptions.
