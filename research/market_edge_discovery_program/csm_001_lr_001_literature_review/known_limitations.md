# Known Limitations

- Momentum can crash sharply during market rebounds after stress periods.
- Turnover can make implementation cost-sensitive.
- Published anomaly returns may decay after discovery.
- Many momentum variants create data-snooping risk.
- Long-short academic evidence does not automatically validate long-only implementation.
- Equal-weight results may differ from value-weight results.
- Microcap and illiquid stocks can distort empirical findings.
- Momentum is not a complete trading strategy; it is a construct family.
