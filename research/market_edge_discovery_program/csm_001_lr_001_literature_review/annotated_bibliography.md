# Annotated Bibliography

## Jegadeesh and Titman (1993)

Foundational U.S. equity paper documenting intermediate-horizon winner-minus-loser continuation. Central source for canonical cross-sectional momentum.

Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1993.tb04702.x

## Carhart (1997)

Introduces a four-factor mutual fund performance model including momentum. Important for momentum as a standard factor-control variable.

Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1997.tb03808.x

## Daniel, Hirshleifer and Subrahmanyam (1998)

Behavioural model based on overconfidence and biased self-attribution. Provides one theoretical explanation for momentum and reversal.

Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/0022-1082.00077

## Hong and Stein (1999)

Gradual information diffusion model. Important behavioural explanation for underreaction and continuation.

Source: https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/jfinan/v54y1999i6p2143-2184.html

## Moskowitz, Ooi and Pedersen (2012)

Documents time-series momentum across futures markets. Related but distinct from cross-sectional equity momentum.

Source: https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v104y2012i2p228-250.html

## Asness, Moskowitz and Pedersen (2013)

Documents value and momentum across markets and asset classes. Important for broad factor evidence and interaction with value.

Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12021

## Fama and French International Evidence

Provides global factor evidence and regional qualifications. Important for understanding cross-country robustness.

Source: https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v105y2012i3p457-472.html

## Daniel and Moskowitz (2016)

Momentum crash literature. Important warning that momentum can be state-dependent and left-tail exposed.

Source: https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v122y2016i2p221-247.html

## Harvey, Liu and Zhu (2016)

Multiple-testing and factor-zoo critique. Important methodological constraint for all later validation.

Source: https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/29/1/5/1843824

## McLean and Pontiff (2016)

Studies out-of-sample and post-publication decay of published predictors. Important for economic realism and decay risk.

Source: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2156623

## Hou, Xue and Zhang / Open Asset Pricing

Large-scale replication infrastructure and stricter anomaly evaluation. Important for reproducibility discipline.

Sources:

- https://academic.oup.com/rfs/advance-article/doi/10.1093/rfs/hhy131/5236964
- https://www.openassetpricing.com/
