# Definition Taxonomy

| definition_family | description | common_elements | stage_note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Canonical cross-sectional momentum | Ranks stocks within a universe by prior returns and studies relative winners versus losers. | fixed universe; prior-return formation period; cross-sectional ranking; winner/loser portfolios | Exact lookback and ranking rules must be frozen later in CD-001, not LR-001. |
| 12-1 momentum | Uses trailing 12-month return excluding most recent month as a common academic convention. | skip-month convention; medium-horizon prior return | Reviewed as literature convention only, not selected as CSM-001 definition. |
| 3-12 month formation momentum | Broad family of medium-horizon formation and holding periods examined in foundational studies. | medium-horizon continuation; relative winners and losers | Reviewed as taxonomy only. |
| Industry / residual / risk-adjusted momentum variants | Variants attempt to isolate industry effects, residual returns, or risk-adjusted signals. | adjusted ranking inputs; alternative comparators | Potential later literature branches; not selected in LR-001. |
| Intermediate-horizon momentum | Emphasizes older portions of prior returns as potentially more explanatory than recent returns. | intermediate prior-return window; definition challenge | Important for CD-001 decision but not resolved in LR-001. |
