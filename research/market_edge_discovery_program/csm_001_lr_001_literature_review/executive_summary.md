# Executive Summary

CSM-001 / LR-001 reviewed Cross-Sectional Momentum as an expected-return mechanism.

Conclusion: **the literature is sufficiently mature to justify proceeding to CD-001.**

Established evidence supports cross-sectional momentum as a serious and widely studied edge-mechanism family. The strongest consensus centers on ranking assets by medium-horizon prior returns and studying relative winners versus losers.

The literature does not justify any specific production rule, parameter choice, long-only implementation or trading strategy. It also highlights major risks: crash episodes, turnover costs, post-publication decay, definition proliferation, multiple-testing risk and universe sensitivity.

Next stage: CD-001 must freeze one exact construct definition before any empirical testing.
