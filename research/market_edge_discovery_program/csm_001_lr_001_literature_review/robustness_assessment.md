# Robustness Assessment

## More Robust Dimensions

- Intermediate-horizon prior-return ranking is widely studied.
- Winner-minus-loser constructions are central to the literature.
- Momentum appears in multiple markets and periods, though not uniformly.

## Less Robust Dimensions

- Exact lookback and holding periods.
- Long-only adaptation.
- Net returns after costs.
- Performance in crash and rebound regimes.
- Small-cap and low-liquidity implementation.
- Post-publication persistence.

## Required Future Robustness Tests

- Predefined sample splits.
- Year-by-year stability.
- Universe robustness.
- Cost and turnover sensitivity.
- Crash-regime analysis.
- Survivorship-free reconstruction where available.
