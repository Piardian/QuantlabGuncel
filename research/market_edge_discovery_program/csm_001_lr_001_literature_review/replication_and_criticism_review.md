# Replication and Criticism Review

## Main Findings

- Cross-sectional momentum has stronger support than many factor zoo candidates, but it is not immune to replication, implementation, and post-publication concerns.
- Multiple-testing concerns require stricter future validation standards.
- Post-publication decay means historical academic evidence cannot be treated as current production alpha.
- Large-scale anomaly replications support a skeptical standard for all future MEDP claims.

## Boundary

This review does not reject or validate any implementable CSM strategy. It only identifies methodological requirements for later stages.
