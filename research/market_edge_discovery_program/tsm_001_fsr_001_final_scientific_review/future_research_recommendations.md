# Future Research Recommendations

Evidence-justified future research:

- Compare TSM-001 against other validated risk constructs in the Construct Comparison Program.
- Re-evaluate TSM-001 on survivorship-free historical membership data if available.
- Test whether TSM-001 adds incremental volatility or drawdown-risk information beyond MR, VOL, BRD and COR constructs.
- If economic utility remains important, perform a separate preregistered production-grade EV study with costs, turnover and implementation constraints.

No new hypothesis should be inferred without preregistration.
