# Executive Summary

RSM-001 / CD-001 is complete.

Frozen construct:

**Fama-French 3-Factor Standardized 12-1 Residual Momentum Rank**

RSM-001 uses monthly excess returns, FF3 residualization, a 36-month rolling regression, 12-1 residual aggregation and residual volatility standardization.

The construct is a cross-sectional ranking construct with TOP_DECILE, BOTTOM_DECILE, MIDDLE and INVALID states.

No implementation, backtest, predictive claim or economic claim was made.
