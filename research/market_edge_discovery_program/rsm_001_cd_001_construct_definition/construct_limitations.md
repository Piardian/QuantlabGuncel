# Construct Limitations

- Residuals depend on the selected Fama-French 3-factor model.
- Omitted factors may remain in residual returns.
- Monthly data reduces timing resolution.
- Securities with limited history will be excluded.
- Residual volatility standardization blends momentum measurement with risk normalization.
- Public factor data must be obtained and aligned correctly.
- No predictive validity is implied by the definition.
