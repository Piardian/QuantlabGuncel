# Construct Maturity Report

## Maturity Assessment

CSM-001 has completed all research lifecycle stages from RP-001 through EV-001 and is ready for FSR-001.

## Maturity Level

**High, with explicit data-scope and implementation limitations.**

## Why High

- Literature foundation is strong.
- Construct definition is canonical and reproducible.
- Implementation is deterministic.
- Construct behavior is internally coherent.
- Mechanism is empirically supported at the construct-behavior level.
- Predictive validation is supported under predefined horizons.
- Economic validation is partially supported under one fixed workflow.

## Why Not Complete Production Maturity

- Survivorship-free historical constituents were not used.
- Execution realism remains simplified.
- Market impact, capacity, taxes and liquidity constraints were not modeled.
- No live or paper trading validation exists.
- EV-001 tested one fixed workflow only.
