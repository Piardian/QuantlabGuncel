# Recommended Applications

## Supported Research Applications

- Use as the first official Market Edge Discovery Program edge construct.
- Use as a canonical cross-sectional momentum baseline.
- Use as a relative leadership sensor in future construct comparison work.
- Use as a benchmark for alternative momentum definitions after preregistration.
- Use as an input to future economic validation studies with explicitly frozen workflows.

## Non-Recommended Applications

- Do not treat CSM-001 as production-ready.
- Do not claim universal alpha generation.
- Do not deploy as a standalone trading strategy from current evidence.
- Do not generalize beyond the evaluated current-constituent U.S. equity universe.
- Do not infer capacity, tax efficiency or live execution behavior.
