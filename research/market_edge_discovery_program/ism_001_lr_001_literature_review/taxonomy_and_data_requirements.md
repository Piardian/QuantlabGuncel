# Taxonomy And Data Requirements

Industry Momentum is more data-sensitive than raw stock momentum.

Required future CD/IM decisions:

- Taxonomy provider.
- Number of groups.
- Point-in-time versus static classification.
- Classification effective date.
- Handling classification changes.
- Minimum securities per industry.
- Equal-weighted or value-weighted industry return.
- Treatment of missing or stale industry labels.

Major risk:

Using current classifications for historical periods can create look-ahead bias.
