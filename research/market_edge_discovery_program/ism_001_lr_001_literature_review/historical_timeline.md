# Historical Timeline

## 1993

Jegadeesh and Titman document intermediate-horizon individual-stock momentum, creating the foundation for later momentum decomposition studies.

## 1999

Moskowitz and Grinblatt publish "Do Industries Explain Momentum?", documenting industry momentum and arguing that industry components explain much of individual stock momentum.

## 2001

Grundy and Martin reexamine momentum sources and argue that industry momentum alone does not fully explain momentum rewards.

## 2000s

Industry information diffusion and lead-lag research expands the theoretical explanation for group-level return predictability.

## 2010s-2020s

Industry, factor and stock momentum are increasingly studied as related but distinct forms of momentum. Replication and post-publication robustness become more important.
