# Replication Review

Industry Momentum has replication support but less universal standardization than canonical cross-sectional momentum.

Replication challenges:

- Historical industry membership.
- Industry classification changes.
- Industry definitions differ across datasets.
- Sample periods and universes differ.
- Equal-weighted and value-weighted implementations can produce different results.

LR-001 conclusion:

Replication feasibility is acceptable, but CD-001 must prioritize reproducibility over theoretical elegance.
