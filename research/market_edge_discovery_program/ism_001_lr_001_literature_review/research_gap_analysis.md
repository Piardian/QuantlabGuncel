# Research Gap Analysis

Open questions before empirical work:

1. Which taxonomy can be implemented reproducibly in this repository?
2. Can point-in-time membership be obtained?
3. Should ISM rank industries directly or assign industry scores to stocks?
4. How much does ISM overlap with CSM-001?
5. Is the mechanism industry persistence, intra-industry diffusion, or broad sector rotation?
6. Does the construct retain predictive information after accounting for CSM/RSM?
7. Is any predictive evidence economically usable after costs and turnover?
