# Known Limitations

- Industry definitions are not universal.
- Point-in-time industry membership may be difficult to obtain.
- Current classifications can introduce look-ahead bias.
- Industry returns may be dominated by large firms under value weighting.
- Equal weighting can increase small-stock and liquidity exposure.
- Industry momentum may overlap heavily with CSM.
- Evidence does not automatically transfer to long-only stock selection.
- Transaction costs and turnover belong to EV-001, not LR-001.
