# Canonical Definitions

The literature does not provide a single universally fixed ISM definition.

Common operational forms include:

## Industry Portfolio Momentum

Rank industry portfolios by trailing industry returns and classify/buy winner industries versus loser industries.

## Parent-Industry Signal

Assign each stock the momentum score of its industry group.

## Industry-Adjusted Momentum

Separate stock-specific momentum from industry component momentum.

## Lead-Lag Industry Momentum

Use returns of economically related industries or leader firms to explain later returns in related stocks or industries.

## Sector Momentum

Use broader sector classifications instead of finer industry groups.

CD-001 must choose one definition and freeze it.
