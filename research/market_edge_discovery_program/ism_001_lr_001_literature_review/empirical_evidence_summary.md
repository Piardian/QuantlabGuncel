# Empirical Evidence Summary

## Strong / Moderate Evidence

- Industry momentum is documented in foundational U.S. equity research.
- Industry-level return components can be empirically important in explaining individual stock momentum.
- Lead-lag and information diffusion effects provide supporting empirical context.

## Conflicting Evidence

- Some studies argue industry momentum does not fully explain stock momentum.
- Stock-specific or residual components can remain important after industry adjustment.
- Business-cycle and risk-based explanations compete with behavioral interpretations.

## Limited Evidence

- Direct evidence depends on industry taxonomy, weighting method and sample period.
- International and post-publication robustness is less standardized than canonical cross-sectional momentum.
