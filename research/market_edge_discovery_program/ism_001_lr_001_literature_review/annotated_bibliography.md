# Annotated Bibliography

## Moskowitz and Grinblatt, 1999

"Do Industries Explain Momentum?" is the primary academic anchor. It documents industry momentum and argues that industry components explain a major part of individual stock momentum.

## Grundy and Martin, 2001

Reexamines momentum rewards and challenges the idea that industry effects alone explain momentum.

## Hou, 2007

Studies industry information diffusion and lead-lag effects in stock returns.

## Hong, Torous and Valkanov

Develops gradual information diffusion across asset markets and industries as a mechanism for cross-asset predictability.

## Chordia and Shivakumar / Related Autocorrelation Literature

Links momentum and industry momentum behavior to autocorrelation and macro/time-varying expected return explanations.

## Practitioner Sector Momentum Literature

Useful for implementation awareness but must be separated from academic evidence.
