# Theoretical Foundations

## Behavioral Underreaction

Industry news may be incorporated gradually if investors underreact to common industry-level information.

## Limited Attention

Investors may focus on headline firms, large firms or their own industry specialization, causing delayed price adjustment in related firms.

## Information Diffusion

Industry-related firms share economic exposures. News can propagate across firms and industries with a lag.

## Common Risk / Macro Exposure

Industries may carry persistent exposure to macroeconomic or business-cycle conditions. Industry momentum may partly reflect changing expected returns rather than mispricing.

## Autocorrelation Structure

Industry returns can display own-autocorrelation. Some research links industry momentum profits to this autocorrelation rather than cross-autocorrelation.
