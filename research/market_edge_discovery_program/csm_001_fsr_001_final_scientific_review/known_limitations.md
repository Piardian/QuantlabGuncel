# Known Limitations

- Current-constituent universe creates survivorship-risk limitations.
- Yahoo adjusted-close data introduces external data quality dependency.
- Economic validation used one fixed workflow only.
- Benchmark was equal-weight eligible universe, not cap-weighted market index.
- Transaction cost was fixed at 10 bps and not calibrated to live execution.
- Market impact, liquidity, tax, borrow and capacity constraints were not evaluated.
- No paper-trading or live-trading validation exists.
- FSR-001 makes no production recommendation.
