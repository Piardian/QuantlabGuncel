# Recommended Applications

## Recommended Research Applications

- Include CSM-001 in the validated edge construct library.
- Use CSM-001 as a canonical cross-sectional momentum baseline.
- Compare CSM-001 with future edge constructs under preregistered comparison protocols.
- Use CSM-001 as an input candidate for future controlled portfolio research.
- Use CSM-001 as a reference construct for studying relative leadership and winner-state persistence.

## Non-Recommended Applications

- Do not treat CSM-001 as production-ready.
- Do not deploy CSM-001 as a standalone trading system.
- Do not claim universal alpha.
- Do not generalize beyond the evaluated data scope.
- Do not ignore drawdown, turnover, capacity or implementation constraints.
