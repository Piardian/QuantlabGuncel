# Future Research Recommendations

## Scientifically Justified Next Research

- Survivorship-free reconstruction and validation.
- Comparison against cap-weighted and sector-neutral benchmarks.
- Transaction cost and turnover sensitivity under preregistered assumptions.
- Capacity and liquidity feasibility study.
- Paper-trading reproducibility study.
- Construct comparison against future validated edge constructs.

## Not Justified From Current Evidence Alone

- Immediate production deployment.
- Parameter optimization.
- Threshold tuning.
- Claiming universal alpha.
- Replacing risk management or execution modeling.
