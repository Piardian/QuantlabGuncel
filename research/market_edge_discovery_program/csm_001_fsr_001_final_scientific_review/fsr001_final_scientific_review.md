# CSM-001 / FSR-001 Final Scientific Review

## Purpose

Produce the final scientific assessment of CSM-001 by integrating frozen evidence from RP-001 through CC-001.

FSR-001 generates no new empirical evidence. It does not modify the construct, optimize parameters, redesign strategy logic, or recommend production deployment.

## Construct

**Construct ID:** CSM-001

**Construct Name:** Canonical 12-1 Cross-Sectional Momentum State

**Frozen Definition:** Cross-sectional percentile rank of prior adjusted-price return from `t-252` to `t-21`, plus top-decile state.

## Final Scientific Classification

**Primary Category:** Edge Construct

**Subcategory:** Cross-sectional relative leadership / intermediate-horizon winner-state construct

**Scientific Maturity:** High, with limitations

**Evidence Strength:** Moderate to strong

**Final Status:** Completed construct within the Market Edge Discovery Program

**Production Readiness:** Not supported

## Integrated Evidence Assessment

CSM-001 completed the full scientific lifecycle from research prioritization through construct classification. The accumulated evidence supports the construct as a scientifically mature edge construct with statistically supported predictive validity and partially supported economic utility under one fixed workflow.

The evidence does not support production deployment, universal superiority, survivorship-free robustness, capacity robustness, or live execution readiness.

## Supported Claims

- CSM-001 is scientifically justified as an independent research construct.
- CSM-001 is grounded in mature cross-sectional momentum literature.
- CSM-001 has a deterministic and reproducible canonical 12-1 implementation.
- CSM-001 behaves as a persistent cross-sectional relative leadership / winner-state construct.
- CSM-001 contains statistical predictive information at predefined horizons under the evaluated data scope.

## Partially Supported Claims

- Construct validity is partially supported because implementation consistency is strong, but the evaluated universe is not survivorship-free.
- Mechanism validity is partially supported because relative leadership persistence is supported, but broad-market-direction disentanglement was not fully established.
- Economic utility is partially supported because the fixed top-decile workflow outperformed the equal-weight eligible-universe benchmark after fixed transaction costs, but maximum drawdown was not improved.

## Not Supported Claims

- Production readiness is not supported.
- Standalone deployment is not supported.
- Universal alpha generation is not supported.
- Superior behavior under realistic market impact, capacity, tax and live execution constraints is not supported.

## Inconclusive Claims

- Survivorship-free robustness remains inconclusive.
- Robustness outside the evaluated U.S. large-cap-style universe remains inconclusive.
- Performance versus cap-weighted benchmarks remains inconclusive.
- Capacity and liquidity-constrained implementation remains inconclusive.
- Live or paper-trading reproducibility remains inconclusive.

## Final Assessment

CSM-001 is a completed and scientifically defensible **Edge Construct** suitable for future research comparison, controlled economic workflow studies, and systematic construct-library inclusion.

It should not be treated as production-ready without a separate production-readiness and implementation-reality program.
