# Hypothesis Registry

## Primary Hypothesis

H1:

The CSM-001 x TSM-001 composite workflow provides complementary state information because CSM-001 identifies narrower cross-sectional leadership inside the broader positive own-trend region captured by TSM-001.

## Null Hypothesis

H0:

The CSM-001 x TSM-001 composite workflow does not provide meaningful information beyond the individual frozen constructs.

## Secondary Hypotheses

H2:

The CSM_HIGH x TSM_HIGH state is a stable nested state rather than a sample-specific artifact.

H3:

The CSM_NOT_HIGH x TSM_HIGH state represents a broad positive own-trend region that is distinct from cross-sectional leadership.

H4:

Direct conflict states, especially CSM_HIGH x TSM_LOW, are too rare to support strong workflow conclusions unless sufficient observations exist in the frozen sample.

H5:

Incremental workflow information must be observable across multiple years and not concentrated in a single historical period.

## Predefined Rejection Conditions

The composite workflow is not supported if:

- Incremental information is negligible after conditioning on individual construct states.
- The observed nesting relationship is unstable across time.
- Workflow conclusions are driven by one year.
- Workflow conclusions are driven by a rare conflict state.
- Results cannot be reproduced from frozen inputs.

## Interpretation Limits

Successful support for H1 does not imply:

- Trading edge
- Alpha
- Portfolio utility
- Production readiness
- Causal mechanism

Failure to support H1 does not reduce the independent scientific status of CSM-001 or TSM-001.
