# Decision Framework

CWS-001 must assign exactly one final decision.

## Workflow Supported

Use only if:

- The composite workflow provides clear incremental information beyond individual states.
- Evidence is stable across time.
- The workflow has an interpretable scientific structure.
- No conclusion is driven by one year or one rare state.

## Workflow Partially Supported

Use if:

- The workflow structure is scientifically meaningful.
- Some incremental information exists.
- Important limitations remain.
- Evidence supports further study but not strong classification.

## Workflow Not Supported

Use if:

- CSM-001 and TSM-001 do not add meaningful information beyond each other in workflow form.
- The interaction is effectively redundant.
- State structure is unstable or not reproducible.

## Inconclusive

Use if:

- Data are insufficient.
- Conflict regions are too sparse.
- Results are unstable.
- Evidence cannot discriminate between complementarity and redundancy.

## Forbidden Decision Language

CWS-001 must not conclude:

- Production ready
- Deployable
- Alpha confirmed
- Superior strategy
- Optimized workflow
- Trading rule approved
