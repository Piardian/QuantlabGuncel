# Historical Timeline

Early asset-pricing literature:

Firm fundamentals such as earnings, book equity and investment enter empirical return prediction and valuation research.

2013:

Novy-Marx publishes the gross profitability premium, emphasizing gross profits-to-assets as a powerful cross-sectional characteristic.

2015:

Fama and French publish the five-factor asset-pricing model including operating profitability and investment factors.

2015 onward:

Hou, Xue and Zhang's q-factor model and related investment CAPM work emphasize profitability and investment as central expected-return mechanisms.

2019:

Asness, Frazzini and Pedersen publish Quality Minus Junk, defining quality through profitability, growth, safety and payout.

2020s:

Open-source and replication projects expand systematic testing of profitability and quality signals across many firm characteristics.
