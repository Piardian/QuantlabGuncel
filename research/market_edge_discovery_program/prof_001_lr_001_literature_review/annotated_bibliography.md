# Annotated Bibliography

Novy-Marx (2013), *The Other Side of Value: The Gross Profitability Premium*.

Central gross profitability paper. Documents gross profits-to-assets as a major cross-sectional return predictor.

Fama and French (2015), *A Five-Factor Asset Pricing Model*.

Introduces profitability and investment factors into a widely used asset-pricing model.

Hou, Xue and Zhang, q-factor / investment CAPM literature.

Connects profitability and investment to expected returns through investment-based asset-pricing theory.

Asness, Frazzini and Pedersen (2019), *Quality Minus Junk*.

Defines quality as a broad set of characteristics including profitability, growth, safety and payout.

Chen and Zimmermann, *Open Source Cross-Sectional Asset Pricing*.

Provides large-scale replication infrastructure and evidence for many cross-sectional predictors, including profitability-related characteristics.

Harvey, Liu and Zhu.

Important multiple-testing and factor-zoo critique relevant to profitability/quality research discipline.

McLean and Pontiff.

Important anomaly decay and post-publication evidence relevant to factor persistence.
