# Quality Minus Junk Review

Quality Minus Junk broadens the construct beyond profitability.

Quality dimensions commonly include:

- Profitability.
- Growth.
- Safety.
- Payout.

Scientific strengths:

- Broad global evidence.
- Strong practitioner and academic relevance.
- Captures multidimensional firm quality.

Research caveats:

- Composite construction reduces construct purity.
- Multiple components introduce researcher degrees of freedom.
- CD-001 must avoid arbitrary weighting or post-hoc component selection.

LR-001 implication:

QMJ is a valuable literature anchor, but PROF-001 should freeze a composite only if the research objective is broad firm quality rather than narrow profitability.
