# Research Gap Analysis

Open questions for this project:

- Should PROF-001 measure narrow profitability or broad quality?
- If narrow, should it use gross profitability or operating profitability?
- Can point-in-time accounting data be obtained?
- If not, what conservative lag is scientifically acceptable?
- How should financial firms and negative book equity be handled?
- Does profitability provide incremental information beyond CSM-001 and risk constructs?
- Does profitability remain predictive after controlling for value and investment?

These questions belong to CD-001 and later stages.
