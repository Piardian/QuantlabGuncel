# Known Limitations

- Profitability has many competing definitions.
- Quality composites can create excessive researcher degrees of freedom.
- Accounting data may be restated.
- Publication lag is difficult without filing dates.
- Sector differences can distort comparisons.
- Financial firms may not be comparable to industrial firms.
- Negative denominators require explicit policy.
- Empirical evidence may vary across sample period, weighting and universe.
