# Theoretical Foundations

## Production / Investment Theory

Investment-based models connect expected returns to firms' investment and profitability decisions. All else equal, expected profitability matters for valuation and required returns.

## Mispricing / Behavioural View

Investors may underappreciate the persistence and value of high profitability, especially when profitable firms look expensive using simple valuation multiples.

## Quality Demand View

High-quality firms should command higher prices because investors value profitability, growth, safety and payout. A quality premium can arise if the price of quality is too low.

## Risk-Based View

Profitability may proxy for exposure to systematic risks, though this is not universally settled.

## CD-001 Implication

The construct definition should preserve mechanism clarity by avoiding unnecessary mixtures of profitability, value, safety and growth.
