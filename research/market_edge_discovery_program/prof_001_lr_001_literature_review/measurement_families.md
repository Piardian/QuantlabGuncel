# Measurement Families

## Gross Profitability

Typical concept:

```text
gross_profitability = gross_profits / total_assets
```

Strength:

Simple, interpretable and directly tied to productive efficiency.

## Operating Profitability

Typical concept:

Operating income or operating profits scaled by book equity or related accounting base.

Strength:

Embedded in the Fama-French five-factor tradition.

## Return On Equity / Return On Assets

Typical concept:

Net income scaled by equity or assets.

Strength:

Intuitive profitability ratio.

Risk:

Net income can include more accounting noise and transitory items.

## Cash-Flow Profitability

Typical concept:

Operating cash flow scaled by assets, equity or enterprise value.

Strength:

May be less accrual-sensitive.

## Quality Composite

Typical concept:

Composite score over profitability, growth, safety and payout.

Strength:

Captures broad firm quality.

Risk:

Lower construct purity and harder mechanism interpretation.
