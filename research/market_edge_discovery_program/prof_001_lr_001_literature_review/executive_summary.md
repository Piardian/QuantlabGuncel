# Executive Summary

PROF-001 / LR-001 is complete.

Main conclusion:

Profitability / Quality is a scientifically mature construct family with strong literature support.

However, the family contains multiple distinct constructs. Gross profitability, operating profitability and QMJ-style broad quality should not be treated as interchangeable.

CD-001 should freeze one narrow, interpretable and reproducible construct. Data integrity, publication lag and accounting restatement policy must be solved before implementation.

No construct was frozen and no predictive or economic claim was made.
