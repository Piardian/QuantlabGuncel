# Replication Review

Profitability and quality signals appear in major replication-oriented asset-pricing datasets.

Important replication considerations:

- Accounting data must be lagged or point-in-time.
- Factor definitions must be faithfully reproduced.
- Universe and weighting choices affect measured evidence.
- Publication decay and multiple testing remain relevant.
- Composite quality signals can be harder to replicate exactly than single accounting ratios.

LR-001 conclusion:

The family is replication-worthy, but CD-001 must choose a definition with high reproducibility and low researcher discretion.
