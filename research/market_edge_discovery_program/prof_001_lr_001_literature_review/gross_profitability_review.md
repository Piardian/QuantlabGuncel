# Gross Profitability Review

Gross profitability is one of the cleanest profitability definitions in the literature.

Core idea:

Firms that generate high gross profits relative to assets have stronger economic productivity.

Scientific strengths:

- Simple formula.
- High interpretability.
- Strong association with the Novy-Marx profitability literature.
- Less affected by downstream accounting choices than net income measures.

Research caveats:

- Requires reliable revenue, cost of goods sold and total asset data.
- Accounting definitions can differ across firms and sectors.
- Financial firms may require special treatment.
- Publication lag must be handled conservatively.
