# Data Quality Requirements

PROF-001 requires:

- Financial statement fields.
- Fiscal period dates.
- Filing dates or conservative publication lag.
- Price and market capitalization data.
- Security identifier mapping.
- Delisting and survivorship controls where possible.
- Restatement policy.

If point-in-time accounting data is unavailable:

A conservative lag policy may be used only if explicitly frozen in CD-001 and acknowledged as a limitation.

Unsafe practices:

- Using modern restated values as if known historically.
- Aligning annual statements to fiscal period end without publication lag.
- Selecting accounting definitions after seeing results.
