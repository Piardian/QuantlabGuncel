# Operating Profitability Review

Operating profitability is central to the Fama-French five-factor framework.

Scientific strengths:

- Major asset-pricing model support.
- Explicitly incorporated into widely used factor datasets.
- Relates profitability to expected returns jointly with investment.

Research caveats:

- Exact formula details matter.
- Book equity and operating income definitions can be vendor-dependent.
- Negative book equity and financial-sector observations require explicit handling.
- Strong interaction with investment factor complicates isolated interpretation.
