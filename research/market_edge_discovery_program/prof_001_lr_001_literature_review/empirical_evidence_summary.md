# Empirical Evidence Summary

Strong evidence:

- Profitability is a major asset-pricing characteristic.
- Gross profitability and operating profitability are supported by influential academic literature.
- Quality composites have documented historical evidence across countries.

Moderate evidence:

- Profitability characteristics appear in replicated asset-pricing signal libraries.
- Profitability is long-only compatible and economically interpretable.

Conflicting or limited evidence:

- Results vary by definition.
- Equal-weighted and value-weighted evidence can differ.
- Small-cap and microcap effects can influence results.
- Accounting data timing can materially affect validity.
- Multiple-testing and publication-decay concerns apply.

Unsupported at LR stage:

- Any claim that PROF-001 works in this repository.
- Any claim that one profitability metric is superior.
- Any production deployment conclusion.
