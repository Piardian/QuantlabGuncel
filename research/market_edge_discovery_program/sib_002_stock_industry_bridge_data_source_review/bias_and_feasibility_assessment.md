# Bias And Feasibility Assessment

## Highest-Risk Bias

Current-classification look-ahead is the dominant risk.

Any source that maps a 2012 ticker using a 2026 industry label is invalid for confirmatory CIP research.

## Survivorship Risk

A bridge must include delisted and historical ticker/security identifier treatment where possible.

If the CSM universe itself remains current-constituent based, that inherited limitation must be reported separately.

## Taxonomy Translation Risk

ISM-001 is based on Ken French 49 industry portfolios.

GICS, NAICS or other taxonomies are not automatically equivalent.

Using them would require a preregistered translation layer.

## Feasibility Assessment

Preferred:

- CRSP/Compustat historical SIC to Ken French 49 mapping.

Secondary:

- GICS History only if a validated translation to FF49 is separately defined.

Rejected:

- Current metadata.
- Manual mapping.
