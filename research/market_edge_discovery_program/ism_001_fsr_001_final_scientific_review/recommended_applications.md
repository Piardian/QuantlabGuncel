# Recommended Applications

## Scientifically Justified Applications

- Include ISM-001 in the validated edge construct library.
- Use ISM-001 as an industry-level leadership and laggard sensor.
- Use ISM-001 as the canonical industry-momentum comparator in preregistered construct comparisons.
- Use ISM-001 in research on future relative industry returns at the predefined horizons.
- Use the frozen benchmark-plus-top-industry tilt as an evaluated research workflow, without treating it as production ready.

## Non-Recommended Applications

- Direct stock selection, ranking or entry generation.
- Standalone production deployment.
- Sector ETF implementation without a separate frozen implementation and validation lifecycle.
- Claims of universal alpha or causal industry rotation.
- Replacement for execution, liquidity, capacity or portfolio risk systems.
