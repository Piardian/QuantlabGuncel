# ISM-001 / FSR-001 Final Scientific Review

## Purpose

Produce the final scientific assessment of ISM-001 by integrating frozen evidence from RP-001 through CC-001.

FSR-001 generates no new empirical evidence. It does not modify the construct, rerun tests, optimize parameters, redesign workflows or recommend production deployment.

## Construct

**Construct ID:** ISM-001

**Construct Name:** Ken French 49 Industry Portfolio 12-1 Cross-Sectional Momentum Rank

**Frozen Definition:** Monthly cross-sectional percentile rank of value-weighted Ken French industry portfolio returns compounded from `t-12` through `t-2`, with predefined top-decile, middle and bottom-decile states.

## Final Scientific Classification

**Primary Category:** Industry-Level Cross-Sectional Edge Construct

**Subcategory:** Intermediate-Horizon Industry Leadership / Laggard State Construct

**Secondary Capabilities:** Industry leadership sensing, industry-relative return information and fixed industry-allocation research support.

**Scientific Maturity:** High within the evaluated industry-portfolio research domain, with limitations.

**Evidence Strength:** Moderate to strong overall.

**Final Status:** Completed construct within the Market Edge Discovery Program.

**Production Readiness:** Not supported.

## Integrated Evidence Assessment

ISM-001 completed the full scientific lifecycle from research prioritization through construct classification. The evidence is internally consistent: the frozen implementation produces reproducible industry ranks; the observed states represent rotating intermediate-horizon industry leaders and laggards; the proposed mechanism survived preregistered validation; predictive information was supported at all predefined horizons; and measurable economic utility was observed in fixed industry-level workflows.

The evidence has clear boundaries. Construct validity remained partially supported because complete 49-industry coverage was not available in every historical post-warmup month. The economic results apply to Ken French research portfolios under fixed monthly workflows and fixed cost assumptions, not to an investable stock- or ETF-level production system.

## Supported Claims

- Industry Momentum is justified as an independent scientific research construct.
- ISM-001 is a deterministic and reproducible implementation of its frozen CD-001 definition.
- ISM-001 represents rotating intermediate-horizon industry leadership and laggard states.
- All four preregistered mechanism hypotheses were supported under HV-001.
- ISM-001 contained statistical information about future relative industry returns at the predefined 1, 3, 6 and 12-month horizons.
- Fixed industry-level economic workflows demonstrated measurable utility under EV-001 assumptions.
- The 50% equal-weight benchmark plus 50% top-industry tilt was the cleanest evaluated economic workflow, improving annualized return, return-to-volatility and maximum drawdown relative to the fixed benchmark in the historical sample.

## Partially Supported Claims

- Construct validity is partially supported: mathematical rank consistency and deterministic reproducibility passed, while complete historical 49-industry coverage did not hold in every post-warmup month.
- The top-decile long-only workflow is partially supported because its aggregate economic metrics improved but its annual benchmark-outperformance frequency was 59%, below the preregistered 60% criterion.

## Not Supported Claims

- Production readiness is not supported.
- Direct stock-level signal assignment is not supported.
- Live alpha generation is not supported by the completed lifecycle.
- Applicability to sector ETFs, alternative industry taxonomies or daily implementations is not supported.
- Universal superiority over other momentum constructs is not supported.
- Causal claims about industry rotation are not supported.

## Inconclusive Claims

- Real-world investability of the historical Ken French industry portfolio series remains inconclusive.
- Robustness under alternative point-in-time industry taxonomies remains inconclusive.
- Net utility after taxes, borrow constraints, capacity and market impact remains inconclusive.
- Generalization outside the evaluated U.S. industry-portfolio domain remains inconclusive.
- Paper-trading and live-execution reproducibility remain inconclusive.

## Consistency Across Stages

No material contradiction was identified across RP-001 through CC-001. CV-001's partial classification concerns historical coverage completeness, not failure of rank calculation or reproducibility. EV-001's supported overall classification coexists with workflow-specific limitations: the long-only workflow was partial, the long-short spread had severe drawdown, and the benchmark-plus-leadership tilt produced the cleanest fixed-workflow evidence.

## Final Assessment

ISM-001 is a completed and scientifically defensible **Industry-Level Cross-Sectional Edge Construct** suitable for inclusion in the validated construct library and for future preregistered construct-comparison research.

Its scientific identity and evidence are limited to the evaluated Ken French industry-portfolio architecture. A separate implementation and validation lifecycle would be required before stock-level assignment, investable ETF mapping or production deployment could be scientifically evaluated.
