# Future Research Recommendations

## Scientifically Justified Future Research

- Preregistered construct comparison against CSM-001, TSM-001 and other completed momentum-family constructs.
- Independent replication using an alternative point-in-time industry taxonomy.
- A separately frozen investable-instrument mapping study for sector or industry funds.
- Preregistered transaction-cost, capacity, borrow and market-impact validation.
- Paper-trading reproducibility after an investable implementation is independently defined and validated.

## Not Justified From Current Evidence Alone

- Immediate production deployment.
- Post-hoc threshold or formation-window optimization.
- Direct transfer of industry states to individual stocks.
- Universal superiority claims.
- Causal claims about industry rotation.

Any future implementation change must begin a new preregistered lifecycle and may not alter the completed ISM-001 evidence record.
