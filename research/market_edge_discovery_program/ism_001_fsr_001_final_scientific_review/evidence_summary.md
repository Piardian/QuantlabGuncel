# Evidence Summary

## RP-001

Industry Momentum received a `GO` decision as an independent and scientifically relevant construct, subject to taxonomy and point-in-time data risks.

## LR-001

The literature review identified industry momentum as a recognized momentum-family mechanism. The literature also showed that taxonomy, formation methodology, weighting and implementation choices materially define the construct.

## CD-001

The construct was frozen as the value-weighted Ken French 49 Industry Portfolio 12-1 Cross-Sectional Momentum Rank with monthly frequency and a minimum of 30 valid industries.

## IM-001

The implementation was completed deterministically. It generated 58,751 rows, including 55,285 valid observations from July 1927 through May 2026, and produced a stable output hash.

## CV-001

Construct validation was partially supported. Rank consistency and reproducibility passed, while complete 49-industry coverage did not hold for every historical post-warmup month.

## MI-001

The identified mechanism was an industry-level, cross-sectional, intermediate-horizon leadership and laggard state. Top- and bottom-decile states showed substantial monthly persistence and broad rotation across industry identities.

## HV-001

All four preregistered mechanism hypotheses were supported. Top and bottom states separated strongly on the frozen formation measure, state identities rotated, and meaningful cross-sectional dispersion was present.

## PV-001

Predictive validation was supported at all predefined 1, 3, 6 and 12-month horizons. The evidence concerned future relative industry returns and did not by itself establish economic utility or production applicability.

## EV-001

Economic validation was supported under fixed industry-level workflows. The benchmark-plus-top-industry tilt produced the cleanest combined evidence. The top-decile long-only workflow was partially supported, and the long-short workflow had a severe historical drawdown despite a positive research spread.

## CC-001

ISM-001 was classified as an Industry-Level Cross-Sectional Edge Construct with high scientific maturity within the evaluated domain, moderate-to-strong overall evidence and unsupported production readiness.
