# Known Limitations

- The construct is defined on Ken French value-weighted 49 Industry Portfolio monthly returns.
- Complete 49-industry historical coverage was not available in every post-warmup month.
- The research did not define point-in-time stock-to-industry membership or stock-level assignment.
- Ken French research portfolios are not a complete investable execution specification.
- EV-001 assumed monthly rebalancing and fixed 10 bps cost per dollar of turnover.
- Taxes, borrow availability, financing, capacity and market impact were not modeled.
- The supported long-short research spread had a severe historical drawdown.
- No alternative taxonomy, equal-weighted industry, sector ETF or international validation was completed.
- No paper-trading or live-trading validation exists.
- The lifecycle does not support causal claims.
