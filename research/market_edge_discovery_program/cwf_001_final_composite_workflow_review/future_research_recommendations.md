# Future Research Recommendations

CWF-001 does not authorize immediate production work.

Scientifically justified future studies:

1. Workflow economic validation

Evaluate whether the supported nested workflow has measurable economic utility under fixed, preregistered portfolio workflows.

2. Out-of-sample workflow reproducibility

Evaluate whether the nested workflow structure persists on data not used in CIP-001/CWS-001.

3. Conflict-region monitoring

Study whether CSM_HIGH x TSM_LOW appears in future data and whether it remains rare.

4. Workflow integration review

Assess how this workflow fits into the broader Market Edge Discovery Program without redefining either component construct.

Any future study must be preregistered and must not modify CSM-001 or TSM-001.
