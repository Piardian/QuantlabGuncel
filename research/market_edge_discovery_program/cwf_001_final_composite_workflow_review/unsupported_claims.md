# Unsupported Claims

The completed evidence does not support the following claims.

- CSM-001 x TSM-001 is a new construct.
- The workflow is production-ready.
- The workflow generates alpha.
- The workflow has confirmed economic utility.
- The workflow should be deployed.
- The workflow is superior to either construct in portfolio terms.
- The workflow can be generalized beyond the evaluated sample without further validation.

These claims require separate preregistered studies.
