# Literature Availability Review

PEAD has a mature literature base.

Important literature streams include:

- Ball and Brown's early earnings-return association work.
- Foster, Olsen and Shevlin's earnings-release anomaly work.
- Bernard and Thomas's delayed price response versus risk-premium framing.
- Later reviews and replication discussions.
- Broader anomaly decay and publication-bias work such as McLean and Pontiff.

Initial source anchors:

- Ball and Brown (1968), *An Empirical Evaluation of Accounting Income Numbers*.
- Foster (1977), *Quarterly Accounting Data: Time-Series Properties and Predictive-Ability Results*.
- Foster, Olsen and Shevlin (1984), *Earnings Releases, Anomalies, and the Behavior of Security Returns*.
- Bernard and Thomas (1989), *Post-Earnings-Announcement Drift: Delayed Price Response or Risk Premium?*
- Bernard and Thomas (1990), follow-up evidence on delayed response.
- Bouchaud et al. / later PEAD review literature where relevant.
- McLean and Pontiff (2016), anomaly decay and post-publication return predictability.

Assessment:

**Literature availability is strong**
