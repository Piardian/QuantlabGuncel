# Data Feasibility Risk Assessment

PEAD has unusually high data-integrity risk compared with CSM-001 and TSM-001.

Major risks:

- Missing or incorrect earnings announcement timestamps.
- Confusion between announcement date and fiscal period date.
- Analyst expectations revised after the fact.
- Split/restatement adjustments.
- Before-open versus after-close trading availability.
- Survivorship bias in available equity universes.
- Vendor inconsistencies in earnings surprise fields.

Risk level:

**Very High**

Mitigation requirement:

CD-001 must not freeze a PEAD definition until LR-001 explicitly documents accepted data standards and rejected unsafe data approaches.
