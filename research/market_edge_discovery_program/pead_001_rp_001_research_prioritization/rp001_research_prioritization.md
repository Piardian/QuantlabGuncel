# PEAD-001 / RP-001 Research Prioritization

## Purpose

Determine whether Post-Earnings Announcement Drift (PEAD) should be accepted as an independent scientific edge construct worthy of full investigation within the Market Edge Discovery Program.

This stage does not define the construct, select indicators, implement code, backtest, optimize, or claim alpha.

## Primary Research Question

Should Post-Earnings Announcement Drift be accepted as an independent scientific construct worthy of full investigation?

## Decision

**GO**

## Rationale

PEAD is one of the most established event-driven return-predictability anomalies in accounting and finance literature. Its scientific relevance is high because it is tied to a clearly observable event class: public earnings announcements and the market's delayed adjustment to earnings news.

The GO decision is based on scientific importance, mechanism clarity, literature maturity, and expected incremental research value.

The decision is not based on expected profitability or historical strategy performance.

## Critical Qualification

PEAD-001 is approved for full scientific investigation only under strict data-integrity controls.

Before CD-001 freezes any construct, the program must explicitly resolve:

- Earnings announcement timestamp availability.
- Point-in-time earnings surprise definition.
- Whether announcements occur before market open, during market hours, or after market close.
- Whether analyst expectations are point-in-time and non-revised.
- Whether price reaction windows avoid look-ahead bias.
- Whether survivorship bias and restatement bias can be controlled or disclosed.

## Evidence Classification

Supported by evidence:

- PEAD is a scientifically recognized market anomaly.
- PEAD has a mature academic literature.
- PEAD has a clear theoretical mechanism family: delayed price response / underreaction to earnings news.
- PEAD is distinct from generic momentum, trend, volatility and market-regime constructs.

Partially supported:

- PEAD can be operationally defined in a reproducible manner, conditional on reliable point-in-time event data.

Not supported at RP stage:

- Any claim of profitability.
- Any claim that PEAD survives in the project's available data.
- Any claim that PEAD is production-ready.

## Final RP-001 Conclusion

PEAD-001 receives a **GO** decision for literature review.
