# Research Gap Summary

The main research gaps for this project are practical and methodological rather than conceptual.

Key gaps:

- Can reliable point-in-time earnings announcement data be obtained?
- Can earnings surprise be defined without revised analyst estimates?
- Can announcement timing be aligned with tradable market sessions?
- Can the construct avoid after-hours and next-open look-ahead errors?
- Does PEAD retain evidence under modern market microstructure and publication-aware conditions?
- Does PEAD add incremental information beyond existing validated constructs?

These gaps justify research, but they also raise the burden of implementation discipline.
