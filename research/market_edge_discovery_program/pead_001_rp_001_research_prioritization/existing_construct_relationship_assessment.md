# Existing Construct Relationship Assessment

PEAD is expected to be distinct from the already evaluated constructs.

Relationship to CSM-001:

- CSM-001 is a cross-sectional relative winner-state construct.
- PEAD is an event-driven earnings-news response construct.
- PEAD may interact with momentum, but it is not identical to momentum.

Relationship to TSM-001:

- TSM-001 is an own-trend risk-state construct.
- PEAD is based on earnings information events.

Relationship to risk constructs:

- PEAD may be conditioned by volatility, liquidity, regime, correlation or breadth.
- These risk constructs do not define PEAD itself.

Assessment:

**High expected construct independence**
