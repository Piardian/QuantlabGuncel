# Construct Landscape

PEAD is an event-driven construct.

It differs from:

- Cross-Sectional Momentum: ranking securities by prior returns.
- Time-Series Momentum: classifying securities by own past trend direction.
- Value: valuation-relative expected return.
- Quality / Profitability: firm fundamental strength.
- Market Regime: aggregate market state.
- Volatility / Liquidity / Breadth constructs: risk-state or market-condition sensors.

PEAD's central object is:

**earnings news followed by delayed post-announcement price adjustment**

Potential construct components, not yet frozen:

- Earnings announcement event.
- Earnings surprise.
- Announcement timing.
- Post-announcement drift window.
- Expected earnings benchmark.

These components must not be finalized until CD-001.
