# Scientific Importance Assessment

PEAD is scientifically important because it directly challenges the hypothesis that public earnings information is immediately and fully incorporated into prices.

The construct belongs to the event-driven expected-return mechanism family.

Key scientific reasons for prioritization:

- It is tied to observable firm-specific information events.
- It has a long research history beginning with earnings-return association studies.
- It provides a clean setting for testing underreaction, information diffusion, and limits-to-arbitrage explanations.
- It is conceptually distinct from CSM-001 and TSM-001.

Assessment:

**High scientific importance**
