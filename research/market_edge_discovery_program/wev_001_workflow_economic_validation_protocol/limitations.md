# Limitations

WEV-001 is a protocol registration stage only.

Known limitations entering WEV-002:

- The workflow is not a standalone construct.
- CSM_HIGH has historically been nested inside TSM_HIGH, so gating may not change selected observations.
- Public data may be current-constituent based and not survivorship-free.
- Equal-weight workflow portfolios are simplified research abstractions.
- Transaction costs, slippage, borrow constraints, tax, capacity and live execution are not automatically included.
- Economic utility evidence, if found, will apply only to the predefined use cases.
