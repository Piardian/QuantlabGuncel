# Executive Summary

PROF-001 / CD-001 is complete.

Frozen construct:

**Conservative-Lag Gross Profitability State**

Formula:

```text
gross_profitability = (revenue - cost_of_goods_sold) / total_assets
```

The construct is intentionally narrow profitability, not broad quality.

Accounting values become available after filing date or, if filing date is unavailable, after a conservative 180-day lag from fiscal period end.

No implementation, backtest or predictive claim was made.
