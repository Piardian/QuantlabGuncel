# Construct Assumptions

- Gross profits-to-assets is a clean and interpretable measure of firm economic productivity.
- Revenue, cost of goods sold and total assets are comparable enough for initial construct validation.
- A conservative 180-day lag is safer than using fiscal period end when filing date is unavailable.
- Narrow profitability is preferable to broad quality composite for first PROF construct.

These assumptions are methodological, not predictive claims.
