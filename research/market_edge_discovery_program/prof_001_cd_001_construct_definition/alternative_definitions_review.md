# Alternative Definitions Review

## Operating Profitability

Rejected for PROF-001 because it is closely tied to Fama-French factor construction but requires more denominator and book-equity policy choices.

## Return On Equity

Rejected because net income and equity denominators can introduce more accounting noise and negative-denominator complexity.

## Return On Assets

Rejected because net income includes more downstream accounting items than gross profitability.

## Cash-Flow Profitability

Rejected for initial construct because cash-flow data availability and definition choices require a separate study.

## Quality Composite

Rejected because it combines profitability, growth, safety and payout, reducing construct purity and increasing researcher degrees of freedom.

## Gross Profitability

Selected because it is narrow, interpretable and strongly supported by the literature.
