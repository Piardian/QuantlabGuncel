# Construct Limitations

- Requires accounting statement data not currently present in the repository.
- Restated accounting values can create historical availability bias.
- Sector differences may affect comparability.
- Financial firms may not be economically comparable under gross profitability.
- The 180-day lag is conservative but less precise than actual filing dates.
- The construct does not include broader quality dimensions such as safety, growth or payout.
- No predictive validity is implied by the definition.
