# Literature Availability Review

Residual Momentum has a focused but meaningful literature base.

Important anchors include:

- Blitz, Huij and Martens (2011), *Residual Momentum*.
- Follow-up practitioner and academic work on idiosyncratic momentum.
- Studies comparing residual momentum with conventional price momentum.
- Broader momentum literature from Jegadeesh and Titman, Carhart and cross-sectional momentum research.
- Factor-model literature required to define residuals.

Assessment:

**Literature availability is moderate-high**

LR-001 should carefully separate residual momentum from raw price momentum and from volatility-scaled momentum.
