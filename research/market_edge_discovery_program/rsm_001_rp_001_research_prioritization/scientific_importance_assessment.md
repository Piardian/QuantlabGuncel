# Scientific Importance Assessment

Residual Momentum is scientifically important because it attempts to isolate stock-specific momentum from common factor exposures.

Scientific value:

- Extends the momentum family beyond raw price ranking.
- Tests whether momentum is driven by idiosyncratic persistence rather than shared factor movement.
- Provides a natural comparison point against CSM-001.
- Requires explicit risk-model discipline, improving the research architecture.

Assessment:

**High scientific importance**

This assessment does not imply predictive validity or profitability.
