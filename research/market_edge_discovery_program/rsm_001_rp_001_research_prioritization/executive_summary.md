# Executive Summary

RSM-001 / RP-001 is complete.

Decision:

**GO**

Residual Momentum is approved for LR-001 because it is a scientifically meaningful extension of the completed momentum research program. It asks whether momentum remains after removing common factor exposures.

RSM-001 is more feasible than PEAD-001 and PROF-001 because it primarily requires return data and public factor returns rather than point-in-time event or accounting data.

No predictive, economic or production claims were made.
