# RSM-001 / LR-001 Literature Review

Purpose: produce a comprehensive literature review of Residual Momentum as a risk-adjusted / idiosyncratic momentum construct.

Required topics:

- Historical development
- Relationship to raw cross-sectional momentum
- Residual return definitions
- Factor models used for residualization
- Regression window design
- Volatility-standardized residuals
- Evidence versus conventional momentum
- Theoretical explanations
- Replication evidence
- Implementation risks
- Data requirements
- Known limitations
- Open research questions

Forbidden:

- Construct freezing
- Factor model selection
- Regression-window selection
- Implementation
- Backtesting
- Alpha claims
- Production recommendations
