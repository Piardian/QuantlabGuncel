# Construct Landscape

Residual Momentum belongs to the risk-adjusted / idiosyncratic momentum family.

It differs from:

- CSM-001: raw cross-sectional price momentum.
- TSM-001: own-trend state.
- PEAD-001: event-driven earnings surprise.
- PROF-001: accounting-based firm profitability.
- Risk constructs: market condition sensors.

Core construct object:

**momentum in residual returns after removing common factor exposures**

Potential subfamilies, not yet selected:

- CAPM residual momentum.
- Fama-French 3-factor residual momentum.
- Fama-French 5-factor residual momentum.
- Industry-neutral residual momentum.
- Volatility-standardized residual momentum.

The exact version must be selected only in CD-001 after LR-001.
