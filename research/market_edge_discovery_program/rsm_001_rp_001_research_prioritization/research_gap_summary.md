# Research Gap Summary

Key project-specific gaps:

- Which factor model should residualize returns?
- Should the construct use daily or monthly returns?
- Should residuals be standardized by residual volatility?
- Does RSM provide information not already captured by CSM-001?
- Does RSM reduce exposure to market, size, value, profitability or investment factors?
- Does residualization improve stability or merely add model risk?
- Can public factor data be integrated reproducibly?

These questions justify research and must be handled in LR/CD stages.
