# Existing Construct Relationship Assessment

RSM-001 is closely related to CSM-001 but scientifically distinct.

Relationship to CSM-001:

- CSM-001 ranks securities by raw prior return.
- RSM-001 would rank or classify securities by prior residual return after factor exposure removal.

Relationship to TSM-001:

- TSM-001 classifies own-trend direction.
- RSM-001 focuses on cross-sectional idiosyncratic momentum.

Relationship to risk constructs:

- RSM explicitly uses risk-model residualization, but it is not itself a market risk-state construct.

Assessment:

**High relevance and moderate expected independence**

RSM may overlap with CSM, but that overlap is itself scientifically valuable to measure.
