# Existing Construct Relationship Assessment

PROF-001 is expected to be distinct from completed constructs.

Relationship to CSM-001:

- CSM-001 measures relative price strength.
- PROF-001 would measure firm fundamental profitability or quality.

Relationship to TSM-001:

- TSM-001 measures own-trend state.
- PROF-001 would measure firm economic characteristics.

Relationship to risk constructs:

- Profitability may interact with liquidity, volatility, regime and credit stress.
- These constructs do not define profitability itself.

Assessment:

**High expected construct independence**
