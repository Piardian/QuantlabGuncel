# Scientific Importance Assessment

Profitability / Quality is scientifically important because it links firm economic strength to expected returns.

Key reasons:

- Novy-Marx documents a gross profitability premium.
- Fama-French five-factor work incorporates operating profitability.
- Hou-Xue-Zhang q-factor models include profitability-related investment theory mechanisms.
- Quality-minus-junk research broadens quality into profitability, growth, safety and payout dimensions.

Assessment:

**High scientific importance**

This assessment does not imply that PROF-001 will be predictive in the project's dataset.
