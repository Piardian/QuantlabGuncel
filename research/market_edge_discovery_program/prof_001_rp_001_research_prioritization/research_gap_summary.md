# Research Gap Summary

Key project-specific gaps:

- Which profitability definition best matches the intended scientific construct?
- Can accounting data be obtained point-in-time or conservatively lagged?
- How should publication lag be handled?
- How should restatements be avoided?
- How should negative book equity, financial firms and sector-specific accounting be treated?
- Does profitability add incremental information beyond CSM-001, TSM-001 and risk constructs?
- Is profitability's evidence return-predictive, risk-explanatory or both?

These questions justify research but must not be answered in RP-001.
