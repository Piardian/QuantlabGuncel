# Data Feasibility Risk Assessment

PROF-001 has high data-integrity risk.

Major risks:

- Missing point-in-time accounting statements.
- Restated financial statement data.
- Incorrect fiscal period alignment.
- Publication lag errors.
- Sector-specific accounting differences.
- Survivorship bias.
- Identifier mapping problems.
- Definition proliferation and post-hoc metric selection.

Risk level:

**High**

Mitigation:

CD-001 must freeze one definition before implementation and must specify a conservative publication-lag policy if point-in-time filing dates are unavailable.
