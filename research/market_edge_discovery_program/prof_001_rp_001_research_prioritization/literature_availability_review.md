# Literature Availability Review

Profitability / Quality has strong literature availability.

Important literature streams include:

- Novy-Marx gross profitability premium.
- Fama-French profitability factor in the five-factor model.
- Hou-Xue-Zhang q-factor model and investment CAPM literature.
- Asness-Frazzini-Pedersen Quality Minus Junk.
- Open Asset Pricing and replication-oriented factor libraries.
- International and robustness studies on profitability/quality factors.

Assessment:

**Literature availability is strong**

LR-001 should separate gross profitability, operating profitability and multidimensional quality because they are related but not identical constructs.
