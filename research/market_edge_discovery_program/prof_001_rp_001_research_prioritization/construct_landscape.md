# Construct Landscape

Profitability / Quality belongs to the firm-fundamental expected-return mechanism family.

It differs from:

- CSM-001: price-relative winner-state construct.
- TSM-001: own-trend risk-state construct.
- PEAD-001: event-driven earnings surprise construct.
- MR/VOL/LIQ/BRD/COR/CRD/FUND/OPT constructs: market condition and risk sensors.

Candidate subfamilies, not yet selected:

- Gross profitability.
- Operating profitability.
- Return on equity.
- Return on assets.
- Cash-flow profitability.
- Quality composite.
- Quality-minus-junk style multidimensional quality.

The exact construct must be selected only in CD-001 after LR-001.
