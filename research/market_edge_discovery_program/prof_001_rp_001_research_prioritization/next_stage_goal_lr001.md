# PROF-001 / LR-001 Literature Review

Purpose: produce a comprehensive literature review of Profitability / Quality as an expected-return construct family.

Required topics:

- Historical development
- Gross profitability
- Operating profitability
- Return on equity / return on assets
- Cash-flow profitability
- Quality-minus-junk definitions
- q-factor and investment theory
- Behavioural explanations
- Risk-based explanations
- Replication evidence
- International evidence
- Data-quality requirements
- Publication lag and restatement risk
- Known limitations
- Open research questions

Forbidden:

- Construct freezing
- Accounting variable selection
- Implementation
- Backtesting
- Profitability claims
- Production recommendations
