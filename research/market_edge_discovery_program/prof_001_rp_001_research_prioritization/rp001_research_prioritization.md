# PROF-001 / RP-001 Research Prioritization

## Purpose

Determine whether Profitability / Quality should be accepted as an independent scientific edge construct worthy of full investigation within the Market Edge Discovery Program.

This stage does not define the construct, select accounting variables, implement code, backtest, optimize, or claim alpha.

## Primary Research Question

Should Profitability / Quality be accepted as an independent scientific construct worthy of full investigation?

## Decision

**GO**

## Rationale

Profitability / Quality has strong scientific motivation and mature empirical support in the asset-pricing literature. Major evidence streams include gross profitability, operating profitability, q-factor profitability, and broader quality-minus-junk definitions.

The GO decision is based on scientific importance, literature maturity, construct independence, long-only compatibility and expected incremental research value.

The decision is not based on expected profitability or historical strategy performance.

## Critical Qualification

PROF-001 is approved for full scientific investigation only under strict accounting-data integrity controls.

Before CD-001 freezes the construct, the program must explicitly resolve:

- Which profitability or quality definition is selected.
- Which accounting statement fields are required.
- Whether data is point-in-time or lagged conservatively.
- Publication lag policy.
- Restatement handling.
- Fiscal period alignment.
- Security identifier mapping.
- Sector, size and value confounding boundaries.

## Evidence Classification

Supported by evidence:

- Profitability / Quality is a recognized expected-return construct family.
- Literature maturity is high.
- The construct is distinct from price momentum, own-trend state and market-risk constructs.
- Long-only compatibility is strong.

Partially supported:

- Operational reproducibility is feasible, conditional on point-in-time or conservatively lagged accounting data.

Not supported at RP stage:

- Any claim of profitability in this repository.
- Any claim that a specific profitability metric is best.
- Any production readiness conclusion.

## Final RP-001 Conclusion

PROF-001 receives a **GO** decision for literature review.
