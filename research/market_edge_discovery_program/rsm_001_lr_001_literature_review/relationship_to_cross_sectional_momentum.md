# Relationship To Cross-Sectional Momentum

Residual Momentum is related to but distinct from raw cross-sectional momentum.

Raw CSM:

```text
score = prior total return
```

Residual Momentum:

```text
score = prior factor-model residual return
```

Scientific distinction:

CSM asks whether recent relative winners continue to outperform.

RSM asks whether stock-specific residual winners continue to contain information after common factor exposures are removed.

RSM may overlap with CSM, but overlap should be measured later, not assumed.
