# Theoretical Foundations

## Idiosyncratic Underreaction

Residual momentum may reflect delayed market reaction to firm-specific information after common factor moves are removed.

## Factor Confounding Reduction

Raw momentum can load on market, size, value or other factors. Residual momentum attempts to remove those exposures.

## Cleaner Signal Hypothesis

If residual returns better isolate stock-specific news, residual momentum may be a cleaner continuation measure.

## Model-Dependence Warning

Residuals are only as meaningful as the factor model used. If the model omits relevant factors, residual momentum may still contain systematic exposures.
