# Annotated Bibliography

Blitz, Huij and Martens (2011), *Residual Momentum*.

Canonical residual momentum paper. Proposes ranking stocks on residual returns rather than total returns and reports reduced common factor exposure.

Jegadeesh and Titman.

Canonical raw cross-sectional momentum foundation. RSM builds on this literature by replacing total return with residual return.

Carhart.

Important factor-model context for momentum in performance attribution.

Fama-French factor literature.

Provides common factor models used for residualization.

Follow-up idiosyncratic momentum studies.

Investigate whether residual or idiosyncratic momentum is distinct and robust across markets and model specifications.
