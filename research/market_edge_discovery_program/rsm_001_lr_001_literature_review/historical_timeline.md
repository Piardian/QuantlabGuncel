# Historical Timeline

1990s:

Cross-sectional price momentum becomes established in academic asset-pricing literature.

2000s:

Researchers and practitioners increasingly examine whether raw momentum returns contain unwanted common factor exposures.

2011:

Blitz, Huij and Martens publish *Residual Momentum*, proposing momentum measurement using residual stock returns.

2010s:

Residual, idiosyncratic and risk-adjusted momentum variants are compared against conventional price momentum across markets and factor models.

2020s:

Research increasingly studies factor-model dependence, omitted-factor risk, and whether residual momentum captures true idiosyncratic continuation or model-specific residual autocorrelation.
