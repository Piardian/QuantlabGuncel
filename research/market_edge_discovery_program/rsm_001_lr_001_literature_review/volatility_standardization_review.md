# Volatility Standardization Review

Some residual momentum approaches standardize residual returns by residual volatility.

Potential benefit:

- Reduces dominance of high-volatility residuals.
- Makes residual momentum scores more comparable across securities.

Potential risk:

- Blends residual momentum with volatility normalization.
- Adds another modeling choice.
- May turn the construct into risk-adjusted residual momentum rather than pure residual momentum.

CD-001 must explicitly decide whether volatility standardization is part of RSM-001.
