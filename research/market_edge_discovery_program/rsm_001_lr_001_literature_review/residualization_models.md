# Residualization Models

Possible residualization models include:

## CAPM

Controls only for market beta.

Advantage:

Simple and interpretable.

Risk:

Residuals may still contain size, value, profitability, investment or industry exposure.

## Fama-French 3-Factor

Controls for market, size and value.

Advantage:

Classic residual momentum literature commonly discusses common factor exposure reduction.

Risk:

Ignores profitability and investment factors.

## Fama-French 5-Factor

Controls for market, size, value, profitability and investment.

Advantage:

More comprehensive.

Risk:

Requires more factor data and may reduce comparability with older residual momentum papers.

## Industry / Sector Residualization

Controls for industry effects.

Risk:

Becomes closer to industry-neutral momentum than general factor-residual momentum.

## CD-001 Implication

The factor model must be frozen before implementation. It is not a tunable parameter.
