# Empirical Evidence Summary

Strong evidence:

- Residual momentum is a documented and recognized momentum variant.
- The literature reports reduced common factor exposure relative to raw momentum.
- It is a natural robustness extension of conventional momentum.

Moderate evidence:

- Some studies report improved consistency versus raw price momentum.
- Follow-up studies investigate residual momentum across markets and model specifications.

Conflicting or limited evidence:

- Results depend on residualization model and regression design.
- Residual momentum may partly reflect model misspecification.
- Volatility-standardized residual momentum can be hard to separate from risk-normalized momentum.

Unsupported at LR stage:

- Any claim that RSM-001 works in this repository.
- Any claim that RSM is better than CSM.
- Any production readiness conclusion.
