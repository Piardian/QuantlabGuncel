# Known Limitations

- PEAD requires high-quality point-in-time event data.
- Earnings surprise is not uniquely defined.
- Analyst estimate data can be revised or stale.
- Announcement timing can create severe look-ahead bias.
- Transaction costs and liquidity constraints may absorb apparent drift.
- Short-side implementation can be difficult.
- Results may decay after publication or crowding.
- Portfolio-level evidence may not always imply broad firm-level consistency.
