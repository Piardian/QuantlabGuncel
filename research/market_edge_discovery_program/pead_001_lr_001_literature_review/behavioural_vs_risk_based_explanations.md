# Behavioural vs Risk-Based Explanations

Behavioural explanation:

- Prices underreact to earnings surprise.
- Drift reflects delayed belief updating.
- Subsequent announcements may reveal that the initial surprise had persistent earnings implications.

Risk-based explanation:

- Drift may reflect compensation for omitted risk.
- Abnormal return estimates can depend on the asset-pricing model used.
- Small, illiquid or high-transaction-cost stocks may drive measured spreads.

Literature status:

The underreaction explanation is prominent and intuitive, but risk, liquidity and implementation frictions remain important unresolved dimensions.
