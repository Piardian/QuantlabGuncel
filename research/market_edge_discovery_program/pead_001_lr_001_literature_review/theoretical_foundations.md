# Theoretical Foundations

## Behavioural Underreaction

The most common explanation is delayed investor reaction to earnings information. Investors may underweight current earnings news, especially when it has implications for future earnings.

## Information Diffusion

Information may be incorporated gradually across investors, especially for less-followed or harder-to-arbitrage firms.

## Limits To Arbitrage

Even if PEAD is recognized, arbitrage may be limited by trading costs, liquidity, short-sale constraints, risk and capacity.

## Risk-Based Interpretation

Some studies examine whether PEAD compensates investors for bearing omitted or mismeasured risk. This remains a competing explanation rather than a settled conclusion.

## CD-001 Implication

The construct should preserve the event-driven delayed-response structure and avoid collapsing PEAD into generic momentum.
