# Executive Summary

PEAD-001 / LR-001 is complete.

Main conclusion:

PEAD is a scientifically mature event-driven expected-return anomaly with a strong literature foundation and clear delayed-response mechanism.

However, it is also one of the most data-sensitive constructs in the research program. Any future PEAD construct must handle announcement timestamp, point-in-time earnings surprise, tradability timing, restatements, survivorship and liquidity limitations explicitly.

No construct was frozen and no predictive or economic claim was made.
