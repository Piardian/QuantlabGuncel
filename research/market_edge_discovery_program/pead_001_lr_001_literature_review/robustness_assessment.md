# Robustness Assessment

PEAD appears robust as a documented phenomenon, but the magnitude and economic interpretation are not universally stable.

Robustness dimensions requiring future evaluation:

- Surprise definition.
- Announcement timing convention.
- Liquidity.
- Firm size.
- Analyst coverage.
- Market regime.
- Transaction costs.
- Publication-period decay.
- International sample differences.

LR-001 does not evaluate these dimensions empirically.
