# Replication Review

PEAD has been repeatedly replicated in academic literature, but replication quality depends heavily on data construction.

Common replication risks:

- Earnings announcement date accuracy.
- Analyst forecast point-in-time availability.
- Correct handling of after-hours announcements.
- Delisting return inclusion.
- Transaction-cost assumptions.
- Universe selection.

McLean and Pontiff-style anomaly decay research raises the broader concern that published return predictors often weaken out of sample and post publication.

PEAD should therefore enter empirical stages only after a strict reproducibility protocol is frozen.
