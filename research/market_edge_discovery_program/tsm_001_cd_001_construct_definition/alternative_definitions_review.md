# Alternative Definitions Review

## Volatility-Scaled TSM

Rejected for TSM-001 because it mixes raw own-history momentum with risk allocation. It may be studied later as a separate construct or economic workflow.

## Moving-Average Trend State

Rejected because moving-average trend is a trend construct, not the canonical own-return TSM baseline selected here.

## 6-1 TSM

Rejected because TSM-001 starts with the canonical 12-1-style baseline to parallel major literature conventions.

## 3-1 TSM

Rejected because shorter horizons may capture different behavior and higher noise.

## Long/Short Strategy Definition

Rejected because strategy direction and position sizing belong to later economic validation, not construct definition.

## Boundary

Rejected alternatives are not judged inferior. They are simply outside TSM-001.
