# Construct Limitations

- CD-001 does not evaluate whether TSM-001 predicts future returns.
- CD-001 does not evaluate economic value.
- TSM-001 excludes volatility scaling, even though much of the literature uses it.
- 252 and 21 trading-day conventions are approximations of 12 months and 1 month.
- Neutral state is expected to be rare because exact zero 12-1 return is uncommon.
- Universe choice can materially affect later aggregate validation.
- TSM-001 is a construct state, not a trading signal or portfolio rule.
