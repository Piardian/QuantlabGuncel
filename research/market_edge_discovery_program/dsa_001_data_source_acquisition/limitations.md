# Limitations

- No licensed institutional dataset is available locally in the repository.
- No sample schema from CRSP, Compustat PIT, CCM, I/B/E/S, or GICS History was inspected locally.
- Access/licensing cost is unknown.
- PEAD requires careful separation of announcement date, announcement timestamp, forecast timestamp, and market reaction window.
- Fundamental constructs require actual availability dates, not only fiscal period-end dates.
- Industry research requires historical classification effective dates, not current labels.
- Sharadar is promising but must not be treated as equivalent to CRSP/Compustat/I/B/E/S without schema validation.
