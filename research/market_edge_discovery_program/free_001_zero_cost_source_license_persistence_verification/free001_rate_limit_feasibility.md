## Decision

`PARTIAL`

## Nasdaq Trader Symbol Directory

Public symbol-directory files are single-file downloads. Operationally, daily retrieval appears feasible for a symbol snapshot prototype.

## Alpaca

Alpaca publicly advertises free market-data access and rate limits on its market-data page, but no account entitlement was verified. Therefore operational feasibility cannot be approved.

## Full Stack Result

`Rate-limit feasibility = PARTIAL`

The symbol-directory component is feasible. Full US equity daily OHLCV capture is not verified.
