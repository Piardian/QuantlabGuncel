# Limitations

- RP-001 is a prioritization study, not a systematic literature review.
- Direct Dual Momentum literature is smaller and more heterogeneous than the CSM and TSM component literatures.
- Some direct evidence is practitioner-origin or portfolio-implementation focused.
- Published implementations use different universes, ranking rules, absolute references and defensive assets.
- RP-001 does not test whether the combination has predictive or economic value.
- RP-001 does not estimate redundancy, interaction or incremental information empirically.
- Open Asset Pricing primarily informs cross-sectional replication standards and does not independently validate Dual Momentum.
- The NO-GO decision is specific to registration as an independent construct; it does not reject future preregistered combination research.
