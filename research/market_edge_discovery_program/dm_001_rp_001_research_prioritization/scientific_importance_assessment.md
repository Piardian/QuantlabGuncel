# Scientific Importance Assessment

## Assessment

**Moderate-High scientific importance; low construct independence.**

Dual Momentum addresses a meaningful question: whether relative selection and absolute trend conditioning provide complementary information when used together.

Its scientific importance is supported by:

- Mature cross-sectional momentum foundations.
- Mature time-series momentum foundations.
- Direct peer-reviewed and practitioner studies of combined rules.
- Relevance to tactical asset allocation and risk conditioning.

Its status as an independent construct is not supported because the candidate's observable information content is defined through CSM-like ranking and TSM-like own-history conditioning. The distinct research contribution is therefore interaction and integration, not a new underlying market mechanism.
