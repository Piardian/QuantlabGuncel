# Research Gap Summary

## Primary Gap

The principal unanswered question is not whether relative and absolute momentum exist separately. Those families already have completed project lifecycles.

The gap is whether their fixed combination provides incremental, nonredundant or complementary information.

## Secondary Gaps

- Whether ordering relative and absolute decisions changes information content.
- Whether apparent benefits arise from momentum information or from fallback-asset allocation.
- Whether volatility scaling or defensive allocation drives reported risk reductions.
- Whether results survive strict out-of-sample and post-publication testing.
- Whether conclusions are stable across universes and market regimes.
- Whether realistic turnover, costs and tax effects alter economic interpretation.

## Scope Boundary

RP-001 identifies these gaps but does not design or execute experiments to answer them.
