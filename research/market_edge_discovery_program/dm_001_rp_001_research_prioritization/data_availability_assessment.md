# Data Availability Assessment

## Assessment

**High availability; high specification sensitivity.**

## Available Inputs

- Public daily or monthly adjusted price histories.
- Public market index and ETF histories.
- Public risk-free reference series from official economic-data providers.
- Existing project datasets supporting CSM-001, TSM-001 and ISM-001.

## Reproducibility Requirements For Any Future Study

- Point-in-time universe definition.
- Total-return or adjusted-price policy.
- Asset-inception and missing-data policy.
- Decision-date availability rule.
- Relative ranking universe.
- Absolute comparison reference.
- Defensive asset or cash proxy policy.
- Rebalance timing.

## Conclusion

Data availability is not a reason for the NO-GO decision. The decision is based on construct independence, not implementation feasibility.
