# Executive Summary

DM-001 / RP-001 evaluated whether Dual Momentum deserves a full scientific lifecycle as an independent Edge Construct.

## Decision

**NO-GO**

Dual Momentum has moderate-to-high scientific importance, high practical relevance, strong rule-based constructability and adequate direct literature. However, its defining information architecture combines cross-sectional/relative momentum with time-series/absolute momentum. Those component families are already represented by completed CSM-001 and TSM-001.

The evidence therefore supports Dual Momentum as a composite or interaction research topic, not as a distinct independent mechanism. The unresolved question is whether the combination contributes incremental or complementary information beyond its components. RP-001 does not answer that question and does not authorize LR-001.

No predictive, economic, alpha or production conclusion was made.
