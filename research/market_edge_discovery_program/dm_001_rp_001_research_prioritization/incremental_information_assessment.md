# Incremental Information Assessment

## Current Evidence Status

**Unproven.**

No completed project study establishes that a Dual Momentum combination contributes information beyond frozen CSM-001 and TSM-001 outputs.

Direct literature suggests that combinations can behave differently from either component alone. This supports a future test of:

- Incremental information.
- Conditional complementarity.
- Redundancy.
- Ordering effects.
- Workflow-specific risk behavior.

These are interaction questions. They do not establish construct independence at RP-001 and cannot be answered without a separate preregistered study.

## Scientific Routing

If human review later authorizes further work, the scientifically appropriate route is a frozen component-comparison or composite-workflow protocol using existing CSM-001 and TSM-001 evidence. DM-001 itself does not proceed to LR-001.
