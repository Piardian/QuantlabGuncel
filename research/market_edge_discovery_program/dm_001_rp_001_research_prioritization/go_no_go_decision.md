# GO / NO-GO Decision

## Decision

**NO-GO**

## Reason

Dual Momentum is a scientifically relevant and reproducible composite framework, but current evidence does not support classifying it as an independent Edge Construct separate from completed CSM-001 and TSM-001.

Its central unresolved value proposition is component complementarity. That is a comparison or interaction question, not sufficient justification for duplicating a full independent construct lifecycle.

## Consequence

- DM-001 terminates at RP-001.
- DM-001 / LR-001 is not authorized.
- No construct definition, implementation or empirical validation may begin under DM-001.
- This decision does not evaluate predictive power, profitability or economic utility.
