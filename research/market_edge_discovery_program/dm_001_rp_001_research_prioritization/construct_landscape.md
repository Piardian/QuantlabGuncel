# Construct Landscape

## Candidate Location

Dual Momentum lies at the intersection of two established momentum families:

```text
Cross-Sectional / Relative Momentum
                 +
Time-Series / Absolute Momentum
                 |
                 v
       Composite selection workflow
```

## Component Families

### Cross-Sectional Momentum

Compares assets with one another and selects relative leaders or winner/loser portfolios. This information family is represented by completed CSM-001 and, at the industry layer, ISM-001.

### Time-Series Momentum

Compares an asset's present state with its own past and conditions direction or exposure on that history. This information family is represented by completed TSM-001.

### Dual Momentum

Applies both dimensions within a joint rule. Published implementations vary in ordering, universe, fallback asset, absolute reference and portfolio construction.

## RP Interpretation

The landscape supports Dual Momentum as a composite research workflow. It does not establish a third independent momentum information family alongside CSM and TSM.
