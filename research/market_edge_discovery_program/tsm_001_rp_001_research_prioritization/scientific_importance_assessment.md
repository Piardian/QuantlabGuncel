# Scientific Importance Assessment

## Assessment

**High**

Time-Series Momentum is an important market construct because it formalizes trend-following behavior as a testable expected-return mechanism: assets with positive own-history returns may continue to have positive subsequent returns, while assets with negative own-history returns may continue to have negative subsequent returns.

## Why It Matters

- It is one of the clearest academic bridges between trend following and asset pricing.
- It is distinct from cross-sectional momentum.
- It is relevant to futures, macro assets, equity indices, commodities, currencies and bonds.
- It may interact with market regime, volatility and crisis behavior.

## Boundary

Scientific importance does not imply profitability or production suitability.
