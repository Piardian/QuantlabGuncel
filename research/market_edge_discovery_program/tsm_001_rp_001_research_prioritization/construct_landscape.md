# Construct Landscape

## Related Construct Families

| Construct family | Relationship to TSM |
| --- | --- |
| Cross-Sectional Momentum | Ranks assets against each other; TSM evaluates each asset against its own past. |
| Trend Following | TSM is a formal academic operationalization of trend-following behavior. |
| Market Regime | Regime may condition TSM behavior but is not identical to it. |
| Volatility State | Volatility scaling often appears in TSM implementations but should not be confused with the construct itself. |
| Crisis Alpha | Trend-following strategies may perform during crisis periods, but crisis alpha is a downstream economic property, not the construct definition. |

## Construct Independence

TSM is sufficiently distinct from CSM-001 because CSM compares assets cross-sectionally, while TSM compares an asset to its own historical trajectory.

## RP-001 Conclusion

TSM is independent enough to justify its own lifecycle.
