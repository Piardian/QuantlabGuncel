# TSM-001 / LR-001 Literature Review

## Purpose

Conduct a comprehensive literature review of Time-Series Momentum as an independent edge mechanism.

- Historical development
- Canonical definitions
- Relationship to trend following
- Relationship to cross-sectional momentum
- Behavioural explanations
- Risk-based explanations
- Futures and multi-asset evidence
- Equity index and single-name equity evidence where available
- Volatility scaling debate
- Replication evidence
- Known failures
- Crash and crisis behavior
- Implementation risks
- Open research questions

## Forbidden

- No construct definition freeze
- No parameter selection
- No implementation
- No backtesting
- No alpha claim
- No trading recommendation

## Success Criterion

LR-001 is complete when the scientific literature around Time-Series Momentum has been objectively synthesized and the next CD-001 stage can define one precise construct.
