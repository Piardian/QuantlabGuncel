# Existing Construct Relationship Assessment

## Relationship to CSM-001

CSM-001 measures relative winner status across securities. TSM-001 would measure whether each instrument has positive or negative own-history momentum.

The two constructs are related through momentum theory but not equivalent.

## Relationship to Validated Risk Constructs

TSM may interact with:

- MR-001 Market Regime
- VOL-001 Volatility State
- BRD-001 Breadth
- COR-001 Correlation

However, RP-001 does not test these relationships.

## Incremental Research Value

High. TSM can help distinguish relative leadership from absolute directional persistence.
