# TSM-001 / RP-001 Research Prioritization

## Purpose

Determine whether Time-Series Momentum should become an independent scientific edge construct within the Market Edge Discovery Program.

RP-001 does not define the construct, choose parameters, implement indicators, run tests, evaluate profitability, or recommend trading strategies.

## Candidate Construct

**Construct ID:** TSM-001

**Construct Name:** Time-Series Momentum

**Candidate Family:** Trend / Momentum / Directional Persistence

## Primary Research Question

Should Time-Series Momentum be accepted as an independent scientific construct worthy of full investigation?

## High-Level Assessment

Time-Series Momentum is a scientifically mature and independently motivated research family. It differs from Cross-Sectional Momentum because it asks whether each asset's own past return predicts its own future return direction or risk state, rather than ranking assets relative to one another.

## Evaluation Matrix

| Dimension | Assessment | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scientific importance | High | Time-series momentum is a central trend-following / managed-futures concept. |
| Literature maturity | High | Moskowitz, Ooi and Pedersen (2012) is a canonical reference; later long-history trend-following work extends the evidence base. |
| Independent motivation | High | TSM measures own-history directional persistence, distinct from CSM's cross-sectional relative ranking. |
| Theoretical clarity | Moderate to high | Explanations include underreaction, delayed overreaction, hedging demand, crisis alpha and behavioral trend persistence. |
| Constructability | High | Can be defined from public historical price returns without fundamentals. |
| Public data feasibility | High | Requires historical price panels; futures data is ideal but equity/ETF proxies are feasible for a first construct if defined carefully. |
| Rule-based representability | High | Own past return sign or trend state can be expressed deterministically. |
| Look-ahead safety | High if designed carefully | Uses historical prices only; must freeze timing and signal availability. |
| Relationship to existing constructs | Distinct but related | Related to CSM and trend constructs, but measures within-asset continuation rather than cross-sectional ranking. |
| Expected research value | High | Provides a natural counterpart to CSM-001 and can clarify directional persistence vs relative leadership. |

## Supported By Preliminary Evidence

- Time-series momentum has a canonical academic literature, especially in futures and multi-asset settings.
- The construct is operationally definable from lagged price data.
- It is conceptually distinct from CSM-001.
- It has meaningful expected research value because it tests a different dimension of momentum: own-series persistence rather than cross-sectional rank.

## Key Risks

- The strongest literature evidence is often in futures and diversified managed-futures contexts, not necessarily single-name equities.
- Volatility scaling and portfolio construction can be entangled with the raw time-series signal.
- Trend-following evidence may depend heavily on crisis periods, diversification and asset-class breadth.
- Long-only equity adaptation may not represent canonical TSM.
- Economic value cannot be inferred from RP-001.

## Decision

**GO**

TSM-001 should proceed to LR-001 Literature Review.

## What This Does Not Mean

This does not claim alpha, profitability, predictive validity, economic utility or production readiness. It only establishes that Time-Series Momentum is scientifically important enough to justify a complete construct lifecycle.
