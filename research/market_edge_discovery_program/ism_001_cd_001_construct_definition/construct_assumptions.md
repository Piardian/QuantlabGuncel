# Construct Assumptions

- Ken French 49 industry portfolios provide a reproducible industry taxonomy.
- Monthly value-weighted industry returns are sufficient to define an industry-level construct.
- Industry-level momentum can be studied without assigning industry scores to individual stocks.
- 12-1 intermediate-horizon formation is literature-aligned.
- Cross-sectional percentile ranks are the appropriate output for construct validation.

These assumptions are methodological, not alpha claims.
