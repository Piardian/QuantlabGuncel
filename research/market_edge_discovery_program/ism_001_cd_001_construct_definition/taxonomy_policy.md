# Taxonomy Policy

Frozen taxonomy:

**Ken French 49 Industry Portfolios**

Reason:

The repository does not currently contain verified point-in-time stock-to-industry membership data.

Using Ken French 49 industry portfolios avoids introducing current-classification look-ahead risk into ISM-001.

Explicitly excluded:

- Current Yahoo industry classifications.
- Current GICS labels.
- Current SIC or NAICS labels.
- Manually assigned sector labels.
- Any taxonomy reconstructed after observing results.
