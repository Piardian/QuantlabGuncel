# Executive Summary

ISM-001 / CD-001 froze one precise Industry Momentum construct:

**Ken French 49 Industry Portfolio 12-1 Cross-Sectional Momentum Rank**

The construct is monthly, industry-level, value-weighted and based on Ken French 49 industry portfolio returns.

It avoids stock-level industry membership because point-in-time membership data is not available in the repository.

No implementation, predictive validation, economic validation, backtest, alpha claim or production recommendation was made.
