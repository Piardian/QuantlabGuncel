# Limitations

WPC-001 is a protocol registration stage only.

Known limitations:

- Monthly equal-weight portfolio is a research abstraction.
- Cost-adjusted performance is not evaluated in WPC-002 unless separately authorized.
- No max position limit is imposed at this stage.
- Portfolio construction does not imply production readiness.
- Current-constituent universe bias remains unresolved.
- Live execution and paper trading remain untested.
