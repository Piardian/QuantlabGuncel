# Known Limitations

- The universe is current S&P 500 constituents, not survivorship-free historical membership.
- Monthly returns inherit Yahoo adjusted-close conventions.
- Fama-French factors come from the public Ken French data library.
- PV-001 used predefined horizons only.
- PV-001 tested statistical predictive information, not economic portfolio implementation.
- EV-001 was not run because PV-001 was Not Supported.
