# Future Research Recommendations

No production change is recommended.

Scientifically justified future work may include:

- Independent replication on survivorship-free historical universe.
- Replication with CRSP-like total return data if available.
- Comparison against CSM-001 and TSM-001 in a construct comparison framework.
- Investigation of whether the negative IC pattern is data-specific, design-specific or sample-specific.

Any such work requires preregistration and may not reinterpret PV-001 retroactively.
