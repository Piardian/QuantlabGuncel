## Supported By Evidence

- Residual Momentum is a scientifically recognized momentum variant.
- RSM-001 was implemented deterministically according to CD-001.
- RSM-001 produces internally coherent cross-sectional residual momentum states.
- TOP_DECILE states represent positive factor-residual intermediate-horizon performance.
- BOTTOM_DECILE states represent negative factor-residual intermediate-horizon performance.
- RSM-001 is related to, but distinguishable from, raw 12-1 momentum.
- Residual volatility standardization materially changes state assignment.

## Not Supported

- The evaluated RSM-001 construct did not demonstrate supported predictive information across 1, 3, 6 or 12-month horizons under PV-001 criteria.
- EV-001 is not scientifically authorized from the current evidence because PV-001 concluded Not Supported.
- No claim of alpha, trading edge, economic utility or production readiness is supported.

## Final Verdict

RSM-001 is a valid descriptive and mechanistic construct under the evaluated implementation, but it is not supported as a predictive edge construct under the current dataset, horizons and preregistered PV-001 criteria.

The construct should not proceed to EV-001 without a new preregistered justification, such as independent replication, revised data integrity design, or a distinct research question.
