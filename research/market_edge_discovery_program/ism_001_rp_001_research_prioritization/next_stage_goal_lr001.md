# ISM-001 / LR-001 Literature Review

Purpose:

Produce a comprehensive scientific literature review of Industry Momentum as an edge mechanism.

Required review areas:

- Moskowitz and Grinblatt industry momentum evidence.
- Industry components of individual momentum.
- Industry information diffusion.
- Lead-lag effects within and across industries.
- Industry taxonomy choices.
- Equal-weighted versus value-weighted industry returns.
- International evidence and replication.
- Practitioner sector rotation literature, clearly separated.
- Known limitations and data-quality risks.

Forbidden:

- Defining the frozen construct.
- Selecting lookback windows.
- Selecting industry taxonomy.
- Implementing code.
- Backtesting.
- Claiming alpha.
- Recommending production use.
