# Executive Summary

ISM-001 / RP-001 evaluated whether Industry Momentum deserves full scientific investigation as an independent edge construct.

Decision:

**GO**

The decision is supported by the existence of a recognized academic literature, conceptual distinction from individual-stock momentum, and a plausible information-diffusion mechanism.

The largest risk is data integrity: industry taxonomy, historical membership, weighting and survivorship controls must be handled explicitly in later stages.

No predictive, economic, alpha or production claim was made.
