# Literature Availability Review

High-quality literature exists but is narrower than the broader cross-sectional momentum literature.

Key literature themes:

- Industry components of stock momentum.
- Industry-level return persistence.
- Industry information diffusion.
- Lead-lag behavior among related firms or industries.
- Sector/industry rotation in practitioner literature.

LR-001 must separate:

- Academic industry momentum evidence.
- Information-diffusion mechanisms.
- Practitioner sector rotation claims.
- Later replication or contradiction.
