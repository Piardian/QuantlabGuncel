# Incremental Value Assessment

ISM-001 has high expected research value because it tests a different level of market organization:

- CSM: individual stock relative winners.
- TSM: own-security trend state.
- RSM: factor-residual individual momentum.
- ISM: group-level industry leadership.

The main incremental question is whether momentum information is better understood at the industry layer than at the individual security layer.

This is not tested at RP-001.
