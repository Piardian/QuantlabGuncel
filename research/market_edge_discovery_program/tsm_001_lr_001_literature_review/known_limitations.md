# Known Limitations

- TSM evidence is strongest in diversified futures settings.
- Volatility scaling can dominate interpretation.
- Transaction costs and turnover can materially affect economic utility.
- Narrow asset universes may not reproduce diversified evidence.
- Trend-following can underperform in choppy or mean-reverting markets.
- Strategy-level trend-following evidence is not equivalent to construct-level TSM validity.
- Long-only equity adaptation is not automatically justified by futures evidence.
