# Annotated Bibliography

## Moskowitz, Ooi and Pedersen (2012)

Canonical academic TSM paper studying equity index, currency, commodity and bond futures. Central reference for TSM-001.

Source: https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v104y2012i2p228-250.html

## AQR Original Paper Data

Dataset associated with the Moskowitz, Ooi and Pedersen study. Important for reproducibility context.

Source: https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Datasets/Time-Series-Momentum-Original-Paper-Data

## Hurst, Ooi and Pedersen (2017)

Long-history trend-following evidence extending analysis back to 1880. Important for historical breadth.

Source: https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Journal-Article/A-Century-of-Evidence-on-Trend-Following-Investing

## Asness, Moskowitz and Pedersen (2013)

Documents value and momentum across asset classes. Useful for placing TSM within broader factor evidence.

Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12021

## Kim, Tse and Wald (2016)

Challenges interpretation of TSM by emphasizing volatility scaling. Important critique for CD/PV/EV design.

Source: https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finmar/v30y2016icp103-124.html

## Baltas and Kosowski

Studies momentum strategies in futures markets and relationships with trend-following funds. Useful for implementation and managed-futures context.

Source: https://www.qminitiative.org/projects/momentum-strategies-in-futures-markets-and-trend-following-funds/
