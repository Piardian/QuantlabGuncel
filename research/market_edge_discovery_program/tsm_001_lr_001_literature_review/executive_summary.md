# Executive Summary

TSM-001 / LR-001 reviewed Time-Series Momentum as an expected-return mechanism.

Conclusion: **the literature is sufficiently mature to justify proceeding to CD-001.**

TSM is scientifically distinct from CSM because it measures own-history directional persistence rather than cross-sectional relative leadership. The strongest evidence comes from diversified futures and trend-following contexts.

The main methodological warning is that TSM evidence is often entangled with volatility scaling, diversification and portfolio construction. CD-001 must freeze whether TSM-001 is a raw construct state or a volatility-scaled construct.

LR-001 does not claim alpha, profitability, economic value or production readiness.
