# Empirical Evidence Summary

## Futures and Macro Assets

Evidence status: **Strong but implementation-sensitive**.

The canonical literature studies diversified futures across equity indices, currencies, commodities and bonds.

## Long-History Trend Following

Evidence status: **Moderate to strong**.

Long-history trend-following studies report persistence across decades and market environments, but these are often strategy-level analyses rather than pure construct validation.

## Single-Name Equities

Evidence status: **Less central and more uncertain**.

TSM is not as canonically established in single-name equity form as CSM. Future CD/PV work must define the target universe carefully.

## Volatility-Scaled Implementations

Evidence status: **Important and contested**.

Some critiques argue that volatility scaling explains much of the reported TSM performance.

## LR-001 Conclusion

TSM has enough empirical support for a lifecycle, but later studies must separate raw directional persistence from volatility scaling and portfolio construction.
