# Relationship To Cross-Sectional Momentum

## Cross-Sectional Momentum

CSM ranks assets against each other. It asks: which assets are winners relative to peers?

## Time-Series Momentum

TSM evaluates each asset against its own history. It asks: is this asset's own trend positive or negative?

## Scientific Distinction

The two constructs can agree or disagree:

- An asset can be positive in its own time series but weak relative to peers.
- An asset can be a cross-sectional winner even if its own absolute return is negative.

## Implication For TSM-001

TSM-001 deserves an independent lifecycle because it measures absolute directional persistence rather than relative leadership.
