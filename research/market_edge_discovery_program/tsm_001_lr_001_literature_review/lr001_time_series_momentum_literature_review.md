# TSM-001 / LR-001 Time-Series Momentum Literature Review

## Purpose

Synthesize the scientific literature on Time-Series Momentum as an independent edge mechanism.

LR-001 does not freeze the construct, select parameters, implement indicators, run tests, evaluate profitability, claim alpha, or recommend trading strategies.

## Primary Research Question

What does the current scientific literature collectively conclude about Time-Series Momentum as an independent expected-return mechanism?

## High-Level Conclusion

Time-Series Momentum is a mature and scientifically important research family, especially in futures, macro assets and trend-following contexts. The literature supports TSM as a serious candidate mechanism based on own-history return persistence. However, the evidence is entangled with diversification, volatility scaling, portfolio construction, costs and asset-class breadth. These issues must be separated in later stages.

## Established Scientific Consensus

- TSM differs from cross-sectional momentum: it evaluates an asset relative to its own past, not relative to peers.
- Canonical academic evidence is strongly associated with Moskowitz, Ooi and Pedersen (2012), who study equity index, currency, commodity and bond futures.
- Trend-following literature extends the TSM idea over longer histories and broader market environments.
- TSM is frequently implemented with volatility scaling, which complicates interpretation of raw signal contribution.
- The literature supports scientific investigation, not automatic production deployment.

## Competing Interpretations

- Behavioural continuation: underreaction and slow information diffusion may create trend persistence.
- Risk compensation: returns may compensate for crash, macro, liquidity or funding risks.
- Crisis convexity: diversified trend-following can perform during extended market dislocations.
- Volatility scaling effect: some evidence suggests performance may be partly driven by volatility targeting rather than raw momentum.

## Unsupported Claims

- LR-001 does not prove TSM alpha.
- LR-001 does not define TSM-001.
- LR-001 does not decide whether TSM should be long-only, long-short, futures-based, equity-based or volatility-scaled.
- LR-001 does not establish economic value after costs.
- LR-001 does not establish production readiness.

## Stage Decision

The literature is sufficiently mature to justify proceeding to CD-001, where one precise TSM-001 construct must be frozen before empirical validation.
