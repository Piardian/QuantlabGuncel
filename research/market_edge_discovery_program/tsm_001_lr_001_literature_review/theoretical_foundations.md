# Theoretical Foundations

## Behavioural Underreaction

Investors may underreact to news, causing prices to continue moving in the same direction over intermediate horizons.

Evidence status: **Moderately supported as theory**.

## Delayed Overreaction

Trends may eventually reverse if prices overshoot fundamental values after prolonged continuation.

Evidence status: **Moderately supported**.

## Risk-Based Explanations

TSM returns may compensate for exposure to macro, liquidity, crash, funding or volatility-related risks.

Evidence status: **Conflicting**.

## Crisis Convexity

Diversified trend-following strategies can benefit from persistent crisis trends, but this is a strategy-level property and should not be automatically attributed to the raw construct.

Evidence status: **Plausible but requires EV-stage separation**.
