# Relationship To Trend Following

## Conceptual Relationship

Time-Series Momentum is an academic formalization of trend-following behavior. Both ask whether price trends persist.

## Important Difference

Trend following is often a full investment style involving signals, risk scaling, portfolio diversification, position sizing and execution rules.

TSM as a construct should be narrower: it should define an observable own-history momentum state before any economic strategy is evaluated.

## Research Implication

CD-001 must prevent strategy logic from leaking into construct definition. Volatility targeting, rebalancing and portfolio construction belong to later EV-stage work unless they are explicitly part of the construct being defined.
