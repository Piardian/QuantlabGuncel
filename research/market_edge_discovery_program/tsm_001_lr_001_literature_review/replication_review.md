# Replication Review

## Supportive Replication Themes

- TSM has been studied across multiple asset classes.
- Long-history trend-following work supports persistence over extended samples.
- AQR provides original paper data for replication of the Moskowitz, Ooi and Pedersen study.

## Critical Replication Themes

- Volatility scaling may drive part of the result.
- Asset-class breadth and diversification matter.
- Results may weaken in narrower universes.
- Strategy-level evidence should not be confused with construct-level evidence.

## LR-001 Conclusion

Replication evidence is sufficient to justify CD-001, but future stages must use preregistered null models and separate raw signal from portfolio construction.
