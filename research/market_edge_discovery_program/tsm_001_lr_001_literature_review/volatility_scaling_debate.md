# Volatility Scaling Debate

## Issue

Many TSM studies use volatility scaling or risk targeting. This can improve portfolio behavior but may blur whether evidence comes from the momentum signal or from risk allocation.

## Key Critique

Kim, Tse and Wald argue that TSM performance in futures may be largely driven by volatility scaling rather than the raw time-series momentum signal.

## Scientific Implication

CD-001 must decide whether TSM-001 is:

- A raw own-return direction construct, or
- A volatility-scaled trend-following construct.

These are not identical constructs.

## LR-001 Boundary

LR-001 does not resolve this choice. It only identifies it as a central construct-definition risk.
