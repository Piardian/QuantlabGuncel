# Research Gap Analysis

## Construct Definition Gap

TSM can be defined as raw own-return sign, moving-average trend, volatility-scaled trend, or signal-strength score. CD-001 must select one.

## Asset-Class Gap

Canonical evidence is futures-heavy. The appropriate TSM-001 universe must be justified before implementation.

## Mechanism Gap

Behavioural, risk-based and volatility-management explanations remain partly entangled.

## Predictive vs Economic Gap

Predictive validity must be separated from economic utility because portfolio construction and volatility scaling can drive realized returns.

## Implementation Gap

Execution timing, rebalancing, costs and capacity must not leak into construct definition.
