# Limitations

- Empirical monthly return data is based on the existing Yahoo Finance adjusted-close panel from the CSM-001 workflow.
- The universe is the current S&P 500 universe file, not a survivorship-free historical constituent universe.
- Fama-French factors were obtained from the Ken French public data library.
- Synthetic verification confirms implementation mechanics; empirical construct behavior remains a CV-001 question.
- No predictive validation was performed.
- No economic validation was performed.
- No alpha claim was made.
- No parameter optimization was performed.
