# Interaction Methodology

## Intended Question

Does ISM-001 contribute statistically meaningful incremental information beyond CSM-001, and does their interaction justify a hierarchical leadership workflow?

## Required Common Unit

The requested interaction matrix requires a common observation unit:

`ticker-date/month x industry-state`

To evaluate cells such as:

- CSM_HIGH x ISM_HIGH
- CSM_HIGH x ISM_LOW
- CSM_LOW x ISM_HIGH
- CSM_LOW x ISM_LOW

each CSM ticker observation must be assigned to a contemporaneous ISM industry state.

## Methodological Blocker

The frozen ISM-001 construct does not provide stock-level industry assignment.

Creating a mapping from current classifications, manual labels, Yahoo metadata, GICS, SIC or NAICS would violate ISM-001 CD-001 and introduce non-frozen assumptions.

## Permitted Analysis

Only an evidence-bound feasibility and unit-alignment audit is permitted.

## Forbidden During CIP-002

- Redefining ISM-001 at stock level.
- Assigning tickers to industries after the fact.
- Introducing current industry classifications.
- Rebuilding ISM-001 with a different taxonomy.
- Inferring performance, alpha or economic value.

## Methodological Conclusion

The requested statistical interaction analysis is not valid under the currently frozen artifacts.
