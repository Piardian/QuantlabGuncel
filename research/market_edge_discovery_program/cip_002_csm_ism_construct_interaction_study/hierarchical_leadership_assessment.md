# Hierarchical Leadership Assessment

## Question

Does industry-level leadership provide an upstream layer for company-level leadership?

## Status

**Inconclusive**

## Reason

A hierarchical leadership workflow requires assigning each company-level CSM observation to a contemporaneous industry-level ISM state.

The frozen ISM-001 construct intentionally avoids stock-to-industry assignment because point-in-time taxonomy data was not available.

## Supported Statement

It is scientifically plausible that industry leadership and company leadership could form a hierarchy.

## Unsupported Statement

The current evidence does not show that such a hierarchy exists for CSM-001 and ISM-001.

## Required Future Evidence

A future study would require a preregistered point-in-time stock-to-industry mapping construct or data bridge before interaction analysis.
