# Executive Summary

CIP-002 evaluated whether the frozen CSM-001 and ISM-001 constructs could be directly compared for overlap, incremental information, complementarity and hierarchical leadership structure.

Final conclusion: **Inconclusive**.

The study could not perform the requested interaction matrix because CSM-001 is ticker-date level while ISM-001 is Ken French 49 industry-month level.

The frozen ISM-001 lifecycle explicitly does not assign industries to individual stocks, excludes current or manually assigned classifications, and classifies stock-level applicability as not supported.

Therefore, forcing a ticker-to-industry bridge would violate the frozen evidence boundary.

CIP-002 does not weaken either construct. It only identifies that a valid CSM x ISM interaction study requires a separately preregistered point-in-time stock-to-industry mapping/data-bridge artifact.
