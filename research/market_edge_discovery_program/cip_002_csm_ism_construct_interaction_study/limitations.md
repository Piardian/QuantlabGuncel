# Limitations

- No frozen ticker-to-industry mapping exists.
- ISM-001 is explicitly industry-level and does not assign states to securities.
- ISM-001 stock-level applicability is not supported by FSR-001.
- CSM-001 and ISM-001 use different observation units.
- No performance, alpha, portfolio or economic analysis was performed.
- The study cannot estimate redundancy, complementarity or incremental information.
- Future interaction research requires a separate preregistered data-bridge or taxonomy construct.
