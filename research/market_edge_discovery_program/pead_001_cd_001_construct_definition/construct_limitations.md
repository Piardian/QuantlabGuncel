# Construct Limitations

- Requires external point-in-time analyst expectation data.
- Analyst consensus may be stale or heterogeneous across vendors.
- EPS comparability can be affected by adjusted versus GAAP definitions.
- Unknown announcement times reduce precision.
- Excluding events with missing data may introduce sample-selection bias.
- The construct does not measure revenue surprise, guidance surprise or market reaction.
- No predictive validity is implied by the definition.
