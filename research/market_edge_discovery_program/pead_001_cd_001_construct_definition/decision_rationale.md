# Decision Rationale

PEAD-001 selects analyst-based earnings surprise because:

- It directly compares actual earnings against market expectations.
- It aligns with a major PEAD measurement family in the literature.
- It preserves the event-driven delayed-response interpretation.
- It avoids defining PEAD as generic price momentum.

The major cost of this choice is data difficulty.

This is intentional. Scientific fidelity takes priority over implementation convenience.
