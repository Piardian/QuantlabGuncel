# Executive Summary

PEAD-001 / CD-001 is complete.

Frozen construct:

**Point-in-Time Analyst Surprise Post-Earnings Announcement Drift State**

The construct measures signed earnings surprise using actual EPS minus pre-announcement analyst consensus expected EPS, scaled by pre-announcement price.

The most important decision is conservative timing: the state is only available after the announcement is public, with after-close and unknown-time events deferred to the next trading day's open.

No implementation, backtest or predictive claim was made.
