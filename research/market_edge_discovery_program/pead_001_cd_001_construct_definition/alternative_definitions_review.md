# Alternative Definitions Review

## Time-Series SUE

Rejected for PEAD-001 because it can be implemented without analyst data but may be less directly tied to market expectations. It remains a valid future comparator.

## Announcement-Window Return Surprise

Rejected for PEAD-001 because it mixes earnings information with price reaction and can blur construct and outcome.

## Revenue Surprise

Rejected because PEAD-001 focuses on earnings surprise only. Revenue surprise may be studied separately.

## Combined Earnings And Revenue Surprise

Rejected for initial construct because it introduces multidimensional interpretation.

## Analyst Surprise

Selected because LR-001 found strong support for analyst-based surprise definitions and because it most directly approximates expected-versus-actual earnings news.
