# EXB-001 Open Limitations

## Open Limitations

- The universe is not survivorship-free.
- Historical delisted securities are not reconstructed.
- Historical point-in-time investability is not available.
- Corporate actions are not available through the verified read-only free path.
- Raw prices may contain split/dividend distortions.
- The free IEX feed may not represent consolidated market history.
- The universe is reduced to 100 symbols.
- Sector/industry history is not available for formal ISM-style research.
- Local immutable storage rights are not established for a production-grade data lake.
- Scientific T0 remains not established.

## Interpretation Boundary

These limitations do not block EXB-002 as a non-formal exploratory exercise. They do block formal production claims, formal robustness validation, and paper/live progression.
