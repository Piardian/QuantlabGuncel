# ISM-001 / CC-001 Construct Classification

## Purpose

Classify ISM-001 using only the accumulated evidence from RP-001 through EV-001.

CC-001 generates no new empirical evidence. It does not modify the frozen construct, rerun validation, optimize parameters, redesign an economic workflow or recommend production deployment.

## Construct Identity

**Construct ID:** ISM-001

**Construct Name:** Ken French 49 Industry Portfolio 12-1 Cross-Sectional Momentum Rank

**Frozen Definition:** Each month, rank valid Ken French value-weighted industry portfolios by compounded return from `t-12` through `t-2`. Assign the cross-sectional percentile score and classify observations as `TOP_DECILE`, `MIDDLE`, `BOTTOM_DECILE` or `INVALID` under the frozen CD-001 rules.

## Final Classification

**Primary Category:** Industry-Level Cross-Sectional Edge Construct

**Subcategory:** Intermediate-Horizon Industry Leadership / Laggard State Construct

**Secondary Capabilities:** Industry leadership sensing, industry-relative return information and industry-level allocation research support.

**Scientific Maturity:** High within the evaluated industry-portfolio research domain, with explicit coverage and implementation limitations.

**Evidence Strength:** Strong for mechanism and predictive validity; moderate to strong overall.

**Economic Utility Classification:** Supported under the evaluated fixed industry-level workflows.

**Production Readiness:** Not supported.

## Supported By Evidence

- ISM-001 is a deterministic and reproducible implementation of the frozen Ken French 49 Industry Portfolio 12-1 cross-sectional momentum rank.
- The construct represents rotating, intermediate-horizon industry leadership and laggard states rather than static industry identities.
- The construct contains statistical information about future relative industry returns at the four predefined horizons within the evaluated dataset.
- Economic utility is supported for the fixed industry-portfolio workflows evaluated in EV-001, including the benchmark-plus-top-industry tilt.

## Partially Supported

- Construct validity is partially supported because rank consistency and reproducibility passed, but complete 49-industry coverage was not present in every historical post-warmup month.
- The top-decile long-only workflow is partially supported because it improved the evaluated aggregate metrics but its annual benchmark-outperformance frequency was below the preregistered 60% criterion.

## Not Supported

- Production readiness is not supported.
- Direct assignment of ISM-001 states to individual securities is not supported.
- Applicability to sector ETFs, alternative taxonomies, equal-weighted industries or daily implementations is not supported.
- Universal superiority over other momentum constructs or industry definitions is not supported.
- Live alpha, execution quality, capacity and market-impact claims are not supported.

## Inconclusive

- Investability of the historical Ken French industry portfolios through real-world instruments remains inconclusive.
- Robustness to alternative industry taxonomies and point-in-time stock membership remains inconclusive.
- Utility after institutional implementation frictions, taxes, borrow constraints and market impact remains inconclusive.
- Generalization outside the Ken French U.S. industry-portfolio domain remains inconclusive.

## Appropriate Research Applications

- Research-grade industry leadership and laggard sensor.
- Canonical industry-momentum comparator within the Market Edge Discovery Program.
- Industry-level relative-return research and construct comparison.
- Fixed, preregistered industry-allocation and benchmark-tilt research.

## Non-Recommended Applications

- Standalone production trading system.
- Direct stock-selection or entry signal without a separately defined and validated mapping layer.
- Live deployment through sector ETFs without a new implementation and validation lifecycle.
- Claim of causal industry rotation or universal alpha.

## CC-001 Conclusion

ISM-001 is classified as a scientifically mature **Industry-Level Cross-Sectional Edge Construct** within the evaluated Ken French industry-portfolio domain. Mechanism validity and predictive validity are supported, and economic utility is supported under fixed industry-level workflows. The construct is eligible for FSR-001, but current evidence does not establish stock-level applicability or production readiness.
