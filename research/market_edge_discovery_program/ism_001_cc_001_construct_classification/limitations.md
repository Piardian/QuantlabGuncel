# Limitations

- CC-001 synthesizes existing RP-001 through EV-001 evidence and performs no new empirical analysis.
- ISM-001 uses Ken French value-weighted 49 Industry Portfolio monthly returns; conclusions are limited to that operational definition.
- Historical post-warmup coverage was not always the complete 49-industry universe, although the frozen minimum-valid-industry rule was satisfied for valid observations.
- The construct does not define point-in-time stock-to-industry membership or stock-level signal assignment.
- Ken French industry portfolios are research portfolios and are not themselves a complete live execution specification.
- EV-001 used fixed monthly workflows and a fixed 10 bps transaction-cost assumption; taxes, borrow constraints, capacity and market impact were not modeled.
- The supported long-short workflow exhibited a severe historical drawdown and must not be interpreted as production risk control.
- No live or paper-trading validation exists.
- Causal interpretations are outside the completed evidence.
- Final scientific closure requires FSR-001.
