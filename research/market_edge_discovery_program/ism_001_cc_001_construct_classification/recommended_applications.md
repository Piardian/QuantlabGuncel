# Recommended Applications

## Evidence-Supported Research Applications

- Industry-level leadership and laggard state measurement.
- Industry-momentum comparison with other validated edge constructs.
- Research on future relative industry returns at the predefined horizons.
- Fixed industry-portfolio allocation and benchmark-tilt studies with preregistered workflows.
- Scientific sensor input for later construct-comparison programs.

## Conditionally Appropriate Applications

- Benchmark-plus-industry-leadership tilt research, limited to the fixed EV-001 assumptions.
- Long-short industry spread research, provided its observed drawdown profile is reported and not described as risk controlled.

## Non-Recommended Applications

- Direct stock selection or entry generation.
- Standalone production deployment.
- Unvalidated sector ETF implementation.
- Claims of live alpha, causal industry rotation or universal applicability.
- Substitution for execution, liquidity, capacity or risk-management models.
