# Construct Maturity Report

## Maturity Assessment

ISM-001 has completed RP-001 through EV-001 and is ready for FSR-001. CC-001 introduces no new evidence.

## Scientific Maturity Level

**High within the evaluated industry-portfolio research domain, with explicit limitations.**

## Evidence Strength

**Moderate to strong overall.**

The strongest evidence supports mechanism validity and predictive validity. Economic utility is supported under fixed industry-level workflows. Overall evidence strength is not classified as universally strong because CV-001 retained a historical coverage limitation and no investable stock- or ETF-level implementation was evaluated.

## Why Maturity Is High

- The construct has a documented literature foundation.
- CD-001 froze a narrow and independently reproducible definition.
- IM-001 produced deterministic, hash-verifiable output.
- CV-001 verified rank consistency and reproducibility.
- MI-001 and HV-001 identified and supported the proposed industry leadership mechanism.
- PV-001 supported predictive information at every predefined horizon.
- EV-001 supported economic utility under fixed industry-level workflows.

## Why This Is Not Production Maturity

- Ken French industry portfolios are research series, not a complete execution specification.
- No point-in-time stock-to-industry mapping was defined.
- No investable ETF mapping was implemented or validated.
- Capacity, borrow, taxes, market impact and live execution were not evaluated.
- Full historical 49-industry coverage did not hold in every post-warmup month.

## Maturity Conclusion

ISM-001 is a mature scientific construct for industry-level research. It is not a production-ready trading system.
