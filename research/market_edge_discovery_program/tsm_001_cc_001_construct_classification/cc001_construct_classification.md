## Final Classification

Primary Category:

**Risk-State / Own-Trend Construct**

Secondary Capabilities:

- Intermediate-horizon own-trend identification
- Signed trend-state classification
- Market-wide trend breadth measurement
- Future realized volatility information
- Future drawdown-risk information

Not Supported As:

- Standalone expected-return alpha construct
- Standalone production trading strategy
- Return forecasting construct under the evaluated evidence

## Scientific Maturity

**Moderate-High**

TSM-001 has mature literature support, a frozen deterministic implementation, validated mechanism, and partial predictive/economic support. Maturity is not classified as High because CV, PV and EV remain limited by current-constituent data and simplified economic workflows.

## Evidence Strength

**Moderate**

The strongest evidence supports mechanism validity and risk-state interpretation. Evidence does not support future return prediction in the evaluated panel.

## Recommended Application Domains

- Risk-state monitoring
- Trend-risk context
- Portfolio risk awareness
- Volatility and drawdown-risk research
- Construct comparison against other validated risk constructs

## Non-Recommended Application Domains

- Direct alpha generation
- Standalone entry signal
- Standalone portfolio allocation model
- Production deployment without additional validation

## Final CC-001 Conclusion

TSM-001 should be classified as a scientifically valid **Risk-State / Own-Trend Construct** with partial economic utility for risk-control workflows, not as a validated standalone alpha construct.
