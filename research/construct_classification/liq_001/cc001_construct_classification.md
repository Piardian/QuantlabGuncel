## Recommended Applications

Supported or partially supported uses:

- Liquidity stress monitoring
- Market risk-state warning
- Liquidity-aware portfolio risk control
- Risk budgeting support
- Volatility targeting support
- Hedge activation support

## Non-Recommended Applications

Not supported uses:

- Standalone alpha signal
- Direct return prediction
- Standalone buy/sell strategy
- Exact transaction-cost model
- Intraday liquidity engine
- Order-book depth or spread measurement

## Classification Boundary

LIQ-001 is scientifically useful as a daily aggregate liquidity-stress construct.

It should not be interpreted as a complete liquidity model, a trading signal, or proof of economic superiority outside the evaluated use cases.
