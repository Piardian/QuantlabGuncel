# Scientific Identity

## LIQ-001 Identity

LIQ-001 is best understood as a **daily aggregate price-impact liquidity stress construct**.

It measures whether broad US equity price movement is large relative to dollar trading volume.

## What It Represents

Supported by evidence:

- Aggregate market illiquidity
- Price-impact stress
- Trading-friction stress
- Market risk-state information

Partially supported:

- Drawdown-risk information
- Liquidity-aware economic utility

Not supported:

- Alpha generation
- Direct return prediction
- Complete liquidity measurement

## Relation to MR-001

LIQ-001 overlaps with MR-001 STRESS periods but is not the same construct.

MR-001 represents latent return/volatility regime state.

LIQ-001 represents price movement per unit of dollar volume, interpreted as aggregate price-impact illiquidity.
