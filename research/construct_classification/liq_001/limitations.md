# Limitations

## Evidence Limitations

- Validation used a capped symbol universe.
- Universe is not survivorship-free.
- Input data came from Yahoo Finance and may revise over time.
- LIQ-001 is daily and cannot capture intraday liquidity dynamics.
- Bid-ask spread, depth, immediacy, and resiliency are excluded.
- Economic validation used simple exposure ladders and zero cash return.

## Interpretation Limitations

- Predictive evidence is risk-focused, not return-focused.
- Economic utility evidence is partial and workflow-specific.
- LIQ-001 should not be generalized beyond evaluated data, assumptions, and use cases.
