# Non-Recommended Usage

## Not Recommended

- Standalone trading signal
- Alpha factor
- Directional return predictor
- Exact execution-cost estimator
- Intraday liquidity model
- Spread model
- Order-book depth model
- Complete liquidity taxonomy

## Why

The completed evidence supports LIQ-001 as a risk-state and liquidity-stress construct.

It does not support treating LIQ-001 as a standalone alpha engine or full market microstructure model.
