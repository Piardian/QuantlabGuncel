# Recommended Usage

## Supported by Evidence

### Volatility-State Monitoring

VOL-001 is appropriate as a daily realized volatility-state monitor for the US equity market.

### Market Turbulence Detection

VOL-001 is appropriate for identifying market turbulence states because high values correspond to larger daily, overnight, open-to-close and range-based movement.

### Future Volatility-Risk Warning

VOL-001 is appropriate as a risk-state warning input for future realized volatility and future high-volatility occurrence.

### Volatility-Aware Risk Budgeting

VOL-001 is supported for predefined risk-budgeting workflows evaluated in EV-001.

### Volatility-Aware Portfolio Risk Control

VOL-001 is supported for predefined portfolio risk-control workflows evaluated in EV-001.

## Partially Supported

### Volatility Targeting Support

VOL-001 may support volatility targeting, but EV-001 classified this use case as partially supported rather than fully supported.

## Use Boundary

These recommendations apply only within the evaluated V3.0 research scope and predefined workflows.

They do not imply production deployment or universal superiority.
