# Limitations

## Proxy Limitation

VOL-001 uses SPY as the sole market proxy, as frozen in CD-001.

## Data Limitation

VOL-001 uses daily OHLC data and does not incorporate intraday prices or options data.

## Measurement Limitation

VOL-001 is a Yang-Zhang realized-volatility construct. It does not represent implied volatility, GARCH conditional volatility, ATR, or cross-sectional dispersion.

## Drawdown Limitation

PV-001 did not support future drawdown-risk prediction. That hypothesis remained inconclusive.

## Economic Limitation

EV-001 was only partially supported. UC-3 dynamic de-risking was not supported.

## Live Validation Limitation

The research remains historical and simulated. No live operational validation has been completed.

## No Alpha Claim

VOL-001 was not validated as an alpha factor or directional return predictor.
