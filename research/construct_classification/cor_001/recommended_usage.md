# Recommended Usage

## Recommended Scientific Use

COR-001 is scientifically justified as:

```text
Market Correlation / Risk Management Construct
```

## Recommended Applications

- correlation state monitoring
- co-movement stress dashboarding
- diversification-risk awareness
- future volatility-risk awareness
- breadth deterioration risk awareness
- correlation persistence monitoring
- diversification-risk budgeting support
- correlation-aware volatility targeting support

## Recommended Interpretation

High COR-001 states should be interpreted as elevated market synchronization and common co-movement stress.

Low COR-001 states should be interpreted as lower market synchronization and greater cross-sectional independence.

## Boundary

Recommended usage is scientific and research-oriented.

It is not a production deployment recommendation.
