# Unsupported Capabilities

## Not Supported

COR-001 is not supported for:

- alpha generation
- directional return forecasting
- standalone buy/sell signal generation
- return-enhancement classification
- universal market timing
- production deployment without operational review
- leverage expansion solely based on low correlation

## Why These Are Not Supported

The completed research lifecycle did not test COR-001 as an alpha signal.

EV-001 showed risk-management utility, not return-enhancement utility.

Production deployment requires separate operational validation, monitoring, cost modeling, and live or paper validation.
