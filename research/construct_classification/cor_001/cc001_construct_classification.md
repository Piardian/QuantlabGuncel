# COR-001 / CC-001: Construct Classification

## Purpose

Classify COR-001 using only the completed evidence body from RP-001 through EV-001.

No new empirical analysis was performed.

## Primary Classification

```text
Market Correlation / Co-Movement Construct
```

## Secondary Classification

```text
Risk Management Construct
```

## Scientific Identity

COR-001 is a realized market-wide synchronization sensor.

It measures whether securities in a fixed broad US equity universe are moving together or independently, using 60-day average pairwise Pearson correlation of daily log returns.

## Supported Capabilities

Supported by evidence:

- Measures broad US equity market co-movement.
- Identifies high versus low market synchronization states.
- Represents common co-movement stress.
- Contains predictive information for future realized market volatility.
- Contains predictive information for future market breadth deterioration.
- Contains predictive information for future correlation persistence.
- Provides economic utility in predefined risk-management workflows.
- Supports diversification-risk budgeting.
- Supports correlation-aware volatility targeting.

Partially supported:

- Future drawdown risk prediction.
- Correlation stress risk control.
- Regime-aware portfolio de-risking support.

Not supported:

- Alpha generation.
- Return-enhancement classification.
- Standalone directional return prediction.
- Production deployment without separate operational review.

## Evidence Strength

Overall evidence strength:

```text
High for co-movement measurement.
High for explanatory mechanism validity.
Moderate-High for predictive risk-state use.
Moderate for economic risk-management utility.
Not supported for alpha or return enhancement.
```

## Scientific Maturity Level

```text
High
```

Reason:

COR-001 completed RP, LR, CD, IM, CV, MI, HV, PV, and EV with coherent evidence and explicit limitations.

## Recommended Applications

COR-001 is scientifically justified for:

- market correlation monitoring
- co-movement stress dashboarding
- diversification risk awareness
- future volatility-risk awareness
- breadth deterioration risk awareness
- correlation persistence monitoring
- diversification-risk budgeting support
- correlation-aware volatility targeting support

## Non-Recommended Applications

COR-001 is not scientifically justified for:

- standalone alpha signal
- directional return forecasting
- buy/sell signal generation
- universal market timing system
- production deployment without separate operational review
- leverage expansion solely based on low correlation

## Final CC-001 Classification

COR-001 is classified as:

```text
Market Correlation / Risk Management Construct
```

The construct is eligible for FSR-001 Final Scientific Review.
