# CC-001 Limitations

## Evidence Scope

Classification is limited to evidence generated from RP-001 through EV-001.

No new analysis was performed in CC-001.

## Universe Limitation

COR-001 uses a fixed current US equity universe.

Historical survivorship limitations remain.

## Data Limitation

The construct relies on Yahoo Finance daily price data.

## Construct Limitation

COR-001 measures linear average pairwise realized correlation.

It does not measure implied correlation, nonlinear dependence, tail dependence, or cross-asset correlation.

## Deployment Limitation

CC-001 does not authorize production deployment.

## Independence Limitation

COR-001 may overlap with VOL-001, BRD-001, LIQ-001, and MR-001.

Incremental independence versus other constructs was not fully evaluated in this lifecycle.
