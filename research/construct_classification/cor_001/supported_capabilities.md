# Supported Capabilities

## Supported By Evidence

COR-001 is supported for:

- measuring broad US equity market co-movement
- identifying high and low market synchronization states
- representing market-wide synchronization / common co-movement stress
- forecasting future realized volatility risk at 20-day and 60-day horizons
- forecasting future market breadth deterioration at 20-day and 60-day horizons
- forecasting future correlation persistence at 20-day and 60-day horizons
- supporting diversification-risk budgeting
- supporting correlation-aware volatility targeting

## Partially Supported

COR-001 is partially supported for:

- future drawdown risk awareness
- correlation stress risk control
- regime-aware portfolio de-risking support

## Evidence Boundary

Supported capabilities are limited to the evaluated data, construct definition, and predefined workflows.
