# Non-Recommended Applications

CRD-001 is not scientifically justified for:

- standalone alpha generation
- standalone buy/sell signal generation
- universal market timing
- credit spread-widening prediction engine
- leverage expansion solely because credit stress is low
- production deployment without separate operational validation
- replacing broader market regime, volatility, liquidity, breadth, or correlation constructs

## Reason

The completed lifecycle supports CRD-001 primarily as a credit-stress risk-management sensor. It does not support standalone trading or universal production claims.
