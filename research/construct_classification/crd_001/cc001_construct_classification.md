# CRD-001 / CC-001: Construct Classification

## Purpose

Classify CRD-001 using only the completed evidence body from RP-001 through EV-001.

No new empirical analysis was performed.

## Primary Classification

```text
Credit Stress Construct
```

## Secondary Classification

```text
Risk Management / Credit-Spread Stress Sensor
```

## Scientific Identity

CRD-001 is a market-level high-yield credit spread stress sensor.

It measures the current state of US speculative-grade corporate credit stress using the ICE BofA US High Yield Option-Adjusted Spread from FRED series `BAMLH0A0HYM2`.

## Supported Capabilities

Supported by evidence:

- Implements the frozen CD-001 high-yield OAS construct deterministically.
- Separates high and low high-yield credit-spread states within the available sample.
- Represents a speculative-grade credit risk compensation state.
- Provides predefined risk-management utility in several evaluated workflows within the available sample.

Partially supported:

- Construct validity across time.
- Mechanism validity across broader credit cycles.
- Predictive information for future realized volatility.
- Economic utility as a credit-stress risk-management tool.

Not supported:

- Future drawdown-risk prediction.
- Future credit spread-widening prediction under the preregistered direction.
- Alpha generation.
- Standalone return-enhancement classification.
- Production deployment without separate operational review.

## Evidence Strength

Overall evidence strength:

```text
High for implementation fidelity.
Moderate for construct measurement.
Moderate for explanatory mechanism validity.
Limited-to-moderate for predictive validity.
Moderate for predefined risk-management economic utility.
Not supported for alpha or standalone trading signal use.
```

## Scientific Maturity Level

```text
Moderate
```

Reason:

CRD-001 completed RP, LR, CD, IM, CV, MI, HV, PV, and EV with coherent evidence and explicit limitations. However, the available normalized sample is short and does not include multiple major credit cycles.

## Recommended Applications

CRD-001 is scientifically justified for:

- high-yield credit spread stress monitoring
- credit-stress dashboarding
- risk-management context
- credit-stress-aware risk budgeting support
- volatility-aware de-risking support
- portfolio risk-control monitoring

## Non-Recommended Applications

CRD-001 is not scientifically justified for:

- standalone alpha signal
- standalone directional equity return model
- credit spread-widening forecast engine
- universal market timing system
- production deployment without operational validation
- leverage expansion solely based on low credit stress

## Final CC-001 Classification

CRD-001 is classified as:

```text
Credit Stress / Risk Management Sensor
```

The construct is eligible for FSR-001 Final Scientific Review.
