# CC-001 Limitations

## No New Evidence

CC-001 performs classification only. It does not run new experiments or generate new empirical evidence.

## Short Sample

The strongest limitation across CV, MI, HV, PV, and EV is the short available normalized CRD-001 sample.

## Component Ambiguity

CRD-001 uses total high-yield OAS. It does not decompose default risk, liquidity premium, excess credit premium, or broad risk appetite.

## Incremental Independence Not Tested

CC-001 does not establish whether CRD-001 adds incremental value beyond MR-001, VOL-001, LIQ-001, BRD-001, or COR-001.

## No Production Readiness Claim

Operational deployment, monitoring, latency, data licensing, and failure handling were not evaluated.
