# Evidence Status Summary

## Supported By Evidence

- Credit Stress is a recognized and mature market construct in the literature.
- CRD-001 has a frozen, reproducible definition.
- CRD-001 implementation is deterministic and faithful to CD-001.
- High and low CRD-001 states separate tighter from wider high-yield spread environments within the available sample.

## Partially Supported

- Construct validity.
- Mechanism validity.
- Predictive validity.
- Economic utility.

The main reason these are partial rather than strong is the short available normalized sample.

## Not Supported

- Alpha generation.
- Standalone trading signal use.
- Future drawdown-risk prediction.
- Future credit spread-widening prediction under the preregistered hypothesis.

## Inconclusive

- Long-cycle behavior across 2008, 2020, and other major credit stress events.
- Incremental value relative to MR-001, VOL-001, LIQ-001, BRD-001, and COR-001.
- Production deployment suitability.
