# BRD-001 / CC-001: Classification Limitations

## Universe Limitation

BRD-001 uses `sp500_current_universe.csv`.

This is a fixed current universe and not survivorship-free historical constituents.

## Implementation Data Limitation

Full-market output depends on Yahoo Finance historical data availability and possible vendor revisions.

## Construct Scope Limitation

BRD-001 is specifically 200-day moving-average breadth.

It does not classify all market breadth definitions.

## Predictive Scope Limitation

Predictive support is strongest for volatility and trend deterioration risk.

Return prediction was not supported.

## Economic Scope Limitation

Economic validation applies only to predefined EV-001 workflows and fixed exposure maps.

No optimized policy was tested.

## Deployment Limitation

CC-001 does not approve production deployment.

Operational, execution, monitoring and governance reviews remain outside this classification.
