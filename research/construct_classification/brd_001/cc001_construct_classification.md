# BRD-001 / CC-001: Construct Classification

## Purpose

Classify BRD-001 using only the completed evidence body from RP-001 through EV-001.

No new empirical analysis was performed.

## Primary Classification

```text
Market Breadth / Market Internals Construct
```

## Secondary Classification

```text
Risk Management Construct
```

## Scientific Identity

BRD-001 is a long-term cross-sectional trend participation sensor.

It measures whether participation across a fixed broad US equity universe is broad or narrow relative to each security's own 200-day moving average.

## Supported Capabilities

Supported by evidence:

- Measures long-term market breadth.
- Measures internal market confirmation.
- Identifies broad versus narrow participation states.
- Contains predictive information for future realized volatility.
- Contains predictive information for future trend deterioration risk.
- Provides economic utility in predefined risk-management workflows.

Partially supported:

- Future drawdown risk prediction.
- Portfolio risk control versus buy-and-hold.

Not supported:

- Future return prediction.
- Alpha generation.
- Return-enhancement classification.

## Evidence Strength

Overall evidence strength:

```text
High for risk-management use.
Moderate for predictive risk-state use.
Not supported for return prediction.
```

## Scientific Maturity Level

```text
High
```

Reason:

BRD-001 completed LR, CD, IM, CV, MI, HV, PV and EV with coherent evidence and explicit limitations.

## Recommended Applications

BRD-001 is scientifically justified for:

- market breadth monitoring
- internal participation dashboarding
- risk-state monitoring
- risk budgeting support
- volatility targeting support
- trend deterioration risk control

## Non-Recommended Applications

BRD-001 is not scientifically justified for:

- standalone alpha signal
- directional return forecasting
- buy/sell signal generation
- universal market timing system
- production deployment without separate operational review

## Final CC-001 Classification

BRD-001 is classified as:

```text
Market Breadth / Risk Management Construct
```

The construct is eligible for FSR-001 Final Scientific Review.
