# OPT-001 / CC-001

# Executive Summary

OPT-001 is classified as:

```text
Options-Implied Volatility Risk Sensor
```

Scientific maturity:

```text
High
```

Evidence strength:

```text
Strong for construct/mechanism/risk-predictive validity; partial for economic utility; inconclusive for directional returns.
```

OPT-001 is best interpreted as a VIXCLS-based market uncertainty and expected-volatility state sensor. It is suitable for research use in risk monitoring, volatility-risk awareness, and risk-management workflow evaluation.

It is not supported as a standalone alpha signal, direct directional return predictor, or complete production trading system.
