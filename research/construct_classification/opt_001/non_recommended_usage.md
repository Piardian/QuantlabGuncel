# OPT-001 / CC-001

# Non-Recommended Usage

Not supported uses:

- Standalone alpha signal.
- Direct directional return predictor.
- Standalone buy/sell strategy.
- Proof of market timing skill.
- Pure expected-volatility measure separated from variance risk premium.
- Replacement for full options-surface modeling.
- Replacement for VIX futures term-structure analysis.
- Live hedge execution engine without separate execution validation.

## Boundary

The completed evidence supports OPT-001 as a risk sensor, not as a complete trading system.
