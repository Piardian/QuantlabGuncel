# OPT-001 / CC-001

# Recommended Usage

Supported or partially supported uses:

- Options-implied volatility state monitoring.
- Market uncertainty / fear-state monitoring.
- Future realized volatility risk indication.
- Future absolute market movement risk indication.
- Future drawdown-risk warning with appropriate caution.
- Volatility-aware risk budgeting support.
- Drawdown risk-control support.
- Volatility targeting support with caution.
- Dynamic hedge activation research support with caution.

## Usage Boundary

These are evidence-limited research applications. They are not automatic production deployment recommendations.
