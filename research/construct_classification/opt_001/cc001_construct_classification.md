# OPT-001 / CC-001: Construct Classification

## Scope

This stage classifies OPT-001 using completed evidence only. No new tests, optimization, or new hypotheses are introduced.

## Final Classification

- Primary Category: Options-Implied Volatility Risk Sensor
- Secondary Capabilities: Market Uncertainty Monitor / Future Risk-State Indicator / Risk-Management Support Sensor
- Scientific Maturity Level: High
- Evidence Strength: Strong for construct validity, mechanism validity, and future risk-variable prediction; partial for economic utility; inconclusive for directional returns.

## Scientific Interpretation

OPT-001 is scientifically useful as a reproducible, VIXCLS-based options-implied volatility state construct. The completed evidence supports interpreting it as a market uncertainty / expected volatility risk sensor rather than as a directional return or alpha construct.

## Boundary

This classification does not claim standalone alpha generation, universal economic value, or full production readiness. Economic support is limited to the predefined EV-001 risk-management workflows.
