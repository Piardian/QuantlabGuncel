# Limitations

- OPT-001 uses VIXCLS only and does not reconstruct the option surface.
- OPT-001 cannot isolate expected volatility from variance risk premium, fear, hedging pressure, or risk aversion.
- SPY-based predictive and economic validation begins in 2008 due to available cached OHLCV data.
- EV-001 uses simplified exposure policies and not live execution or derivatives implementation.
- Directional return evidence is inconclusive.
- Economic utility is partially supported, not universally established.
- No claim is made about standalone alpha, universal superiority, or production readiness.
