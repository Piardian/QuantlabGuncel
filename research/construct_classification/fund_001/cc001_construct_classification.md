# FUND-001 / CC-001: Construct Classification

## Scope

This stage classifies FUND-001 using completed evidence only. No new tests, optimization, or new hypotheses are introduced.

## Final Classification

- Primary Category: Funding Spread Risk Sensor
- Secondary Capabilities: Predictive Risk Indicator / Funding Conditions Monitor
- Scientific Maturity Level: Moderate scientific maturity; low economic-validation maturity
- Evidence Strength: Mixed: strong implementation and predictive-risk evidence; weak economic utility evidence

## Scientific Interpretation

FUND-001 is scientifically useful as a reproducible funding-spread risk sensor and partially supported predictive risk indicator.

It is not supported as an economic risk-management overlay under the predefined EV-001 workflows.

## Boundary

This classification does not claim production readiness, alpha generation, universal economic value, or standalone portfolio usefulness.
