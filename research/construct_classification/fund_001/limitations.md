# FUND-001 / CC-001

# Limitations

- FUND-001 is based on DCPF3M - DTB3 only.
- It is not a pure funding-liquidity measure.
- It mixes commercial paper funding cost, Treasury bill safe-asset behavior, credit/counterparty concern, and short-rate context.
- Historical coverage is constrained by DCPF3M availability.
- Credit-stress predictive validation has short overlap.
- Economic utility was not supported in predefined EV-001 workflows.
- No live validation exists.
- No production readiness is claimed.
