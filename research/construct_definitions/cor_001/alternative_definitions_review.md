# Alternative Definitions Review

## Selected Definition

US Equity Market Average Pairwise Correlation State.

## Alternative 1: Cross-Asset Equity-Bond Correlation

Status: Rejected for COR-001.

Reason: Scientifically important, but answers a different question about cross-asset diversification rather than internal US equity co-movement.

Potential future construct: COR-002 or XAC-001.

## Alternative 2: Dynamic Conditional Correlation

Status: Rejected for COR-001.

Reason: Literature-supported methodology, but more complex, model-dependent, and less transparent for the first correlation construct.

Potential future construct: model-based conditional correlation construct.

## Alternative 3: Implied Correlation

Status: Rejected for COR-001.

Reason: Forward-looking and useful, but requires options data and represents market-implied expectations rather than realized equity co-movement.

Potential future construct: implied correlation / dispersion construct.

## Alternative 4: PCA / Eigenvalue Concentration

Status: Rejected for COR-001.

Reason: Strong systemic-risk relevance, but it measures common-factor concentration rather than direct average pairwise co-movement.

Potential future construct: systemic concentration construct.

## Alternative 5: Tail Dependence

Status: Rejected for COR-001.

Reason: Crisis-relevant but nonlinear, data-intensive, and not the simplest first correlation-state definition.

Potential future construct: tail co-movement construct.

## Alternative 6: Sector Correlation

Status: Rejected for COR-001.

Reason: Useful for market structure, but requires sector taxonomy choices and is narrower than broad market correlation.

Potential future construct: sector co-movement construct.

## Alternative 7: Rolling Correlation To SPY

Status: Rejected for COR-001.

Reason: Measures market-beta alignment of securities to SPY rather than pairwise synchronization across the full universe.

Potential future construct: market beta cohesion construct.
