# Construct Limitations

## Segment Specificity

CRD-001 measures high-yield credit stress, not the entire credit market.

## Component Ambiguity

High-yield OAS may reflect expected default risk, credit risk premium, liquidity stress, risk appetite, and bond-market structure. CRD-001 does not decompose these components.

## Vendor Methodology Dependence

The source series is public through FRED, but the underlying index construction follows ICE BofA methodology.

## Historical Composition Changes

The high-yield bond market composition can change over time by issuer, rating mix, maturity structure, and sector exposure.

## No CDS Information

CRD-001 excludes CDS-based credit stress. Bond and CDS markets can diverge.

## No Investment-Grade Credit Stress

CRD-001 excludes investment-grade corporate credit. Investment-grade credit may behave differently from high-yield credit.

## No Predictive or Economic Claim

CD-001 defines measurement only. Predictive validity and economic utility require later lifecycle stages.
