# Alternative Definitions Review

## Selected

### High-Yield Option-Adjusted Spread Stress

Selected as CRD-001.

Rationale:

- strong literature relevance
- public daily data availability
- direct credit-market interpretation
- high sensitivity to speculative-grade credit stress
- reproducible implementation path

## Rejected Alternatives

### Investment-Grade Option-Adjusted Spread Stress

Rejected for CRD-001 because it measures a different credit-quality segment. It remains a valid candidate for a future investment-grade credit construct.

### Moody's Baa Minus Treasury Spread

Rejected because it is a broad long-history proxy but less specifically tied to the high-yield stress mechanism selected here.

### Moody's Baa Minus Aaa Spread

Rejected because it measures rating-tier spread separation rather than broad speculative-grade credit stress.

### Excess Bond Premium

Rejected because it is model-dependent and harder to reproduce deterministically from simple public inputs. It remains scientifically important and may justify a separate construct.

### CDS-Based Credit Stress

Rejected because public reproducibility and contract-convention consistency are weaker than the selected FRED high-yield OAS path.

### Composite Credit Stress Index

Rejected because composites can mix credit, liquidity, funding, volatility, and broader financial stress. CRD-001 prioritizes a narrow construct.

## Important Boundary

Rejected alternatives are not scientifically invalid. They are excluded only from this frozen CRD-001 definition.
