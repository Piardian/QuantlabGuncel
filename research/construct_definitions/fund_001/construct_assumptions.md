# FUND-001 / CD-001

# Construct Assumptions

## Assumption 1: Commercial Paper Spread Represents Funding Stress

The selected spread assumes that the premium of 90-day AA financial commercial paper over 3-month Treasury bills is an observable proxy for short-term private financial funding stress.

Evidence status: Supported by literature, with measurement caveats.

## Assumption 2: Treasury Bills Are A Short-Term Safe Funding Benchmark

The construct assumes that 3-month Treasury bill rates provide an appropriate short-term public-sector benchmark for comparison with private financial commercial paper rates.

Evidence status: Supported by market convention.

## Assumption 3: Spread Level Is Informative Without Decomposition

The construct assumes the spread itself can be measured as a funding-stress state variable even though it may contain liquidity, credit, counterparty, and safe-asset-demand components.

Evidence status: Partially supported.

## Assumption 4: Post-LIBOR Measurement Benefits From Non-LIBOR Inputs

The construct assumes a commercial-paper-to-T-bill spread is preferable to LIBOR-based proxies for a modern reproducible research construct.

Evidence status: Supported by LIBOR measurement limitations, but not proven superior.

## Assumption 5: Daily Missing Values Should Not Be Filled Inside The Construct

The construct assumes that missing source observations should remain missing rather than be forward-filled as if observed.

Evidence status: Methodological assumption for reproducibility and data integrity.

## Assumption 6: Rolling 252 Valid Observations Are Suitable For State Normalization

The construct assumes that trailing 252 valid observations provide a reproducible one-year normalization context.

Evidence status: Operational convention; not a predictive claim.
