# FUND-001 / CD-001

# Construct Limitations

## Proxy Contamination

The selected spread can reflect multiple forces:

- funding liquidity stress,
- counterparty credit risk,
- financial-sector credit concern,
- Treasury safe-asset demand,
- monetary policy expectations,
- market technicals.

FUND-001 does not decompose these components.

## Not A Pure Funding Liquidity Measure

The construct is a funding-spread proxy, not a direct observation of funding liquidity, haircuts, margins, or dealer financing terms.

## Commercial Paper Market Scope

The construct focuses on AA financial commercial paper. It may not represent:

- repo funding,
- secured funding,
- lower-quality issuers,
- nonfinancial commercial paper,
- money-market fund stress,
- collateral scarcity,
- dealer balance-sheet constraints.

## Missing Data Risk

The selected commercial paper rate may not be observed on every calendar or trading day. Missing values are not filled inside the construct.

## Maturity And Basis Risk

Both inputs are short-term rates, but their conventions and markets differ. The spread is an approximation, not a perfect matched-funding premium.

## Historical Comparability

Commercial paper market structure, Federal Reserve facilities, monetary policy implementation, and benchmark conventions have changed over time.

## Distinction From CRD-001

FUND-001 may contain credit-risk information, but it is not a corporate credit-spread construct. CRD-001 remains the dedicated high-yield credit stress construct.

## Stage Boundary

CD-001 does not test whether FUND-001 predicts future risk, improves decisions, or has economic utility.
