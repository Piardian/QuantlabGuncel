# Executive Summary

CD-001 freezes the official Market Regime construct for the V3.0 research program.

The selected construct is a **two-state latent regime inferred from SPY daily log returns and 20-day realized volatility**. This choice is grounded in the strongest literature-supported family from LR-001: latent Markov-switching / hidden-state regime models.

The construct was selected because it is:

- literature-supported
- theoretically clear
- operationally simple
- reproducible
- easy to preregister

The definition intentionally excludes macro-heavy, breadth-heavy, liquidity-heavy, and high-granularity alternatives so that the construct remains stable and implementable.

This stage does not evaluate predictive validity, economic value, or trading performance. It only establishes the official regime construct for subsequent validation stages.
