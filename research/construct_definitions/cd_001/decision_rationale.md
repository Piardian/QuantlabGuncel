# Decision Rationale

## Selected Definition

The official Market Regime construct is a **two-state latent regime inferred from SPY daily returns and 20-day realized volatility**.

## Rationale

This definition was chosen because it is:

- more canonical than ad hoc rule-based labels
- more reproducible than macro-heavy composites
- more parsimonious than multi-state taxonomies
- more directly aligned with the regime-switching literature than single-variable regime heuristics

## Decision Priority Order

Selection was based on the following priority order:

1. Strength of literature support
2. Theoretical clarity
3. Operational simplicity
4. Reproducibility
5. Measurement reliability

Expected predictive performance was not considered.

## Why This Is the Best Fit for CD-001

The literature repeatedly shows that returns and volatility are the most common and most stable ingredients in regime models. That makes them the cleanest foundation for a preregistered construct.
