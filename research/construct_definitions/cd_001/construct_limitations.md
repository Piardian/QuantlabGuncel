# Construct Limitations

## Known Limitations Before Empirical Testing

1. **Detection lag:** latent regimes are typically identified with some delay relative to turning points.
2. **Model dependence:** results depend on the chosen state count and emission specification.
3. **Sample sensitivity:** estimated regimes may vary across decades or structural market changes.
4. **Proxy limitation:** SPY captures the US equity market reasonably well, but not perfectly.
5. **Binary simplification:** reducing market conditions to two states may omit meaningful substructure.
6. **Ex post labeling:** state interpretation is assigned after estimation, which is standard but not purely objective.
7. **Missing broader context:** macro, credit, liquidity, and breadth information are intentionally excluded, so the construct is narrower than some literature definitions.

## Interpretation Boundary

This construct is designed for reproducible research, not for claiming that the market has only two states or that these two states exhaust the regime concept in the literature.
