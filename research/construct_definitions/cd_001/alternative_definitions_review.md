# Alternative Definitions Review

## Rejected Alternative 1: Rule-Based Bull / Bear Definition

**Description:** define regime using drawdown or moving-average thresholds.

**Reason rejected:** simple to interpret, but threshold choices are arbitrary and less theoretically canonical than latent regime-switching models.

## Rejected Alternative 2: Volatility-Only Regime

**Description:** define regime only by realized volatility level.

**Reason rejected:** supported in the literature, but too narrow to represent the broader market-state concept emphasized in LR-001.

## Rejected Alternative 3: Macro-Heavy Regime Composite

**Description:** combine macro, credit, liquidity, and market variables.

**Reason rejected:** broader coverage, but harder to reproduce and more sensitive to missing data and variable selection.

## Rejected Alternative 4: Multi-State High-Granularity Regime Model

**Description:** use 3+ latent states or many sub-regimes.

**Reason rejected:** potentially informative, but less parsimonious and harder to preregister cleanly as the first official construct.

## Rejected Alternative 5: Cross-Asset Correlation Regime

**Description:** define regime from cross-sectional correlation structure or breadth networks.

**Reason rejected:** well-supported in the literature, but less direct and less standard than a return-plus-volatility latent state for the first construct definition.

## Selection Summary

The selected construct is the best compromise between literature support, clarity, and reproducibility.
