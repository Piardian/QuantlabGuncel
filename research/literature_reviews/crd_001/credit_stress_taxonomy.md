# Credit Stress Taxonomy

## Purpose

This file summarizes major Credit Stress taxonomies found in academic and professional literature. It does not select the CRD-001 construct.

## Taxonomy Families

| Family | Description | Typical Observables | Evidence Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Broad Corporate Credit Stress | Stress across corporate debt markets. | Corporate spread indexes, aggregate OAS. | Strongly supported |
| High-Yield Credit Stress | Stress in below-investment-grade credit. | High-yield OAS, HY CDS index. | Strongly supported |
| Investment-Grade Credit Stress | Stress in higher-quality corporate credit. | IG OAS, A-rated or Baa spreads. | Strongly supported |
| Rating-Tier Stress | Relative compensation by credit quality. | Baa-Treasury, Baa-Aaa. | Moderately supported |
| Default-Risk Stress | Compensation for expected default loss. | Default probabilities, CDS-implied default risk. | Strongly supported |
| Non-Default Credit Premium | Spread component beyond expected default compensation. | Excess bond premium, residual spread models. | Strongly supported |
| Credit Liquidity Stress | Illiquidity component in credit markets. | Bond liquidity measures, liquidity-adjusted spreads. | Moderately supported |
| Composite Financial Stress | Credit as part of broader stress. | STLFSI, NFCI, OFR FSI credit category. | Strongly supported |

## Key Taxonomy Issue

Credit Stress is not identical to credit spread level. Spread level is an observable proxy that may include default risk, liquidity, risk premium, and market-structure effects.

## CD-001 Implication

CD-001 must decide whether CRD-001 is intended to represent:

- Broad credit-market stress.
- High-yield stress.
- Investment-grade stress.
- Default-risk stress.
- Non-default credit risk premium.
- A composite credit stress state.

Only one operational construct should be frozen.
