# LR-001 Limitations

## Literature Scope

This review prioritizes high-quality academic and institutional literature, but it is not a complete census of all credit-risk research.

## Data Scope

Some important credit-stress measures are proprietary, model-dependent, or only partially available through public data.

## Construct Ambiguity

Credit Stress can refer to default risk, spread widening, liquidity stress, risk premium expansion, or composite financial stress. These are related but not identical.

## Proxy Dependence

Different proxies may produce different conclusions. High-yield OAS, investment-grade OAS, Baa-Treasury spreads, CDS spreads, and excess bond premium estimates should not be assumed interchangeable.

## No Empirical Validation in LR

This stage does not test construct validity, predictive power, trading performance, economic utility, or implementation feasibility inside the local project.

## Reproducibility Risk

Model-based measures such as excess bond premium may be theoretically attractive but harder to reproduce than simple public spread series.
