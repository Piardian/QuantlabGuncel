Market Signal Discovery Program

CRD-001

CD-001

--------------------------------------------------

## BACKGROUND

CRD-001 has completed:

[x] RP-001 Research Prioritization

[x] LR-001 Literature Review

LR-001 concluded that Credit Stress is a mature and scientifically important market construct, but also found that the term can refer to multiple related mechanisms:

- default-risk compensation
- credit-risk premium
- bond-market liquidity stress
- high-yield spread stress
- investment-grade spread stress
- rating-tier spread stress
- composite financial stress

CD-001 must freeze one precise, reproducible research construct.

No predictive or economic claims will be evaluated during this stage.

--------------------------------------------------

## PURPOSE

Define the exact CRD-001 Credit Stress construct that will be investigated throughout the remainder of the Market Signal Discovery Program lifecycle.

The definition must be:

- scientifically justified
- operationally measurable
- reproducible
- deterministic
- sufficiently narrow for preregistered testing

--------------------------------------------------

## PRIMARY RESEARCH QUESTIONS

1.

Which Credit Stress definition from LR-001 should become the official CRD-001 research construct?

2.

Why is this definition preferred over competing alternatives?

3.

Which theoretical mechanism does it represent?

4.

Which observable variables define the construct?

5.

Which observable variables are intentionally excluded, and why?

6.

What is the exact operational definition?

7.

Can two independent researchers implement it identically from the specification?

8.

What assumptions and limitations exist before empirical testing begins?

--------------------------------------------------

## CANDIDATE DEFINITION FAMILIES FROM LR-001

Evaluate and either select or explicitly reject:

- High-yield option-adjusted spread stress
- Investment-grade option-adjusted spread stress
- Moody's Baa minus Treasury spread
- Moody's Baa minus Aaa spread
- Excess bond premium style construct
- CDS-based credit stress
- Composite credit stress index

--------------------------------------------------

## DECISION CRITERIA

Selection should prioritize:

- strength of literature support
- theoretical clarity
- operational simplicity
- public data availability
- reproducibility
- measurement reliability
- independence from already completed constructs where possible

Do not select based on expected predictive performance.

--------------------------------------------------

## FORBIDDEN

Do NOT:

- run backtests
- evaluate prediction
- evaluate profitability
- evaluate alpha
- optimize thresholds
- tune parameters
- select the construct because it appears profitable
- mix multiple incompatible definitions without justification

## SUCCESS CRITERIA

At the conclusion of CD-001, the following questions must have unambiguous answers:

- Which Credit Stress definition has been selected?
- Why was it selected?
- Which alternatives were rejected?
- Which observable variables define the construct?
- Which variables are explicitly excluded?
- Can an independent researcher reproduce the construct without ambiguity?

The construct definition becomes frozen after CD-001.

Any future modification requires a new preregistered construct and may not alter the current CRD-001 pipeline.

No statements regarding predictive validity, trading performance, economic value, or production suitability are permitted.
