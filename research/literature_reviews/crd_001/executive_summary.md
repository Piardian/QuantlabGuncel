# Executive Summary

## CRD-001 / LR-001 Decision

Credit Stress should proceed to CD-001.

## Main Finding

The literature supports Credit Stress as a mature, distinct and scientifically important market construct. It is commonly observed through corporate credit spreads, high-yield spreads, rating spreads, CDS measures, excess bond premium estimates, and credit components of financial stress indices.

## Most Important Caution

Credit Stress is multidimensional. A spread increase may reflect default risk, liquidity stress, risk premium expansion, or broader financial stress. Therefore CD-001 must freeze one narrow, reproducible operational definition.

## What LR-001 Does Not Claim

This review does not claim predictive validity, trading value, economic utility, production suitability, or alpha generation.
