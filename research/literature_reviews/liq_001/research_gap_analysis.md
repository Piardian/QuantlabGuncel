# Research Gap Analysis

## Main Scientific Gaps

1. Which liquidity dimension is most appropriate for a broad daily-data research program?
2. Can daily liquidity proxies capture enough of the construct to be scientifically useful?
3. How independent is liquidity from market regime and volatility?
4. Should liquidity be modeled at the asset level, market level, or both?
5. Is liquidity best treated as a state, a cost, a risk factor, or an execution constraint?

## Practical Gaps

- Direct bid-ask and order-book data may not be available.
- Capacity and slippage implications require careful economic validation.
- Liquidity can change rapidly intraday, which daily data may smooth away.

## Most Important CD-001 Question

CD-001 must decide what LIQ-001 is intended to measure:

- market trading friction
- asset-level illiquidity
- systematic liquidity state
- execution capacity
- liquidity stress

This decision must be made before implementation.
