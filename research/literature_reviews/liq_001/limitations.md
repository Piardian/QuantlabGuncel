# Limitations

## Literature Limitations

- There is no single universally accepted liquidity measure.
- Liquidity is multidimensional.
- Some dimensions require expensive or unavailable high-frequency data.
- Daily proxies may miss spread dynamics, order-book depth, immediacy, and resiliency.
- Liquidity often interacts with volatility, market stress, and funding conditions.
- Measurement quality differs across asset classes and historical periods.
- Asset-level liquidity and market-wide liquidity are related but not identical.

## Project-Specific Limitations for Future Stages

- The current project primarily uses daily OHLCV data.
- Direct quote and order-book data may not be available.
- A daily-data construct may capture only a subset of liquidity.
- CD-001 must explicitly choose whether LIQ-001 is asset-level liquidity, aggregate market liquidity, or systematic liquidity risk.
