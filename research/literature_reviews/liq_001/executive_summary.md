# Executive Summary

LIQ-001 / LR-001 reviews Market Liquidity as a financial construct.

## Main Finding

Market Liquidity is strongly supported in the literature as a fundamental, multidimensional market construct.

## What Liquidity Represents

Liquidity describes how easily, quickly, cheaply, and safely an asset can be traded without large price impact.

## Main Dimensions

- Tightness
- Depth
- Immediacy
- Breadth
- Resiliency
- Price impact

## Measurement Families

- Spread-based measures
- Volume and turnover measures
- Price-impact measures
- Transaction-cost proxies
- Order-book measures
- Aggregate market liquidity measures

## Key Scientific Point

Liquidity is not the same as volatility, market regime, volume, or momentum. It can interact with them, but it represents a distinct trading-friction and market-quality construct.

The literature supports moving LIQ-001 to `CD-001`.
