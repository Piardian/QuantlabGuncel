# Annotated Bibliography

## Kyle (1985), Continuous Auctions and Insider Trading

- **Link:** https://people.duke.edu/~qc2/BA532/1985%20EMA%20Kyle.pdf
- **Use:** Foundational microstructure model for price impact, depth, and liquidity.
- **Takeaway:** Liquidity includes depth, tightness, and resiliency concepts.

## IMF / Sarr and Lybek (2002), Measuring Liquidity in Financial Markets

- **Link:** https://www.imf.org/en/publications/wp/issues/2016/12/30/measuring-liquidity-in-financial-markets-16211
- **Use:** Broad measurement taxonomy.
- **Takeaway:** Liquidity measures include bid-ask spreads, turnover ratios, and price-impact measures across tightness, immediacy, depth, breadth, and resiliency.

## Amihud (2002), Illiquidity and Stock Returns

- **Link:** https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~mkearns/finread/amihud.pdf
- **Use:** Major daily-data illiquidity proxy.
- **Takeaway:** Introduces a widely used return-to-dollar-volume illiquidity measure.

## Pastor and Stambaugh (2003), Liquidity Risk and Expected Stock Returns

- **Link:** https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w8462/w8462.pdf
- **Use:** Systematic liquidity risk in asset pricing.
- **Takeaway:** Studies market-wide liquidity as a state variable relevant to expected returns.

## Acharya and Pedersen (2005), Asset Pricing with Liquidity Risk

- **Link:** https://docs.lhpedersen.com/liquidity_risk.pdf
- **Use:** Liquidity-adjusted CAPM framework.
- **Takeaway:** Expected returns can depend on expected liquidity and liquidity-related covariances.

## Chordia, Roll, and Subrahmanyam (2001), Market Liquidity and Trading Activity

- **Link:** https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/0022-1082.00335
- **Use:** Aggregate market liquidity and trading activity.
- **Takeaway:** Supports studying liquidity as a market-wide phenomenon.

## Brunnermeier and Pedersen (2009), Market Liquidity and Funding Liquidity

- **Link:** https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/22/6/2201/1592184
- **Use:** Link between funding constraints and market liquidity.
- **Takeaway:** Market liquidity and funding liquidity can reinforce each other, especially under stress.

## Roll (1984), A Simple Implicit Measure of the Effective Bid-Ask Spread

- **Link:** https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1984.tb03897.x
- **Use:** Price-only implied spread estimator.
- **Takeaway:** Useful when quote data is unavailable, but relies on strong assumptions.

## Lesmond, Ogden, and Trzcinka (1999), A New Estimate of Transaction Costs

- **Link:** https://www.jstor.org/stable/2645977
- **Use:** Transaction-cost proxy from return behavior.
- **Takeaway:** Useful when direct transaction-cost data is unavailable.

## Central Bank of Ireland, The Multiple Dimensions of Liquidity

- **Link:** https://www.centralbank.ie/docs/default-source/publications/research-technical-papers/11rt20-the-multiple-dimensions-of-liquidity-%28garabedian-and-inghelbrecht%29.pdf
- **Use:** Modern review of liquidity dimensionality.
- **Takeaway:** Emphasizes tightness, depth, and resilience and the lack of consensus on dimensionality.
