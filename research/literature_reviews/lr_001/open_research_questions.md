# LR-001 Open Research Questions

1. Which momentum definition is most robust after realistic costs, turnover, market impact, and capacity within each market segment?
2. How much published momentum evidence is sensitive to construction choices such as universe, skip month, weighting, rebalance frequency, and delisting treatment?
3. Which mechanism, if any, explains momentum across different asset classes and decades: behavioral underreaction, risk compensation, institutional trading, or a combination?
4. How reliably can momentum-crash exposure be identified before rather than after a crash state?
5. How stable are stock-level, industry-level, and benchmark-relative momentum signals across regimes and countries?
6. What portion of apparent momentum survives with survivorship-free data and fully investable liquidity screens?
7. When does benchmark-relative strength add information beyond cross-sectional ranking or factor-adjusted momentum?
8. How should researchers distinguish persistent factor premia from data-mined variants of known momentum effects?
