# Literature Review Limitations

## Strongly Supported

- There is no single agreed definition of market regime across finance.
- Many regime papers are model-dependent and sensitive to sample choice.
- Real-time use is harder than ex post classification because of detection lag.

## Moderately Supported

- Performance claims in practitioner regime research may not generalize across decades or market structures.
- Hidden-state models often require subjective interpretation after estimation.

## Limited Evidence

- Sector and liquidity regime taxonomies are less standardized than bull/bear or volatility regimes.
- Cross-country generalization is less consistent than within-market regime work.

## Conflicting Evidence

- The literature disagrees on whether regime models provide robust practical forecasting value in every setting.
- Some studies find clear regime benefits in allocation or risk management; others emphasize instability and specification risk.
