# Annotated Bibliography

## Hamilton (1989)
- **Citation:** Hamilton, J. D. (1989). *A New Approach to the Economic Analysis of Nonstationary Time Series and the Business Cycle.*
- **Link:** [PDF](https://www.ssc.wisc.edu/~bhansen/718/Hamilton1989.pdf)
- **Use in this review:** Canonical origin of regime-switching econometrics.
- **Takeaway:** Establishes the foundational latent-state framework for regime shifts.

## Ang & Timmermann (2012)
- **Citation:** Ang, A., & Timmermann, A. (2012). *Regime Changes and Financial Markets.*
- **Link:** [Annual Review / IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/anr/refeco/v4y2012p313-337.html)
- **Use in this review:** Survey-level synthesis of regime-switching in finance.
- **Takeaway:** Regime switching is a central and useful framework, but regime definitions remain model-dependent.

## Ang & Bekaert (2003)
- **Citation:** Ang, A., & Bekaert, G. (2003). *How do Regimes Affect Asset Allocation?*
- **Link:** [NBER](https://ideas.repec.org/p/nbr/nberwo/10080.html)
- **Use in this review:** Evidence that regime-aware allocation can matter.
- **Takeaway:** Regime changes can be economically relevant for asset allocation.

## Guidolin & Timmermann (2006)
- **Citation:** Guidolin, M., & Timmermann, A. (2006). *Asset Allocation under Multivariate Regime Switching.*
- **Link:** [PDF](https://files.stlouisfed.org/files/htdocs/wp/2005/2005-002.pdf)
- **Use in this review:** Multi-asset regime-switching and higher-moment variation.
- **Takeaway:** Regimes often involve differences in covariance and higher-order moments.

## Bull and Bear Regime Classification Papers
- **Citation:** Several papers on bull/bear dating and state identification.
- **Link:** [Top-down bull/bear states](https://pureadmin.qub.ac.uk/ws/files/137453711/Bullbear_TD_IRFA_Revised.pdf)
- **Use in this review:** Practical taxonomy and classification challenges.
- **Takeaway:** Bull/bear is a common taxonomy, but definitions vary.

## Correlation-Network Regime Classification
- **Citation:** Mayo, Zhu, and Nam (2019).
- **Link:** [PDF](https://econ.unc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/1423/2019-Mayo-Zhu_Nam.pdf)
- **Use in this review:** Breadth/correlation-driven regime classification.
- **Takeaway:** Cross-sectional correlation structure can help define regime changes.

## Regime-Switching Factor Investing with HMM
- **Citation:** Regime-Switching Factor Investing with Hidden Markov Models (2020).
- **Link:** [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/1911-8074/13/12/311)
- **Use in this review:** Practitioner-adjacent regime-aware factor investing.
- **Takeaway:** Regime detection is actively used in applied portfolio research.

## ECB Volatility Regime Note
- **Citation:** ECB Financial Stability publication on regime shifts in stock volatility.
- **Link:** [PDF](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/financial-stability-publications/fsr/focus/2018/pdf/ecb~bcaaae16c3.fsrbox201805_03.pdf)
- **Use in this review:** Real-world volatility regime discussion.
- **Takeaway:** Volatility regime shifts are observable and policy-relevant.

## Practitioner Regime Detection Example
- **Citation:** QuantStart article on HMM-based regime detection.
- **Link:** [Article](https://www.quantstart.com/articles/market-regime-detection-using-hidden-markov-models-in-qstrader/)
- **Use in this review:** Clear example of practitioner framing.
- **Takeaway:** Regime detection is often used as a risk filter in practitioner systems.
