# Research Gap Analysis

## Strongly Supported Gaps

- No universal, canonical market regime taxonomy exists.
- Regime definitions vary by asset class, horizon, and model family.
- Many studies identify regimes ex post rather than in genuinely prospective settings.

## Moderately Supported Gaps

- Better comparison of latent-state, threshold, and rule-based regime definitions on identical data would be useful.
- More work is needed on regime stability across decades and across different market structures.

## Limited Evidence Gaps

- How much regime information is incremental versus already contained in volatility, breadth, and credit variables.
- Which observables are most robust across equities, rates, credit, and FX.

## Speculative Gaps

- Whether one unified regime construct can serve all practical investment tasks.
- Whether regime detection can be made fully stable in real time without material lag.
