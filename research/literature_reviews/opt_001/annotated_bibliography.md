# Annotated Bibliography

## Cboe VIX Methodology

Source: https://cdn.cboe.com/resources/indices/Volatility_Index_Methodology_Cboe_Volatility_Index.pdf

Contribution:

Defines VIX as an options-based volatility index reflecting market expectations of future volatility.

Use in OPT-001:

Official methodology source for implied volatility level constructs.

## Bollerslev, Tauchen, and Zhou, Expected Stock Returns and Variance Risk Premia

Source: https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/22/11/4463/1565787

Contribution:

Develops variance risk premium as a key options-implied risk construct.

Use in OPT-001:

Core academic foundation for variance-premium constructs.

## Bakshi, Kapadia, and Madan, Stock Return Characteristics, Skew Laws, and Differential Pricing

Source: https://people.umass.edu/nkapadia/docs/Bakshi_Kapadia_Madan_2003_RFS.pdf

Contribution:

Provides foundational risk-neutral moment estimation from option prices.

Use in OPT-001:

Core theoretical source for skewness and higher-moment constructs.

## Xing, Zhang, and Zhao, Individual Option Volatility Smirk

Source: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-financial-and-quantitative-analysis/article/what-does-the-individual-option-volatility-smirk-tell-us-about-future-equity-returns/ECFD16BA9ACBDC8D577D1BD866FBEA72

Contribution:

Reports that individual option smirk shape contains information about future equity outcomes.

Use in OPT-001:

Key source for volatility smirk and downside information constructs.

## Cremers and Weinbaum, Deviations from Put-Call Parity

Source: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-financial-and-quantitative-analysis/article/deviations-from-putcall-parity-and-stock-return-predictability/D9BA8F97580328AAFD7988B092FE5D50

Contribution:

Studies return predictability from deviations in call-put option pricing.

Use in OPT-001:

Key source for put-call parity deviation constructs.

## Cboe Implied Correlation White Paper

Source: https://cdn.cboe.com/resources/indices/documents/Implied_Correlation-WhitePaper-v1.0.5.pdf

Contribution:

Defines implied correlation from relative pricing of index and component options.

Use in OPT-001:

Official methodology source for option-implied correlation constructs.

## Implied Volatility Forecasting Survey

Source: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/303739858_Implied_Volatility_Forecasting_in_the_Options_Market_A_Survey

Contribution:

Summarizes debate over whether implied volatility dominates historical volatility in realized volatility forecasting.

Use in OPT-001:

Important caution against overclaiming implied-volatility predictive superiority.
