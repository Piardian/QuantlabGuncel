## BACKGROUND

OPT-001 has completed:

- RP-001 Research Prioritization: GO
- LR-001 Literature Review: Proceed to CD-001

LR-001 concluded that options-implied information is scientifically mature and distinct, but it is a broad construct family rather than one construct.

--------------------------------------------------

## PURPOSE

Define the exact OPT-001 construct that will be investigated throughout the remainder of the v3.0 research lifecycle.

The construct must be:

- scientifically justified,
- operationally measurable,
- reproducible,
- narrow enough for preregistered testing,
- distinct from existing constructs where possible.

--------------------------------------------------

## PRIMARY RESEARCH QUESTIONS

1. Which options-implied definition from LR-001 should become the official OPT-001 research construct?

2. Why is this definition preferred over competing alternatives?

3. Which theoretical mechanism does it represent?

4. Which observable variables define the construct?

5. Which observable variables are explicitly excluded, and why?

6. What is the exact mathematical and operational definition?

7. What assumptions does the construct make?

8. What known limitations exist before implementation?

--------------------------------------------------

## CANDIDATE DEFINITION FAMILIES TO REVIEW

Review and accept or reject each before selecting the final construct:

- implied volatility level,
- implied volatility term structure,
- volatility / variance risk premium,
- risk-neutral skewness,
- implied tail risk,
- volatility smirk,
- implied correlation,
- put-call parity deviations,
- option flow / positioning.

--------------------------------------------------

## DECISION CRITERIA

Prioritize:

- literature support,
- theoretical clarity,
- public data availability,
- reproducibility,
- interpretability,
- minimal microstructure assumptions,
- distinction from VOL-001, MR-001, COR-001, LIQ-001, CRD-001, and FUND-001.

Do NOT prioritize expected profitability.

--------------------------------------------------

## FORBIDDEN

Do NOT:

- run backtests,
- evaluate prediction,
- evaluate trading returns,
- optimize thresholds,
- tune parameters,
- implement code,
- claim alpha,
- claim economic value,
- claim production suitability.
