# Research Gap Analysis

## Gap 1: Which Option-Implied Construct Comes First?

The literature supports many options-implied constructs, but CD-001 must select exactly one.

## Gap 2: Public Reproducibility vs Theoretical Richness

Theoretically rich constructs such as risk-neutral skewness require detailed option surface data. Public VIX-style constructs are easier to reproduce but narrower.

## Gap 3: Incremental Value Over VOL-001

OPT-001 may overlap with VOL-001. Future studies must distinguish implied volatility information from realized volatility information.

## Gap 4: Physical Forecast vs Risk Compensation

Options-implied measures can forecast risk, price risk premium, or reflect hedging demand. These are different mechanisms.

## Gap 5: Cross-Sectional vs Market-Level Construct

Option-implied skew and parity deviations often require stock-level option panels. Index-level implied volatility and variance-premium constructs are market-level.

## Gap 6: Data Vendor Dependency

Many academic options studies rely on OptionMetrics or similar vendor data. A reproducible desktop research framework may initially need public index-level series.

## Gap 7: Economic Use Case Unclear

Potential uses include risk monitoring, volatility forecasting, hedge activation, tail-risk warning, and volatility strategy research. None is validated at LR-001.

## CD-001 Implication

CD-001 should prefer a narrow construct with:

- strong literature support,
- public reproducible data,
- clear theoretical interpretation,
- minimal microstructure assumptions.
