# Executive Summary

## Study

OPT-001 reviews options-implied market information as a construct family.

## Main Conclusion

Options-implied information is scientifically mature and distinct enough to proceed to CD-001.

Decision:

```text
Proceed to CD-001
```

## Strongest Literature Areas

- Implied volatility level
- Variance risk premium
- Risk-neutral moments and skewness
- Volatility smirk
- Put-call parity deviations
- Implied correlation

## Most Important Warning

OPT-001 is not one construct yet.

CD-001 must freeze one narrow definition. Mixing VIX, variance premium, skew, smirk, parity deviations, and option flow into one unfrozen construct would be methodologically weak.

## Practical CD-001 Implication

The first OPT construct should likely favor public, reproducible, index-level option-implied data unless the project has reliable access to full option surface data.

## Boundary

No predictive validity, alpha, profitability, economic utility, or production suitability claim is made.
