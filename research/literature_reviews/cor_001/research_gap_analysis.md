# Research Gap Analysis

## Gap 1: Construct Specificity

Market correlation can refer to many different objects. Future work must choose one narrow definition.

Status: Requires CD-001.

## Gap 2: Volatility Bias

Correlation estimates can mechanically change when volatility changes. Any future construct should explicitly address whether it is measuring co-movement itself, volatility-conditioned co-movement, or a combined stress state.

Status: Requires CD-001 and CV-001.

## Gap 3: Universe Dependence

Average correlation can vary depending on whether the universe is broad equities, index constituents, sectors, asset classes, or liquid tradable instruments.

Status: Requires CD-001.

## Gap 4: Realized Versus Implied Correlation

Realized correlation measures observed historical co-movement. Implied correlation measures market-implied expected co-movement from options.

Status: Requires CD-001 decision.

## Gap 5: Linear Versus Tail Dependence

Pearson correlation may not capture crisis co-movement or downside dependence.

Status: Requires LR-to-CD interpretation.

## Gap 6: Portfolio Relevance

A construct may be statistically coherent but economically irrelevant. That cannot be evaluated until EV.

Status: Out of scope until EV.

## Gap 7: Independence From Existing Constructs

COR-001 must later be compared against MR-001, VOL-001, LIQ-001, and BRD-001 to determine whether it contributes a distinct measurement dimension.

Status: Not evaluated in LR-001.
