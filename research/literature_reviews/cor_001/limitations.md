# Literature Limitations

## Measurement Limitations

Correlation estimates are sensitive to:

- sample window
- return frequency
- universe definition
- missing data handling
- volatility regime
- outliers
- non-synchronous trading
- linear dependence assumptions

## Interpretation Limitations

Higher correlation does not automatically imply contagion.

Lower correlation does not automatically imply better portfolio construction.

Correlation is not the same as causation.

Correlation is not the same as volatility.

Correlation is not the same as liquidity stress.

## Data Limitations

Equity-only correlation and cross-asset correlation can answer different questions.

Current-constituent universes can introduce survivorship bias.

Historical constituent datasets may be difficult to obtain.

Options-based implied correlation may not be available for all research periods.

## Methodological Limitations

Pearson correlation captures linear co-movement but may miss nonlinear or tail dependence.

DCC and GARCH-based methods require modeling assumptions.

PCA/eigenvalue methods can be informative but may be harder to interpret as direct correlation.

Network methods can be sensitive to graph construction choices.

## LR-001 Boundary

LR-001 does not resolve these limitations. It identifies them so CD-001 can make a transparent and preregistered construct-definition decision.
