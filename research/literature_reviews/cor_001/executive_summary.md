# Executive Summary

COR-001 / LR-001 reviewed Market Correlation as a financial construct.

Conclusion: proceed to CD-001.

The literature strongly supports correlation as a mature and distinct construct. It is central to portfolio theory, covariance estimation, diversification, systemic risk, contagion/interdependence research, cross-asset allocation, and options-based implied correlation.

The most important finding is not that one correlation measure dominates. The opposite is true: "correlation" can mean multiple related but distinct constructs, including realized average pairwise correlation, cross-asset correlation, implied correlation, dynamic conditional correlation, correlation concentration, and tail dependence.

The main methodological warning is that correlation must be interpreted carefully during high-volatility periods. Observed correlation changes can reflect true co-movement, volatility-induced bias, changing common factors, liquidity stress, or tail dependence.

LR-001 therefore supports COR-001 as a valid candidate for full research, but CD-001 must freeze one narrow operational definition before any implementation or validation occurs.

No predictive, trading, alpha, profitability, or economic value claim is made in LR-001.
