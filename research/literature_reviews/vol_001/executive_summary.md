# Executive Summary

VOL-001 / LR-001 reviews Market Volatility as a financial construct.

## Main Finding

Market Volatility is strongly supported in the literature as a fundamental, measurable and distinct financial construct.

## What Volatility Represents

Volatility represents the magnitude of return or price variation over a defined horizon.

It is a dispersion construct, not a directional signal and not an alpha claim.

## Main Measurement Families

- Close-to-close realized volatility
- High-frequency realized volatility
- Range-based OHLC estimators
- ATR-derived range volatility
- ARCH/GARCH conditional volatility
- Implied volatility such as VIX
- Cross-sectional volatility / dispersion

## Key Scientific Point

Volatility is not one universal scalar. Different estimators represent different dimensions: realized past variation, expected future variation, conditional variance, intraday range or cross-sectional dispersion.

The literature supports moving VOL-001 to `CD-001`.

CD-001 must freeze one precise operational volatility construct.

## Boundary

LR-001 does not select the final estimator and does not evaluate prediction, economic value, alpha, or production suitability.
