# RESEARCH PROGRAM

Market Signal Discovery Program

Version 3.0

Construct ID

BRD-001

Construct Family

Market Breadth

Construct Definition

CD-001

--------------------------------------------------

BACKGROUND

--------------------------------------------------

BRD-001 has completed:

Completed: RP-001 Research Prioritization

Completed: LR-001 Literature Review

LR-001 concluded that Market Breadth is a recognized market-internals construct measuring participation across securities.

The literature supports proceeding to construct definition.

No final breadth indicator has been selected yet.

--------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE

--------------------------------------------------

Define the exact Market Breadth construct that will be investigated throughout the remainder of the V3.0 research pipeline.

The construct must be:

- scientifically justified

- operationally measurable

- deterministic

- reproducible

- sufficiently narrow for preregistered testing

--------------------------------------------------

PRIMARY RESEARCH QUESTIONS

--------------------------------------------------

1.

Which Market Breadth definition from LR-001 should become the official BRD-001 research construct?

2.

Why is this definition preferred over competing breadth alternatives?

3.

Which theoretical framework does it belong to?

4.

Which observable variables define the construct?

Examples may include:

- advancing securities

- declining securities

- percentage above moving average

- new highs / new lows

- up-volume / down-volume

- sector participation

Only include variables supported by LR-001.

5.

Which observable variables are intentionally excluded?

Explain why.

6.

What is the exact operational definition?

The construct must be expressed so that two independent researchers could implement it identically.

7.

What assumptions does the construct make?

8.

What known limitations exist before any empirical testing begins?

--------------------------------------------------

DECISION CRITERIA

--------------------------------------------------

Selection should prioritize:

- strength of literature support

- theoretical clarity

- operational simplicity

- reproducibility

- data availability

- measurement reliability

NOT expected predictive performance.

--------------------------------------------------

FORBIDDEN

--------------------------------------------------

Do NOT:

- Run backtests.

- Optimize thresholds.

- Tune parameters.

- Select the construct because it looks profitable.

- Invent new breadth theories.

- Mix incompatible breadth definitions without justification.

- Evaluate predictive validity.

- Evaluate economic value.

--------------------------------------------------

EXPECTED OUTPUTS

--------------------------------------------------

Generate:

construct_specification.md

variable_definition_table.csv

construct_assumptions.md

executive_summary.md

--------------------------------------------------

SUCCESS CRITERIA

--------------------------------------------------

At the conclusion of CD-001, the following questions must have unambiguous answers:

- Which Market Breadth definition has been selected?

- Why was it selected?

- Which alternatives were rejected?

- Which observable variables define the construct?

- Which variables are explicitly excluded?

- Can an independent researcher reproduce the construct without ambiguity?

The construct definition becomes frozen after CD-001.

Any future modification requires a new preregistered construct and may not alter the current research pipeline.

No statements regarding predictive validity, trading performance, alpha generation, economic value, or production suitability are permitted.

The sole purpose of CD-001 is to establish a precise and reproducible research construct for subsequent validation stages.
