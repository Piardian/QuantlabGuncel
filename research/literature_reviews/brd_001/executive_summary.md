# Executive Summary

BRD-001 / LR-001 reviews Market Breadth as a financial construct.

## Main Finding

Market Breadth is supported as a distinct market-internals construct measuring participation across securities.

The literature supports proceeding to `CD-001`.

## What Breadth Represents

Breadth measures whether market movement is broadly shared or narrowly concentrated.

It is a participation construct, not a return forecast, alpha signal, or profitability claim.

## Main Measurement Families

- Advance / decline participation
- Up-volume / down-volume breadth
- New high / new low breadth
- Percentage above moving averages
- Breadth momentum / breadth thrust
- Sector breadth
- Equal-weighted versus cap-weighted confirmation

## Key Scientific Point

Market Breadth is well recognized but not universally standardized.

CD-001 must freeze one precise operational definition before implementation or validation.

## Final LR-001 Conclusion

BRD-001 should proceed to `CD-001`.

## Boundary

LR-001 does not select the final breadth indicator and does not evaluate prediction, economic value, alpha, or production suitability.
