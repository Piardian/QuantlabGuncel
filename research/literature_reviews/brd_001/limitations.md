# BRD-001 / LR-001: Limitations

## Scope Limitation

LR-001 is a literature review.

It does not run empirical tests, backtests, predictive validation, or economic validation.

## Definition Limitation

Market Breadth has no single universal definition.

Different breadth indicators can measure different aspects of participation.

## Universe Limitation

Breadth is highly sensitive to universe construction.

Key issues include:

- Current constituents versus historical constituents
- Survivorship bias
- IPOs and delistings
- Exchange coverage
- Sector composition
- Small-cap versus large-cap inclusion

## Data Quality Limitation

Some breadth measures require clean constituent-level historical data.

Volume-based breadth additionally requires reliable volume data.

New-high/new-low breadth requires sufficiently long security histories.

## Methodological Limitation

Breadth can overlap with other constructs:

- Trend
- Momentum
- Market regime
- Volatility
- Liquidity stress
- Cross-sectional dispersion

CD-001 must define BRD-001 narrowly enough to avoid construct ambiguity.

## Literature Limitation

Practitioner literature is extensive, but academic standardization is weaker than for volatility or liquidity.

This means construct definition must be especially careful.

## Interpretation Limitation

LR-001 cannot support statements about:

- Predictive power
- Alpha
- Profitability
- Drawdown reduction
- Production readiness
- Economic value

Those questions belong to later lifecycle stages.
