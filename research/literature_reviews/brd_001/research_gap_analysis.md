# BRD-001 / LR-001: Research Gap Analysis

## Purpose

This document identifies open questions that CD-001 and later BRD-001 stages may need to address.

## Gap 1: No Single Canonical Breadth Definition

The literature supports breadth as a concept but does not identify one universal operational definition.

CD-001 must therefore select a precise construct using scientific clarity and reproducibility, not expected performance.

## Gap 2: Universe Construction

Breadth measurement depends heavily on which securities are included.

Open question:

Should BRD-001 measure participation in a broad US equity universe, a benchmark constituent universe, or a liquid large-cap universe?

This must be resolved in CD-001.

## Gap 3: Participation Versus Trend Participation

Moving-average breadth can measure trend participation rather than raw daily participation.

Open question:

Should BRD-001 represent daily participation, trend participation, or broad market-internal state?

## Gap 4: Equal-Weighted Versus Cap-Weighted Interpretation

Breadth generally treats securities more equally than capitalization-weighted indices.

Open question:

Should BRD-001 intentionally measure equal-weighted participation, or should capitalization-adjusted participation be considered?

## Gap 5: Breadth and Existing Constructs

Breadth may interact with MR-001, LIQ-001, and VOL-001.

Open question:

Does breadth provide a distinct internal participation signal beyond regime, liquidity, and volatility constructs?

This cannot be tested until after BRD-001 completes construct definition, implementation, and validation.

## Gap 6: Historical Constituent Bias

Breadth measures are vulnerable to survivorship bias when computed from current constituents.

Open question:

Can the project obtain sufficiently clean historical constituents, or must it explicitly accept a fixed-universe limitation?

## Gap 7: Breadth Timing

Breadth may be noisy at a daily frequency and may require smoothing to represent stable participation state.

Open question:

Should BRD-001 be a raw participation construct or a smoothed breadth-state construct?

This must be decided before implementation and without performance-based selection.
