--------------------------------------------------

## BACKGROUND

FUND-001 has completed:

- RP-001 Research Prioritization: GO
- LR-001 Literature Review: GO to CD-001

LR-001 concluded that Funding Stress is a scientifically recognized and distinct construct, but its empirical measurement is proxy-sensitive.

The key CD-001 challenge is selecting one narrow, reproducible funding-stress definition without mixing incompatible funding, credit, liquidity, volatility, and broad financial-condition concepts.

--------------------------------------------------

## PURPOSE

Define the exact Funding Stress construct that will be investigated throughout the remainder of the v3.0 research lifecycle.

The construct must be:

- scientifically justified,
- operationally measurable,
- reproducible,
- narrow enough for preregistered testing,
- distinct from existing constructs where possible.

--------------------------------------------------

## PRIMARY RESEARCH QUESTIONS

1. Which Funding Stress definition from LR-001 should become the official FUND-001 research construct?

2. Why is this definition preferred over competing alternatives?

3. Which theoretical funding-stress mechanism does it represent?

4. Which observable variables define the construct?

5. Which observable variables are explicitly excluded, and why?

6. What is the exact mathematical and operational definition?

7. What assumptions does the construct make?

8. What known limitations exist before implementation?

--------------------------------------------------

## CANDIDATE DEFINITION FAMILIES TO REVIEW

Review and accept or reject each before selecting the final construct:

- spread-based unsecured funding stress,
- SOFR or reference-rate based funding stress,
- repo / secured funding stress,
- institutional funding-stress index component,
- dealer balance-sheet funding capacity,
- central-bank liquidity operations stress,
- narrow composite funding stress.

--------------------------------------------------

## DECISION CRITERIA

Prioritize:

- literature support,
- theoretical clarity,
- public data availability,
- daily or sufficiently frequent measurement,
- reproducibility,
- interpretability,
- distinction from CRD-001, LIQ-001, VOL-001, COR-001, BRD-001, and MR-001.

Do NOT prioritize expected profitability.

--------------------------------------------------

## FORBIDDEN

Do NOT:

- run backtests,
- evaluate prediction,
- evaluate trading returns,
- optimize thresholds,
- tune parameters,
- implement code,
- claim economic value,
- claim production suitability.

CD-001 is successful if it unambiguously answers:

- Which Funding Stress definition has been selected?
- Why was it selected?
- Which alternatives were rejected?
