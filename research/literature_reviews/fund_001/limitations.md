# FUND-001 / LR-001

# Limitations

## No Construct Definition Yet

LR-001 does not select the official FUND-001 construct. It only reviews the literature.

## Measurement Ambiguity

Funding Stress is not directly observable. Most measures are proxies.

This creates risk that a chosen variable may measure:

- credit risk,
- market liquidity,
- volatility,
- counterparty risk,
- monetary policy expectations,
- or broad financial stress,

instead of funding stress alone.

## LIBOR Transition

Many historical funding stress measures use LIBOR. Because LIBOR has been discontinued and underlying bank funding markets changed materially, LIBOR-based proxies require explicit treatment in CD-001.

## Proxy Instability

The same proxy may not have identical meaning across decades. Market structure, central-bank policy, collateral practices, and benchmark-rate conventions can change.

## Data Availability

Some theoretically attractive measures are not easily available as long, clean, public, daily time series.

Examples:

- haircuts,
- margin requirements,
- dealer funding terms,
- institution-level secured funding conditions.

## Construct Overlap

Funding Stress overlaps empirically with:

- Credit Stress,
- Liquidity,
- Volatility,
- Correlation Stress,
- Market Regime.

CD-001 must explicitly state how FUND-001 differs from previously studied constructs.

## Jurisdiction Risk

Some measurement approaches are designed for European central-bank operations or specific banking systems. They may not transfer cleanly to a US equity market research platform.

## No Predictive Or Economic Claims

This literature review does not support any claim about predictive power, trading edge, portfolio improvement, or economic value.
