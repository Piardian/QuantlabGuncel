# FUND-001 / LR-001

# Executive Summary

## Study

FUND-001 evaluates whether Funding Stress is a scientifically mature construct worthy of a full research program.

LR-001 reviewed academic and practitioner literature only. It did not define, implement, test, or economically evaluate the construct.

## Main Conclusion

Funding Stress is a recognized and scientifically meaningful market construct.

Decision: Proceed to CD-001.

## What The Literature Supports

- Funding stress is distinct from market liquidity, credit stress, volatility, and broad financial conditions.
- Funding stress is especially important during crisis periods.
- Funding and market liquidity can reinforce each other through margin, collateral, and deleveraging mechanisms.
- Funding conditions are commonly represented in institutional financial stress frameworks.

## Main Caution

Funding Stress is difficult to measure cleanly.

Most available proxies contain contamination from credit risk, liquidity conditions, monetary policy, or broad financial stress. LIBOR-based measures are especially problematic after the LIBOR transition.

## CD-001 Implication

The next stage must select one narrow, reproducible Funding Stress definition.

The highest-risk methodological decision will be proxy choice.

## Forbidden Interpretation

LR-001 does not support any claim about prediction, alpha, profitability, economic value, or production readiness.
