# FUND-001 / IM-001

# Limitations

- FUND-001 implements a commercial-paper-to-Treasury-bill spread proxy only.
- It does not measure repo funding, margin haircuts, dealer balance-sheet constraints, or central-bank facility usage.
- It does not decompose funding stress from credit risk, counterparty risk, safe-asset demand, or policy-rate effects.
- It does not forward fill official construct values; missing source dates remain missing.
- FRED data revisions can affect future reruns unless input snapshots are archived.
- No predictive, trading, alpha, or economic claim is made.
