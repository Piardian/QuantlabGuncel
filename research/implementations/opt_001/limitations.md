# OPT-001 / IM-001

# Limitations

- OPT-001 implements VIXCLS implied-volatility state only.
- It does not reconstruct the SPX option surface.
- It does not decompose expected volatility, variance risk premium, hedging demand, risk aversion, or tail-risk compensation.
- It does not forward fill official construct values; missing source dates remain missing.
- FRED/Cboe data revisions can affect future reruns unless input snapshots are archived.
- No predictive, trading, alpha, or economic claim is made.
