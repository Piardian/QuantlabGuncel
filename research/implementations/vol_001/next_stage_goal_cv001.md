## PURPOSE

Evaluate whether the implemented VOL-001 construct demonstrates the expected characteristics of a valid market volatility-state construct.

This study evaluates construct validity only.

No predictive or economic conclusions are permitted.

--------------------------------------------------

## PRIMARY RESEARCH QUESTIONS

1.

Does VOL-001 generate internally coherent volatility-state observations?

2.

Are the rolling volatility, z-score and percentile outputs statistically stable and interpretable?

3.

Do high volatility-state observations align with recognizable historical volatility stress periods?

4.

Are warmup behavior, missingness and eligibility diagnostics consistent with CD-001?

5.

Does the empirical behavior remain consistent with theoretical expectations documented in LR-001?
