# HG-002 Future Research Questions

These are questions only. They are not experiment designs, strategy recommendations, or claims that any result is expected.

1. Does multi-horizon relative strength associate with post-entry continuation in candidate signals rather than only production-selected trades?
2. Does persistent relative strength remain associated with outcomes after accounting for the existing selection pipeline and portfolio constraints?
3. Does the relationship differ when SPY is replaced by sector, industry, or style benchmarks?
4. Does relative-strength persistence retain a relationship after matching on beta, volatility, liquidity, market capitalization, and sector exposure?
5. Does the relationship differ around earnings releases or other identifiable information events?
6. Does persistent relative strength show horizon-dependent continuation followed by reversal?
7. Are independently observed institutional flows, ownership changes, or order-flow proxies aligned with multi-horizon relative strength?
8. Are any observed relationships stable across market regimes, sectors, and genuinely disjoint multi-period samples?
9. Does relative-strength persistence remain observable under a shared-capital portfolio and documented execution assumptions?
10. Is the current binary operationalization less informative than an alternative measurement construct, without assuming that an alternative should enter production?
