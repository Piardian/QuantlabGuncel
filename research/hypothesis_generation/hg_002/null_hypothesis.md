# HG-002 Null Hypothesis

## Conservative Null

Persistent Relative Strength, as currently operationalized by the RS20/RS60/RS120 production conditions, has no independent and reproducible relationship with future trade quality within `leadership_expansion_v1`.

Any observed production performance can exist without an independent Persistent Relative Strength contribution because it may arise from one or more of the following non-exclusive explanations:

- joint selection by other production filters and their interactions;
- position, exit, risk-sizing, and path-dependent execution effects;
- selected-trade conditioning in observational analyses;
- market, sector, factor, volatility, or benchmark confounding;
- historical regime dependence;
- current-constituent survivorship bias and data construction choices;
- modeled execution and cost assumptions;
- sampling variation, multiple testing, or random coincidence.

This null is consistent with the completed RC-A through RC-E program: Relative Strength has not been directly tested in a completed intervention, RC-C1 did not observe a meaningful stable RS association within selected trades, and RC-D1 did not establish general OOS reproducibility.

Rejecting this null would require future, separately designed evidence. This document does not specify an experiment or claim that rejection is likely.
