# CPP-000

# Methodological Boundaries

## Confirmatory vs Exploratory

CPP-000 performs no confirmatory or exploratory statistical analysis.

CPP-000.5 must pre-register which analyses are confirmatory and which, if any, are exploratory.

## Immutable Evidence

All prior FSR conclusions are frozen. CPP may compare constructs, but may not change construct definitions, implementations, classifications, or scientific maturity labels.

## Interpretation Standard

Allowed classifications:

- Supported by evidence
- Partially supported
- Not supported
- Inconclusive

Forbidden interpretation patterns:

- Treating correlation as causation.
- Treating redundancy as construct invalidation.
- Treating orthogonality as economic usefulness.
- Treating complementarity as trading edge.
- Treating incremental information as production recommendation.
