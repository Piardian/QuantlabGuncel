# RESEARCH PROGRAM

Construct Comparison Program (CPP)

CPP-007

Limited Information Architecture Synthesis

--------------------------------------------------

## BACKGROUND

CPP-001 completed data inventory and found pairwise readiness but insufficient all-construct common sample for multivariate stages.

CPP-002 completed pairwise dependence mapping.

CPP-003 found incremental information analysis not eligible.

CPP-004 completed limited-scope pairwise evidence review.

CPP-005 found complementarity and multivariate information analysis not eligible.

CPP-006 lead-lag analysis is also not eligible under current sample rules.

--------------------------------------------------

## PURPOSE

Synthesize the available CPP evidence into a limited information architecture summary.

The synthesis must explicitly distinguish observed pairwise association evidence from unavailable incremental, multivariate, and lead-lag evidence.

--------------------------------------------------

## FORBIDDEN

Do NOT infer complete information architecture, redundancy, orthogonality, complementarity, hierarchy, causality, predictive value, economic value, or production relevance beyond the available evidence.

--------------------------------------------------

## EXPECTED OUTPUTS

- `cpp007_limited_information_architecture.md`
- `construct_information_matrix.csv`
- `available_evidence_summary.md`
- `unavailable_evidence_summary.md`
- `architecture_limitations.md`
- `executive_summary.md`

--------------------------------------------------

## SUCCESS CRITERIA

CPP-007 succeeds if it produces a bounded synthesis that preserves scientific constraints and clearly identifies which architecture questions remain unanswered.
