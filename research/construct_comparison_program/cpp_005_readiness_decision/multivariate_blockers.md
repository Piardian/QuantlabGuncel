# CPP-005 Multivariate Blockers

## Primary Blocker

CPP-000.5 preregistered `N >= 756` common aligned observations for complementarity and multivariate information analysis. CPP-001 found only `188` common valid observations across all eight frozen construct alignment series.

## Lead-Lag Consequence

CPP-006 requires `N >= 756` aligned observations after lagging. With a 60-day lag buffer, the available common sample would be `128`, so CPP-006 is also not eligible under the current protocol.

## Consequence for CPP

The current CPP evidence body can support pairwise dependence mapping and limited evidence review only. It cannot support complete multivariate information architecture claims.

## Non-Implications

This does not imply complementarity is absent. It does not imply hierarchy is absent. It does not imply constructs lack independent information. It only means those questions cannot be answered under the current preregistered data eligibility rules.
