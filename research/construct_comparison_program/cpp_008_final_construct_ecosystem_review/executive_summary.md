# CPP-008 Executive Summary

## Final Status

`LIMITED_PAIRWISE_ARCHITECTURE_ONLY`

## What CPP Established

CPP established that the eight frozen constructs can be audited and compared pairwise under the current data outputs. It produced a bounded pairwise association map and limited pairwise evidence profile.

## What CPP Did Not Establish

CPP did not establish independent information contribution, redundancy, orthogonality, complementarity, hierarchy, lead-lag structure, predictive value, economic value, or production relevance.

## Why

The all-construct common sample was only 188 observations, below the preregistered 756-observation threshold required for incremental and multivariate analysis.

## Final Scientific Conclusion

CPP is scientifically complete as a limited pairwise architecture review, but incomplete as a full construct ecosystem architecture.
