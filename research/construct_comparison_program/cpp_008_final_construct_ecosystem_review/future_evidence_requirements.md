# CPP-008 Future Evidence Requirements

A complete construct information architecture would require one of the following before further statistical work:

## Option 1: Longer Frozen Output History

Regenerate or extend frozen construct outputs under a new preregistered data remediation protocol, especially for constructs whose normalized histories limit common-sample availability.

## Option 2: Predefined Construct Subset Architecture

Preregister construct subsets with sufficient common sample before analysis. Subsets must be selected before observing statistical relationship results.

## Option 3: Staged Architecture Program

Separate short-history macro/credit constructs from long-history equity-market constructs, with predefined within-family and cross-family protocols.

## Required Analyses For Complete Architecture

- Incremental information analysis.
- Conditional dependence analysis.
- Multivariate complementarity analysis.
- Robustness and sensitivity checks.
- Lead-lag temporal association analysis.
- Scientific interpretation review.

No future program should convert pairwise association into final ecosystem classification without these additional evidence layers.
