# CPP-008 Final Limitations

## Primary Limitation

The all-construct common sample contains only 188 valid observations, below the preregistered 756-observation threshold for incremental and multivariate analyses.

## Consequences

- Independent information contribution cannot be evaluated.
- Conditional redundancy cannot be evaluated.
- Orthogonality cannot be definitively classified.
- Complementarity cannot be evaluated.
- Lead-lag hierarchy cannot be evaluated.
- Complete construct ecosystem architecture cannot be established.

## Interpretation Limitation

Pairwise association evidence can be informative but is not sufficient to classify ecosystem information structure.

## Scope Limitation

CPP did not evaluate trading strategies, alpha, predictive power, economic value, or production use.
