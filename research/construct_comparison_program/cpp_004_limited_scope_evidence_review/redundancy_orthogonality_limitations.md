# CPP-004 Redundancy & Orthogonality Limitations

## Primary Limitation

CPP-004 cannot complete a full redundancy or orthogonality classification because CPP-003 incremental information analysis is not eligible under the current common-sample rule.

## Why Pairwise Evidence Is Insufficient

Pairwise association can show that two construct series co-move, but it cannot determine whether one construct adds no information beyond another, whether apparent association is mediated by a third construct, or whether two constructs are conditionally independent inside the full ecosystem.

## Unavailable Evidence

- Partial dependence evidence.
- Conditional mutual information evidence.
- Nested model delta evidence.
- Multivariate factor / common-component evidence.
- Lead-lag evidence.

## Valid Conclusion Boundary

CPP-004 may identify pairs with high, moderate, mixed, or low pairwise association evidence. It may not classify constructs as definitively redundant or orthogonal.
