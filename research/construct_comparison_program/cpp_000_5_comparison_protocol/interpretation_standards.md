# CPP-000.5

# Interpretation Standards

## Allowed Statements

- Construct A overlaps statistically with Construct B.
- Construct A provides incremental information beyond Construct B under the preregistered model.
- Construct A and Construct B appear partially redundant.
- Construct A appears temporally upstream of Construct B under lead-lag association tests.
- Evidence is inconclusive under minimum sample or stability criteria.

## Forbidden Statements

- Construct A causes Construct B.
- Construct A is better than Construct B in trading terms.
- Construct A should be added to a trading strategy.
- Construct A is profitable.
- Construct A invalidates Construct B.
- Redundant constructs are useless.

## Language Rule

Use `temporal association`, not `causality`, unless a future causal identification design is explicitly preregistered.
