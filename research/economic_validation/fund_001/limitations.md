# Limitations

- EV-001 uses predefined simple exposure ladders, not optimized policies.
- Policies are SPY/cash exposure proxies; no real hedge instrument, transaction cost, tax, financing, or slippage model is included.
- Results are limited to the overlap between FUND-001 and available SPY/VOL output.
- FUND-001 remains a mixed funding-spread sensor, not a pure funding-liquidity construct.
- No universal production superiority is claimed.
- No alpha discovery claim is made.
