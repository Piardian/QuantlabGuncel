# EV-001 Limitations

## Short Sample

The economic validation sample is short and begins after CRD-001 normalized outputs become available. This limits robustness and generalizability.

## Simple Risk Policies

Policies are intentionally simple and predefined. They are not optimized and may not represent best possible use of the construct.

## Cash Assumption

Unused exposure is assumed to remain in cash with zero return. Financing, transaction costs, taxes, and implementation frictions are not modeled.

## No Universal Claim

Results apply only to the predefined use cases and sample evaluated in EV-001.
