# OPT-001 / EV-001

# Limitations

- EV-001 evaluates only predefined risk-management workflows.
- The analysis uses a simplified SPY exposure model, not live order execution.
- Dynamic hedge activation is represented as net exposure reduction, not as an options or futures hedge implementation.
- Transaction costs are modeled as a simple 1 bp exposure-turnover cost.
- Policies were fixed before execution and were not optimized for performance.
- Results do not imply alpha, universal superiority, or production readiness.
- Conclusions are limited to the evaluated workflows, dataset, assumptions, and benchmark definitions.
