# Executive Summary

COR-001 / EV-001 evaluated whether the Market Correlation construct provides economic utility in predefined risk-management workflows.

Overall result:

```text
Supported by evidence
```

Use-case classifications:

- UC-1 Diversification Risk Budgeting: Supported by evidence
- UC-2 Correlation-Aware Volatility Targeting: Supported by evidence
- UC-3 Correlation Stress Risk Control: Partially supported
- UC-4 Regime-Aware Portfolio De-Risking Support: Partially supported

COR-001 showed measurable utility as a risk-management construct. Its clearest use cases are diversification-risk budgeting and volatility targeting.

The evidence does not support COR-001 as an alpha or return-enhancement construct.

No production-deployment claim is made.

COR-001 is ready for CC-001 after human approval.
