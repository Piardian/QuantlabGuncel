# EV-001 Limitations

## Predefined Workflow Scope

EV-001 evaluates only four predefined risk-management workflows.

No conclusions may be generalized beyond these workflows.

## No Alpha Claim

COR-001 is not evaluated as an alpha signal.

The tested utility is risk-management utility.

## No Optimization

Policy exposures and volatility targets were fixed before analysis.

No thresholds or parameters were optimized after observing results.

## Simplified Execution

The analysis does not model transaction costs, slippage, taxes, margin constraints, borrow constraints, or implementation frictions.

## Single Market Proxy

Economic validation used SPY as the market proxy.

## Universe Limitation

COR-001 uses a fixed current US equity universe and Yahoo Finance data, so historical survivorship and data-source limitations remain.

## Production Boundary

EV-001 does not authorize production deployment.

Operational review, cost modeling, monitoring design, and live/paper validation would be required before any production use.
