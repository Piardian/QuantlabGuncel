# Benchmark Comparison

## Purpose

Compare each predefined COR-001 risk-management workflow against its predefined benchmark.

## Summary

| Use Case | Benchmark | Classification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UC-1 | Static 0.75 exposure | Supported by evidence |
| UC-2 | Static 12% volatility target | Supported by evidence |
| UC-3 | Static 0.75 exposure | Partially supported |
| UC-4 | Buy-and-hold | Partially supported |

## Supported Findings

COR-001 improved risk metrics in the strongest predefined workflows:

- UC-1 Diversification Risk Budgeting
- UC-2 Correlation-Aware Volatility Targeting

## Partial Findings

UC-3 showed favorable full-sample metrics but weak cross-period robustness.

UC-4 reduced risk materially but produced a large return penalty relative to buy-and-hold.

## Boundary

Benchmark comparison is limited to the predefined workflows.

No claim is made about production deployment or universal superiority.
