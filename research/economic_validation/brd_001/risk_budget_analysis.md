# BRD-001 / EV-001: Risk Budget Analysis

## Use Case

UC-1 Risk Budgeting.

## Policy

BRD policy:

- LOW_BREADTH: 0.50 exposure
- MID_BREADTH: 0.75 exposure
- HIGH_BREADTH: 1.00 exposure

Benchmark:

- Static 0.75 exposure

## Results

| Metric | BRD Policy | Benchmark | Delta |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| CAGR | 0.0896 | 0.0939 | -0.0043 |
| Annualized volatility | 0.1105 | 0.1287 | -0.0182 |
| Max drawdown | -0.1988 | -0.2644 | +0.0656 |
| Downside volatility | 0.0859 | 0.1050 | -0.0190 |
| Average exposure | 0.7499 | 0.7500 | -0.0001 |

## Assessment

BRD-001 improved risk allocation by reducing volatility, downside volatility and max drawdown while preserving nearly identical average exposure.

CAGR was slightly lower than benchmark.
