# BRD-001 / EV-001: Robustness Analysis

## Purpose

Evaluate whether economic utility is stable across broad historical blocks.

## Period Findings

Risk reduction was generally observed across periods, especially in:

- lower annualized volatility
- lower downside volatility
- shallower drawdown during stress-heavy periods

## Important Nuance

CAGR improvement was not uniform across periods.

BRD-001 is therefore not supported as a general return-enhancement construct.

## Strongest Period Behavior

The BRD policies were most economically useful during periods containing substantial market stress or trend deterioration, such as 2018-2021 and 2022-2025.

## Classification

```text
Partially supported
```

Reason:

Risk-control evidence is robust, but return improvement is not stable.
