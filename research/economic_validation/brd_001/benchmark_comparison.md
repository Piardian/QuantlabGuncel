## Main Benchmark Finding

BRD-001 consistently improved risk metrics across predefined workflows.

The evidence is strongest when BRD-001 is used as a risk-state control input rather than as a return-enhancement mechanism.

## Boundary

Benchmark comparisons are limited to the predefined workflows and fixed exposure maps.

No optimized policy was tested.
