# BRD-001 / EV-001: Limitations

## Fixed Policy Limitation

The tested exposure maps were fixed before analysis and not optimized.

Different maps may produce different results, but they were not tested in EV-001.

## Single Asset Proxy

Economic validation used SPY as the market exposure proxy.

This does not prove utility across all portfolios.

## Universe Limitation

BRD-001 uses a fixed current universe, not survivorship-free historical constituents.

## Cost Limitation

The analysis does not include transaction costs, taxes or implementation frictions.

Turnover was reported as a diagnostic.

## No Alpha Claim

BRD-001 is not validated as an alpha signal.

Its supported economic role is risk management.

## Generalization Limitation

Conclusions apply only to the predefined EV-001 use cases and benchmarks.

They must not be generalized to all trading or portfolio workflows.
