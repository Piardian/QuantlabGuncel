# BRD-001 / FSR-001: Known Limitations

## Universe Limitation

BRD-001 uses a fixed current universe:

```text
sp500_current_universe.csv
```

It is not survivorship-free historical constituents.

## Data Vendor Limitation

Full-market outputs depend on Yahoo Finance historical data availability and revisions.

## Construct Scope

BRD-001 is only 200-day moving-average breadth.

It does not cover all breadth definitions.

## Predictive Scope

Predictive evidence supports risk-state outcomes more than returns.

Future returns were not supported.

## Economic Scope

Economic validation used predefined fixed workflows and SPY market exposure.

No optimized policy, multi-asset portfolio deployment, transaction costs, taxes or live execution were evaluated.

## Deployment Scope

Production readiness remains inconclusive.

Operational validation is required before deployment.
