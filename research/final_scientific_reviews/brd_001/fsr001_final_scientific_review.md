## Supported Scientific Claims

BRD-001 is supported as a market breadth construct measuring long-term cross-sectional trend participation.

BRD-001 is supported as an internal market confirmation sensor.

BRD-001 is supported as a risk-state predictive construct for future realized volatility and future trend deterioration risk.

BRD-001 is supported as economically useful in predefined risk-management workflows.

## Partially Supported Claims

Future drawdown risk prediction is partially supported.

Portfolio risk control versus buy-and-hold is partially supported because risk metrics improved materially but CAGR penalty was material.

## Not Supported Claims

BRD-001 is not supported as:

- a future return prediction construct
- a standalone alpha signal
- a return-enhancement construct
- a standalone buy/sell signal
- a universal market timing system

## Inconclusive Claims

Production deployment readiness is inconclusive.

Operational review, monitoring, execution assumptions and governance were not evaluated in this lifecycle.

## Final Assessment

BRD-001 is a completed construct within the Market Signal Discovery Program.

Market Breadth / Risk Management Construct

The completed evidence does not support BRD-001 as:
