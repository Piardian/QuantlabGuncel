# BRD-001 / FSR-001: Recommended Applications

## Supported Applications

BRD-001 is scientifically justified for:

- market breadth monitoring
- internal market participation dashboards
- risk-state monitoring
- volatility-risk awareness
- trend deterioration risk monitoring
- risk budgeting support
- volatility targeting support
- trend deterioration risk control workflows

## Partially Supported Applications

BRD-001 is partially justified for:

- broad portfolio risk control versus buy-and-hold
- future drawdown risk awareness

## Usage Boundary

Recommended applications are limited to research and risk-management contexts supported by completed evidence.

They do not imply production deployment approval.
