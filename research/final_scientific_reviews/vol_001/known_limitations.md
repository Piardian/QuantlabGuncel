# Known Limitations

## Proxy Limitation

VOL-001 uses SPY as the sole US equity market proxy.

## Data Frequency Limitation

VOL-001 uses daily OHLC data. It does not use intraday price sampling.

## Measurement Scope

VOL-001 is a Yang-Zhang realized-volatility construct. It does not measure implied volatility, GARCH conditional variance, ATR or cross-sectional dispersion.

## Drawdown Limitation

PV-001 did not support future drawdown-risk prediction. That result remained inconclusive.

## Economic Validation Limitation

EV-001 was partially supported. Dynamic de-risking was not supported.

## Implementation-Cost Limitation

EV-001 did not model transaction costs, financing costs, taxes or operational implementation costs.

## Live Validation Limitation

The research remains historical and simulated. No live operational validation has been completed.

## No Alpha Claim

VOL-001 was not validated as a standalone alpha factor or directional return predictor.

## No Universal Validity Claim

Conclusions apply only to the evaluated construct definition, data, horizons and predefined workflows.
