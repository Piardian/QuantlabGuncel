# VOL-001 Construct Maturity Report

## Primary Category

**Volatility State / Risk Forecasting Construct**

## Secondary Capabilities

- Realized market turbulence detection
- Future realized volatility risk indication
- Future absolute market movement indication
- Future high-volatility occurrence indication
- Volatility-aware risk budgeting support
- Volatility-aware portfolio risk-control support

## Scientific Maturity Level

**High**

## Evidence Strength

**Strong for volatility-state measurement, mechanism validity and volatility-risk prediction; partial for economic utility.**

## Maturity Assessment

### Supported by Evidence

- Literature recognition of volatility as a distinct construct.
- Deterministic implementation of the frozen CD-001 specification.
- Construct validity.
- Mechanism validity.
- Hypothesis validation.
- Predictive information for future realized volatility, absolute movement and high-volatility occurrence.
- Engineering reproducibility.

### Partially Supported

- Economic utility across predefined workflows.
- Volatility targeting support.

### Inconclusive

- Future drawdown-risk prediction.

### Not Supported

- Alpha generation.
- Standalone directional return prediction.
- Exact drawdown forecasting.
- VIX or implied-volatility substitution.
- Dynamic de-risking as tested in UC-3.

## Overall Maturity

VOL-001 has completed the full V3.0 scientific lifecycle and is mature enough to be treated as a completed validated volatility-state construct within the evaluated assumptions.

Its maturity is not a license for universal deployment, alpha claims or unrestricted production use.
