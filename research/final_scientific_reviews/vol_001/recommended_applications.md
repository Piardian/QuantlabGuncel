# Recommended Applications

## Supported by Evidence

### Daily Volatility-State Monitoring

VOL-001 is appropriate as a daily realized volatility-state monitor for US equities using SPY as the market proxy.

### Market Turbulence Detection

VOL-001 is appropriate for identifying market turbulence states driven by larger overnight, intraday and range-based movement.

### Future Volatility-Risk Warning

VOL-001 is appropriate as a risk-state input for future realized volatility and future high-volatility occurrence.

### Volatility-Aware Risk Budgeting

VOL-001 is supported for predefined risk-budgeting workflows evaluated in EV-001.

### Volatility-Aware Portfolio Risk Control

VOL-001 is supported for predefined portfolio risk-control workflows evaluated in EV-001.

## Partially Supported

### Volatility Targeting Support

VOL-001 may support volatility targeting, but EV-001 classified this use case as partially supported rather than fully supported.

## Not Recommended

### Dynamic De-Risking

The specific dynamic de-risking workflow tested in EV-001 was not supported.

## Use Boundary

These recommendations apply only within the evaluated V3.0 research scope. They do not imply production deployment, universal superiority or alpha generation.
