# Future Research Recommendations

## Supported by Evidence

### Alternative Market Proxy Replication

Replicate VOL-001-style validation on SPX, total-market ETFs or broader market baskets as separate preregistered constructs or replication studies.

### Data-Provider Replication

Replicate the construct using an institutional daily OHLC data source to confirm robustness beyond Yahoo Finance snapshots.

### Cross-Construct Comparison

Compare VOL-001 with MR-001 and LIQ-001 only through a preregistered cross-construct synthesis after each construct is completed.

## Partially Supported

### Workflow-Specific EV Expansion

Additional predefined risk-control workflows may be tested in future EV studies, but not by modifying VOL-001 retroactively.

### Volatility Targeting Refinement

Future research may evaluate volatility targeting more carefully with preregistered, non-optimized policy specifications.

## Not Recommended Without New Preregistration

- Turning VOL-001 into an alpha signal.
- Using VOL-001 as a direct entry or exit trigger.
- Replacing implied volatility or VIX with VOL-001.
- Changing the Yang-Zhang estimator or windows inside the existing construct lifecycle.
- Optimizing exposure thresholds after observing EV-001 results.

## Methodological Rule

Any material construct modification requires a new preregistered construct definition and a new lifecycle.
