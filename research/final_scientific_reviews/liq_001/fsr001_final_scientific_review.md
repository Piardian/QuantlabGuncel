## Evidence Consistency

The LIQ-001 evidence body is internally consistent.

The literature supports liquidity as a distinct construct. CD-001 selected a narrow but defensible daily price-impact proxy. IM-001 implemented it deterministically. CV-001 found coherent behavior but limited scope. MI-001 and HV-001 supported the interpretation of LIQ-001 as aggregate liquidity stress. PV-001 showed predictive information for future risk-state variables. EV-001 found partial economic utility in predefined risk workflows. CC-001 classified the construct accordingly.

No stage supports interpreting LIQ-001 as alpha. No stage supports using it as a complete liquidity model.

## Principal Limitations

- Empirical validation used a capped 59-symbol universe.
- Yahoo Finance OHLCV data is not institutional-grade liquidity data.
- The construct measures one liquidity dimension: daily price-impact illiquidity.
- It excludes spread, depth, immediacy and resiliency.
- Some conclusions depend on MR-001 definitions.
- No live validation exists.
- Economic validation was limited to predefined workflows.

## Final Scientific Assessment

LIQ-001 is a completed scientific construct within the Market Signal Discovery Program V3.0.

The accumulated evidence supports classifying LIQ-001 as a **Liquidity Stress / Risk Control Construct** with **Medium-High** scientific maturity.

The construct is most defensible as a risk-state input, especially for future volatility and liquidity-stress awareness.

The construct is not scientifically justified as an alpha factor, return predictor or execution-cost substitute.
