# Recommended Applications

## Supported by Evidence

### Daily Liquidity-Stress Monitoring

LIQ-001 is appropriate as a daily aggregate liquidity-stress monitor for US equities under the evaluated data and implementation assumptions.

### Risk-State Identification

LIQ-001 is appropriate as a risk-state input because it contains evidence of association and prediction for future realized volatility, absolute market movement and MR-001 STRESS occurrence.

### Regime-Aware Portfolio Risk Control

LIQ-001 is appropriate for predefined portfolio risk-control workflows. EV-001 found the strongest economic utility evidence in this use case.

## Partially Supported

### Risk Budgeting Support

LIQ-001 may support risk budgeting decisions, but evidence was partial because performance tradeoffs remained.

### Volatility Targeting Support

LIQ-001 may support volatility targeting as a risk-management input, but it should not be interpreted as a complete volatility model.

### Hedge Activation Support

LIQ-001 may support hedge activation logic, but EV-001 classified this use case as partial rather than fully supported.

## Use Boundary

These recommendations apply only within the evaluated V3.0 research scope. They do not imply production deployment, universal superiority or alpha generation.
