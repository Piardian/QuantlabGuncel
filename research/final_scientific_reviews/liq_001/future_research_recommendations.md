# Future Research Recommendations

## Supported by Evidence

### Larger Universe Replication

Repeat construct validation and predictive validation on a broader, fixed and preferably survivorship-aware US equity universe.

### Data-Provider Replication

Replicate LIQ-001 using an institutional or alternative data source to test whether results depend on Yahoo Finance OHLCV data.

### Cross-Construct Comparison

Compare LIQ-001 with other completed constructs only after those constructs complete the same scientific lifecycle.

## Partially Supported

### Liquidity Dimension Expansion

Future constructs may separately investigate spreads, depth, turnover, resiliency or intraday liquidity. These should be new frozen constructs rather than modifications to LIQ-001.

### Portfolio Workflow Refinement

Future EV studies may examine additional preregistered risk-control workflows, but not by modifying LIQ-001 retroactively.

## Not Recommended Without New Preregistration

- Turning LIQ-001 into an alpha signal.
- Using LIQ-001 as a direct entry or exit trigger.
- Changing CD-001 definitions inside the existing construct lifecycle.
- Optimizing liquidity thresholds after observing EV-001 results.

## Methodological Rule

Any material construct modification requires a new preregistered construct definition and a new lifecycle.
