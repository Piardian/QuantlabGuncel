# LIQ-001 Construct Maturity Report

## Primary Category

**Liquidity Stress / Risk Control Construct**

## Secondary Capabilities

- Future realized volatility risk indication
- Future absolute market movement indication
- Future MR-001 STRESS occurrence indication
- Partial future drawdown-risk indication
- Liquidity-aware risk-control support

## Scientific Maturity Level

**Medium-High**

## Evidence Strength

**Moderate to strong for risk-state information; partial for economic utility.**

## Maturity Assessment

### Supported by Evidence

- Literature recognition of liquidity as a distinct construct.
- Deterministic implementation of the frozen CD-001 specification.
- Predictive information for future volatility and MR-001 STRESS occurrence.
- Engineering reproducibility.

### Partially Supported

- Construct validity due capped 59-symbol empirical validation universe.
- Mechanism validity due daily proxy limitations and residual coverage concerns.
- Economic utility because some predefined use cases improved risk-adjusted outcomes while others involved tradeoffs.
- Drawdown-risk prediction due smaller effect size than volatility-related targets.

### Not Supported

- Alpha generation.
- Standalone directional return prediction.
- Exact execution-cost measurement.
- Intraday liquidity measurement.

## Overall Maturity

LIQ-001 has completed the full V3.0 scientific lifecycle and is mature enough to be treated as a completed validated risk-state construct within the evaluated assumptions.

Its maturity is not high enough to justify universal generalization, execution-cost substitution, or alpha-factor classification.
