# Known Limitations

## Data Scope

The empirical validation used a capped 59-symbol universe loaded from Yahoo Finance. This limits breadth, survivorship robustness and generalization.

## Data Source

Yahoo Finance daily OHLCV data is not an institutional-grade liquidity database. Corporate actions, volume quality and symbol history issues may affect results.

## Liquidity Dimension Coverage

LIQ-001 measures only a daily price-impact / illiquidity proxy. It does not measure quoted spreads, order-book depth, intraday immediacy, resiliency or venue-level liquidity.

## Coverage Artifact Risk

HV-001 found the coverage relationship small but not fully dismissible. This remains a partial limitation.

## MR-001 Dependency

Some predictive validation evidence references future MR-001 STRESS occurrence. Those conclusions depend on the MR-001 definition and validation status.

## Economic Validation Scope

EV-001 evaluated predefined risk-management workflows only. It did not evaluate all possible risk-control applications.

## No Alpha Claim

LIQ-001 was not validated as a standalone alpha signal or directional return predictor.

## No Live Validation

The research remains historical and simulated. No live operational validation has been completed.

## No Execution-Cost Replacement

The construct should not be used as a substitute for transaction-cost analysis, spread modeling or order-book liquidity measurement.
