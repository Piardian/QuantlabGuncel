# Future Research Recommendations

## Recommendation 1: Construct Dependency Analysis

Evaluate incremental information versus:

- VOL-001
- BRD-001
- LIQ-001
- MR-001

Purpose:

Determine whether COR-001 adds independent information beyond existing validated constructs.

## Recommendation 2: Alternative Correlation Constructs

Potential future constructs:

- implied correlation
- tail dependence
- PCA/eigenvalue concentration
- cross-asset correlation
- sector correlation

These should be treated as separate constructs, not modifications to COR-001.

## Recommendation 3: Operational Validation

Before any production use, evaluate:

- data latency
- monitoring requirements
- failure modes
- cost assumptions
- live or paper behavior
- governance process

## Recommendation 4: Economic Workflow Expansion

Future EV studies may evaluate additional preregistered workflows, but only after separate preregistration.

No workflow should be added after observing results.
