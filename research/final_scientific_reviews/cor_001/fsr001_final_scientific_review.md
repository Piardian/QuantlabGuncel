## Supported Scientific Claims

COR-001 is supported as a market-correlation construct measuring broad US equity market co-movement.

COR-001 is supported as a market-wide synchronization and common co-movement stress sensor.

COR-001 is supported as a risk-state predictive construct for future realized volatility, future breadth deterioration, and future correlation persistence.

COR-001 is supported as economically useful in predefined diversification-risk budgeting and correlation-aware volatility-targeting workflows.

## Partially Supported Claims

Future drawdown risk prediction is partially supported.

Correlation stress risk control is partially supported because full-sample results were favorable but cross-period robustness was weak.

Regime-aware portfolio de-risking support is partially supported because risk metrics improved materially but return penalty was material.

## Not Supported Claims

COR-001 is not supported as:

- a standalone alpha signal
- a return-enhancement construct
- a standalone directional return predictor
- a standalone buy/sell signal
- a universal market timing system
- a production-deployment-ready construct

## Inconclusive Claims

Incremental independence from VOL-001, BRD-001, LIQ-001, and MR-001 remains incompletely evaluated.

Production readiness remains inconclusive because operational review, cost modeling, monitoring, execution assumptions, governance, and live or paper validation were not evaluated.
