# Known Limitations

## Universe Limitation

COR-001 uses a fixed current US equity universe.

Historical survivorship bias may be present.

## Data Limitation

The construct relies on Yahoo Finance daily price data.

## Measurement Limitation

COR-001 measures linear average pairwise realized correlation.

It does not measure:

- implied correlation
- tail dependence
- nonlinear dependence
- cross-asset correlation
- sector-specific correlation

## Independence Limitation

Incremental independence from VOL-001, BRD-001, LIQ-001, and MR-001 remains incompletely evaluated.

## Economic Validation Limitation

EV-001 used simplified risk-management workflows and did not model full implementation frictions.

## Deployment Limitation

Production deployment was not evaluated.

Operational monitoring, governance, cost modeling, execution assumptions, and live or paper validation remain required.
