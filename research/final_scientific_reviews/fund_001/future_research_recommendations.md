# FUND-001 / FSR-001

# Future Research Recommendations

## Supported By Evidence

Future research may use FUND-001 as a context sensor in preregistered studies of market risk conditions.

## Partially Supported

Future work may compare FUND-001 against alternative funding constructs, such as repo stress, SOFR-based funding spreads, or broader funding composites. Each alternative should begin at CD-001 or RP-001 as a separate construct.

## Not Supported

Current evidence does not justify deploying FUND-001 as a production risk overlay or standalone trading signal.

## Required Before Production Consideration

- A new preregistered economic workflow with stronger theoretical justification.
- Independent out-of-sample economic validation.
- Transaction cost and implementation assumptions.
- Live or paper validation.
