## Integrated Assessment

OPT-001 completed the full Market Signal Discovery Program lifecycle.

The construct is scientifically defensible as a reproducible VIXCLS-based options-implied volatility state sensor. The evidence consistently supports interpreting it as an index option-implied market uncertainty / expected volatility risk sensor. CV-001, MI-001, and HV-001 align cleanly: high OPT-001 states correspond to turbulent market conditions, wider market ranges, deeper drawdown context, and recognizable stress periods.

PV-001 found meaningful predictive information for future realized volatility, future absolute movement, and future drawdown risk. Directional return evidence remained inconclusive and should not be interpreted as alpha.

EV-001 translated some of the risk information into measurable economic utility under predefined risk-management workflows, especially risk budgeting and drawdown control. Economic utility was partial rather than universal.

## Final Verdict

OPT-001 is a completed research-grade Options-Implied Volatility Risk Sensor.

It is scientifically justified for risk monitoring, volatility-risk awareness, and predefined risk-management workflow research. It is not scientifically justified as a standalone alpha factor, direct directional return predictor, or complete production trading system under the completed evidence.
