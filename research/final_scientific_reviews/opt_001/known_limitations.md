# OPT-001 / FSR-001

# Known Limitations

- OPT-001 uses VIXCLS only and does not reconstruct the option surface.
- VIXCLS cannot isolate pure expected volatility from volatility risk premium, hedging demand, fear, or risk aversion.
- SPY-based predictive and economic validation begins in 2008 due to available cached OHLCV data.
- EV-001 uses simplified exposure workflows and not live order execution.
- Dynamic hedge activation is represented as exposure reduction, not implemented derivatives hedging.
- Transaction costs are simplified.
- Directional return evidence is inconclusive.
- No paper-trading or live validation exists.
