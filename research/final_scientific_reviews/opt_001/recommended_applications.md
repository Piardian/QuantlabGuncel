# OPT-001 / FSR-001

# Recommended Applications

Supported or partially supported applications:

- Options-implied volatility state monitoring.
- Market uncertainty / fear-state monitoring.
- Future realized volatility-risk awareness.
- Future absolute market movement-risk awareness.
- Future drawdown-risk warning with caution.
- Volatility-aware risk budgeting research.
- Drawdown-control workflow research.
- Volatility-targeting support with caution.
- Hedge-activation research support with caution.

## Non-Recommended Applications

- Standalone alpha signal.
- Direct directional return predictor.
- Complete buy/sell trading system.
- Proof of universal economic superiority.
- Pure expected-volatility decomposition.
- Replacement for full option-surface analytics.
