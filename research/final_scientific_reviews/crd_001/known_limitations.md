# Known Limitations

## Short Historical Coverage

The available normalized CRD-001 sample is short and does not cover multiple major credit cycles such as 2008 or 2020.

## Component Ambiguity

High-yield OAS may reflect default expectations, liquidity stress, credit risk premium, and risk appetite. CRD-001 does not decompose these components.

## Mixed Predictive Evidence

PV-001 did not support future drawdown-risk prediction or the preregistered future spread-widening hypothesis.

## Incremental Independence Not Established

CRD-001 has not been tested for incremental independence versus MR-001, VOL-001, LIQ-001, BRD-001, or COR-001.

## No Production Readiness Review

Data licensing, monitoring, alerting, failure handling, operational controls, and live or paper validation were not evaluated.
