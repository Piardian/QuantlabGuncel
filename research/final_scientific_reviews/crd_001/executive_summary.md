## Final Classification

```text

```

## Scientific Maturity

```text
Moderate
```

## Supported

CRD-001 is supported as a deterministic high-yield credit spread stress sensor for research use.

## Partially Supported

CRD-001 has partially supported construct validity, mechanism validity, predictive validity, and risk-management economic utility.

## Not Supported

CRD-001 is not supported as an alpha signal, standalone return-enhancement construct, standalone future drawdown predictor, or future credit spread-widening predictor under the preregistered hypothesis.

## Main Limitation

The available normalized sample is short and does not include multiple major credit cycles.

## Final Note

CRD-001 is completed and eligible for comparison with future validated constructs.
