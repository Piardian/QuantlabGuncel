## Supported Scientific Claims

CRD-001 is supported as a deterministic implementation of a frozen high-yield option-adjusted spread construct.

CRD-001 is supported as a high-yield credit-spread stress sensor within the available sample.

CRD-001 is supported as representing a speculative-grade credit risk compensation state within the available sample.

CRD-001 is supported for research-use monitoring of high-yield credit stress.

## Partially Supported Claims

Construct validity is partially supported because schema fidelity and internal coherence were demonstrated, but historical coverage is limited.

Mechanism validity is partially supported because high and low states separate wider and tighter high-yield spread environments, but not across multiple credit cycles.

Predictive validity is partially supported. Future realized volatility was partially supported and future equity return association was supported within the available sample, but future drawdown risk and future spread widening were not supported.

Economic utility is partially supported in predefined risk-management workflows, especially credit-stress risk budgeting, volatility-aware de-risking support, and portfolio risk-control monitoring.

## Not Supported Claims

CRD-001 is not supported as:

- a standalone alpha signal
- a standalone return-enhancement construct
- a standalone buy/sell signal
- a universal market timing system
- a future drawdown-risk prediction construct under PV-001
- a future credit spread-widening prediction construct under PV-001
- a production-deployment-ready construct

## Inconclusive Claims

Incremental independence from MR-001, VOL-001, LIQ-001, BRD-001, and COR-001 remains incompletely evaluated.

Production readiness remains inconclusive because operational review, data licensing, monitoring, alerting, failure handling, and live or paper validation were not evaluated.

Long-cycle credit stress behavior remains incompletely evaluated because the available normalized sample is short.

## Final Assessment

CRD-001 is a completed construct within the Market Signal Discovery Program.

Credit Stress / Risk Management Sensor

The completed evidence does not support CRD-001 as:
