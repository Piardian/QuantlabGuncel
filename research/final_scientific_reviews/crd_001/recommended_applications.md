# Recommended Applications

CRD-001 is scientifically justified for:

- high-yield credit spread stress monitoring
- credit-market risk dashboards
- risk-management context
- credit-stress-aware risk budgeting support
- volatility-aware de-risking support
- drawdown-warning support with caution
- portfolio risk-control monitoring

## Application Boundary

These recommendations are limited to the evaluated evidence body. They do not imply production deployment readiness.
