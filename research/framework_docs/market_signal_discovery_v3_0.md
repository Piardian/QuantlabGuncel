# Market Signal Discovery Program v3.0

v3.0 is a new research program focused on discovering reproducible predictive market constructs.
It does not attempt to explain an existing production signal. It evaluates candidate constructs
independently under preregistered protocols.

## Scientific Goal

Identify observable market constructs that demonstrate reproducible predictive information under
rigorous out-of-sample evaluation.

## Core Principle

Every candidate construct must pass the same pipeline:

1. Research Prioritization
2. Literature Review
3. Construct Definition
4. Implementation
5. Construct Validation
6. Mechanism Identification
7. Hypothesis Validation
8. Predictive Validation
9. Economic Validation
10. Construct Classification
11. Final Scientific Review

No construct skips stages and no construct receives special treatment.

## Discovery Domains

Allowed domains include:

- Trend
- Momentum
- Volatility
- Market Regime
- Liquidity
- Relative Volume
- Cross-sectional Breadth
- Sector Leadership
- Market Internals
- Dispersion
- Mean Reversion
- Order Flow
- Market Structure
- Behavioral Effects

Additional domains may be added only before preregistration.

## Research Rules

- Every study is preregistered.
- No parameter optimization.
- No threshold tuning.
- No post-hoc rule modification.
- No p-hacking or data snooping.
- Negative findings are valid scientific outcomes.

## Output Standard

Each construct should produce:

- Research Prioritization
- Literature Review
- Construct Definition
- Implementation Report
- Validation Report
- Statistical Report
- Economic Validation
- Construct Classification
- Final Scientific Review
- Limitations
- Executive Summary

Every conclusion must be labeled as:

- Supported by evidence
- Not supported by evidence
- Inconclusive
- Speculation

## Interpretation

v3.0 is not about maximizing historical backtest performance.
It is about building a scientifically validated library of market constructs with independently
verified predictive properties.
