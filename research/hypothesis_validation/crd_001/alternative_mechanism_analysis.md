# Alternative Mechanism Analysis

## Scope

HV-001 does not introduce new constructs or external variables. Alternative mechanism review is therefore conceptual and limited to LR-001 mechanisms.

## Alternatives

- Default-risk compensation
- Credit-risk premium
- Credit-market liquidity stress
- Broad risk appetite

## Assessment

Inconclusive. CRD-001 uses total high-yield OAS and cannot decompose these components. The evidence supports a high-yield spread-stress mechanism but does not distinguish which spread component dominates.
