# Executive Summary

## CRD-001 / HV-001 Result

Partially supported.

## Main Finding

The evidence supports strong internal separation between high and low CRD-001 credit-spread states within the available sample.

## Key Results

- H1 high-minus-low HY OAS difference: 0.8705
- H1 Cohen's d: 3.7827
- H2 high-minus-low 20d spread change: 0.5026
- H2 Cohen's d: 1.5654

## Main Limitation

The validation sample is short and the state groups are construct-internal. Therefore the mechanism is not classified as fully supported across credit cycles.

## Boundary

No predictive, trading, alpha, economic, or production-deployment conclusion is made.
