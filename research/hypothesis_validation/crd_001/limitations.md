# HV-001 Limitations

## Short Historical Coverage

The available CRD-001 sample covers only 2023-2026. This prevents strong validation across multiple credit cycles.

## Construct-Internal State Definitions

High and low states are defined using CRD-001's own percentile. This is appropriate for explanatory validation but limits external mechanism inference.

## Component Ambiguity

High-yield OAS combines default expectations, liquidity stress, risk premium, and risk appetite. HV-001 does not decompose these components.

## No Predictive or Economic Test

HV-001 does not test forecasting ability, trading performance, alpha generation, economic value, or production deployment.
