# Effect Size Analysis

## Summary

- H1 Cohen's d: 3.7827
- H2 Cohen's d: 1.5654
- H3 Cohen's d: 3.1723
- H4 Cohen's d: -1.1667

## Assessment

Supported by evidence: H1, H3, and H4 show large mechanical separation between CRD-001 state groups.

Partially supported: H2 indicates spread-widening behavior is directionally consistent, but the available sample and short high-state history limit stronger interpretation.

## Boundary

Effect sizes describe explanatory separation only. They do not measure prediction or economic value.
