# Executive Summary

## Main Result

```text

```

## Supported Mechanism

BRD-001 represents:

```text
Long-term cross-sectional trend participation and internal market confirmation.
```

LOW_BREADTH states are empirically associated with:

- weaker same-day SPY trend condition
- higher same-day/trailing realized volatility
- deeper same-day drawdown from 52-week high
- lower SPY-above-SMA200 frequency

HIGH_BREADTH states are empirically associated with:

- broad participation
- stronger same-day SPY trend condition
- lower realized volatility
- shallower drawdown

## Key Evidence

- H1-H5 were all classified as Supported by evidence.
- Bootstrap confidence intervals excluded zero in the expected direction.
- Fixed-seed permutation p-values were approximately 0.0002.
- Directional evidence persisted across four broad historical blocks.

## Important Limitation

This is explanatory validation only.

No predictive, economic, alpha, profitability, trading-performance, or production-deployment claim is made.
