# OPT-001 / HV-001

# Executive Summary

OPT-001 mechanism hypotheses were formally evaluated.

Overall conclusion:

```text
Supported by evidence
```

Supported findings:

- High OPT states are associated with higher trailing realized market volatility.
- High OPT states are associated with larger same-day absolute market movement.
- High OPT states are associated with wider same-day market range.
- High OPT states are associated with deeper market drawdown context.
- Low OPT states are associated with calmer realized market conditions.
- VIXCLS alone cannot be interpreted as a pure expected-volatility-only measure.

This stage makes no forecasting, alpha, trading, or economic-value claims.
