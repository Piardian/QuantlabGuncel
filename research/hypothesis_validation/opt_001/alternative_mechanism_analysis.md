# OPT-001 / HV-001

# Alternative Mechanism Analysis

## Evaluated Alternative

A narrow interpretation that OPT-001 measures pure expected volatility alone is not supported by the construct specification.

## Evidence Classification

Supported by evidence:

VIXCLS is an option-implied volatility index. It is influenced by expected future variance pricing, option demand, volatility risk premium, fear/risk aversion, convexity demand, and market stress pricing. Because OPT-001 only uses VIXCLS daily close, it cannot decompose these components.

## Conclusion

OPT-001 should be interpreted as an index option-implied uncertainty / expected volatility state, not as a purified expected-volatility-only measurement.

This is a construct interpretation statement, not a predictive or economic claim.
