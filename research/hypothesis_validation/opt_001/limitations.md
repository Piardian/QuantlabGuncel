# OPT-001 / HV-001

# Limitations

- HV-001 evaluates explanatory validity only; it does not test forecasting ability.
- The SPY OHLCV profile sample begins in 2008, while VIXCLS begins in 1990.
- State buckets are descriptive groups based on trailing OPT-001 percentile; they are not trading rules.
- VIXCLS cannot isolate volatility risk premium, risk aversion, hedging demand, or pure expected volatility without additional option and realized-volatility data.
- Year-level comparisons can be sparse because high and low option-implied volatility regimes cluster over time.
- No prediction, alpha, trading performance, economic utility, or production deployment claim is made.
