# Executive Summary

HV-001 tested whether the mechanism hypotheses from MI-001 are empirically supported.

## Result

The evidence supports the interpretation that:

- **EXPANSION** is a low-volatility, lower-drawdown market state.
- **STRESS** is a high-volatility, deeper-drawdown market state.
- The two states are statistically distinct and persist across historical periods.

## Final Classification

**Supported by evidence**

## Important Boundary

This conclusion is explanatory only. It does not imply predictive value, trading edge, alpha, or economic superiority.
