# HV-001: MR-001 Hypothesis Validation

## Objective

Evaluate whether the mechanism hypotheses generated in MI-001 are consistently supported by empirical evidence.

## Preregistered Hypotheses

- **H1:** STRESS is associated with significantly higher realized volatility than EXPANSION.
- **H2:** STRESS is associated with deeper and more persistent drawdowns.
- **H3:** STRESS represents a statistically distinct high-risk market environment.
- **H4:** EXPANSION represents a statistically distinct low-risk market environment.

## Final Classification

- **H1:** Supported by evidence
- **H2:** Supported by evidence
- **H3:** Supported by evidence
- **H4:** Supported by evidence

## Summary

The empirical evidence is strongly consistent with MI-001. STRESS is materially more volatile, experiences deeper drawdowns, and forms a distinct high-risk state. EXPANSION is the corresponding lower-risk state. These results remain stable across the historical sample and are consistent with the theoretical regime-switching framework documented in LR-001.

The study remains explanatory only. It does not address predictive validity, trading performance, alpha, or economic value.
