# Cross-Period Validation

## Observed Pattern by Historical Period

The state mechanism is consistent across major historical regimes:

- 2008: STRESS dominates
- 2009: STRESS still dominant but easing
- 2010-2011: mixed transition period
- 2012-2014: EXPANSION dominates strongly
- 2020: STRESS reappears strongly
- 2022: STRESS dominates strongly
- 2023-2025: EXPANSION dominates again

## Interpretation

The same two-state mechanism persists across time, but the balance between states shifts in line with major market stress episodes. This is the expected behavior of a regime model rather than a static label scheme.
