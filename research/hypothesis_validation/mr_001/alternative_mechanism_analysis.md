# Alternative Mechanism Analysis

## Alternative Explanations Considered

### 1. Simple bull/bear labeling

The state sequence is not identical to a simple bull/bear flag because:

- STRESS can occur inside broader non-crash periods
- EXPANSION can persist through some drawdown periods
- the hidden state is inferred from return-volatility structure rather than a single price threshold

### 2. Pure return-sign explanation

Unsupported as a full explanation because both states contain mixed daily return signs. The separation is strongest in volatility and drawdown, not in daily sign alone.

### 3. Pure trend explanation

Not sufficient on its own. The mechanism is better described as a market risk-state separation than a simple trend filter.

## Conclusion

The best-supported explanation remains the regime-switching risk-state interpretation from MI-001.
