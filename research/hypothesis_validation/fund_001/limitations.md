# FUND-001 / HV-001

# Limitations

- High-state definitions are descriptive state comparisons, not optimized thresholds.
- H1 is partly mechanical because FUND-001 is itself a spread construct.
- CP and T-bill component decomposition uses trailing means, not a structural model.
- Funding liquidity, credit risk, counterparty risk, safe-asset demand, and policy context cannot be fully separated with only DCPF3M and DTB3.
- No predictive validity is evaluated.
- No economic value is evaluated.
- No trading, alpha, or production recommendation is made.
