# Effect Size Analysis

## Purpose

Summarize the magnitude of high-versus-low COR-001 state differences.

No predictive or economic interpretation is made.

## Effect Sizes

| Hypothesis | Variable | Difference: High - Low | Cohen's d | Classification |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| H1 | SPY 20d realized volatility | 0.1539 | 1.5476 | Supported by evidence |
| H2 | SPY 52w drawdown | -0.0808 | -1.6699 | Supported by evidence |
| H3 | BRD-001 breadth | -0.2815 | -1.7485 | Supported by evidence |
| H4 | COR-001 raw correlation | 0.2399 | 2.6336 | Supported by evidence |

## Interpretation

Supported by evidence:

The observed differences are large in standardized effect-size terms.

Partially supported:

H4 is construct-internal, so it should be interpreted as state-separation evidence rather than independent mechanism evidence.

Not evaluated:

Whether these effect sizes imply predictive information or economic utility.
