# Cross-Period Validation

## Purpose

Evaluate whether the COR-001 mechanism differences remain directionally consistent across broad historical blocks.

## Periods

The study used four fixed blocks:

- 2011-2014
- 2015-2018
- 2019-2022
- 2023-2025

## Result

All four preregistered hypotheses preserved the expected direction in every evaluated period.

## Direction Consistency

| Hypothesis | Direction Consistency |
| --- | ---: |
| H1 | 4 / 4 |
| H2 | 4 / 4 |
| H3 | 4 / 4 |
| H4 | 4 / 4 |

## Interpretation

Supported by evidence:

The COR-001 explanatory mechanism is directionally stable across the evaluated historical blocks.

Limitation:

The 2023-2025 block has fewer high-correlation observations than earlier blocks.
