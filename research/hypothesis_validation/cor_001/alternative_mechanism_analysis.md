# Alternative Mechanism Analysis

## Purpose

Assess whether alternative interpretations better explain COR-001 behavior at HV-001.

## Alternative 1: COR-001 Is Only Volatility

Status:

Partially supported, but insufficient as a complete explanation.

Evidence:

COR-001 has a strong contemporaneous association with SPY 20-day realized volatility, and high COR-001 states have substantially higher realized volatility.

Limitation:

COR-001 is constructed from cross-sectional pairwise co-movement, not univariate SPY volatility. It also aligns with breadth deterioration and drawdown state.

## Alternative 2: COR-001 Is Only Breadth In Reverse

Status:

Partially supported, but insufficient as a complete explanation.

Evidence:

High COR-001 states correspond to materially lower BRD-001 breadth.

Limitation:

BRD-001 measures long-term trend participation. COR-001 measures cross-sectional co-movement of returns. The constructs are related but not identical.

## Alternative 3: COR-001 Is Only Market Drawdown

Status:

Partially supported, but insufficient as a complete explanation.

Evidence:

High COR-001 states correspond to deeper contemporaneous drawdowns.

Limitation:

Drawdown is a level/path condition of SPY. COR-001 is a cross-sectional dependence measure.

## HV-001 Interpretation

Supported by evidence:

COR-001 overlaps with stress-like market conditions.

Inconclusive:

Whether COR-001 contains independent information beyond VOL-001, BRD-001, LIQ-001, or MR-001. That question is outside HV-001 and would require a separate preregistered construct-dependency study.
