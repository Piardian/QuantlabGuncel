# RC-E1 Research Limitations

- No completed study establishes causality or edge creation.
- RC-A and RC-B test only the specified components under the present production architecture; they do not cover all possible strategy mechanisms.
- RC-C associations are restricted to executed production-selected trades and cannot estimate independent component contribution.
- RC-D is a disjoint OOS comparison but has only one partial calendar year and no evaluable symbol-level repetition under the existing minimum sample rule.
- Current S&P 500 constituents create survivorship bias risk.
- Independent single-symbol runs do not evaluate capital competition, signal ranking, portfolio breadth, correlated exposure, or shared drawdown.
- Yahoo Finance history and modeled fills/costs may differ from investable execution conditions.
- The research program has no paper-trading or live-validation evidence.
- Multiple completed analyses require conservative interpretation of weak or isolated findings.
