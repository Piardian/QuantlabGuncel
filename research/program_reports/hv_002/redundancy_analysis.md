# Redundancy Analysis

## Supported by evidence
- Composite Trend and Trend Strength are highly redundant descriptively.
- The overlap gap between the two leading mechanisms is small.
- Both families remain stable across years and both preserve positive agreement throughout the yearly slices evaluated in TCM-001.

## Not supported by evidence
- That Composite Trend is uniquely identified as the mechanism.
- That Trend Strength is uniquely identified as the mechanism.

## Interpretation
The evidence suggests a shared trend-like mechanism rather than a clean separation between Composite Trend and Trend Strength. The data are strong enough to rule out the lower-ranked trend families, but not strong enough to separate the top two candidates decisively.
