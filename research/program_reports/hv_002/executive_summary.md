# HV-002 Executive Summary

HV-002 asked whether the evidence can distinguish between Composite Trend and Trend Strength as competing explanations for the observable behavior of the Production Relative Strength Gate.

## Result
**Current evidence cannot distinguish between the two mechanisms.**

## Why
- Composite Trend is slightly stronger on overlap-based measures.
- Trend Strength is slightly stronger on rank-agreement behavior.
- Both are highly stable across years.
- The gap between them is too small and too metric-dependent to justify a definitive mechanism winner.

## What is supported
- A trend-based explanation is supported.
- The top two trend mechanisms are both plausible.

## What is not supported
- A unique mechanism identification.
- Any causal, predictive, or economic claim.
