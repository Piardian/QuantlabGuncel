# Recommended Applications

## Scientifically justified use cases

- Risk budgeting
- Volatility targeting
- Dynamic hedge activation
- Regime-aware portfolio risk control
- Broad market risk-state monitoring
- Downside-risk awareness

## Why these are appropriate

MR-001 is supported as a risk forecasting construct and as a predictor of future realized volatility.
That makes it suitable for decision support in risk-centric workflows.

## Notes

These applications are supported as utility-oriented use cases only.
They are not endorsements of alpha generation or directional market timing.
