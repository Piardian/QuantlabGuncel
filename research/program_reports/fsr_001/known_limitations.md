# Known Limitations

- The construct is evaluated on historical data only.
- Surviving evidence does not prove causality.
- Return prediction is not supported.
- Economic utility was evaluated inside fixed workflows only.
- No paper-trading validation has been completed.
- No live-execution validation has been completed.
- The historical universe may still contain survivorship bias.
- Execution, slippage, and financing assumptions remain modeled rather than fully observed in production.
- Temporal behavior changes across market periods, which is expected for a regime construct but limits blanket stability claims.
