# Evidence Summary

MR-001 progressed through a full preregistered research lifecycle.

## What is supported

- The literature supports market regimes as persistent latent states.
- The construct was well-defined, deterministically implemented, and reproducible.
- The latent states are coherent and persistent.
- The states are meaningfully interpretable as low-risk expansion versus high-risk stress.
- The mechanism hypotheses were supported.
- The construct predicts future realized volatility and some drawdown-risk behavior.
- The construct has measurable utility in fixed risk-management workflows.

## What is not supported

- Robust future return prediction.
- Any claim that MR-001 is an alpha signal.
- Any claim of universal superiority across all investment tasks.

## What remains partial

- Drawdown predictability.
- Temporal stability in the sense of unchanged state mix across all periods.

## Overall reading

MR-001 is best understood as a validated risk-regime / volatility-forecasting construct rather than a directional return-prediction construct.
