# Supported Conclusions

## SUPPORTED BY MULTIPLE STUDIES
- The production Relative Strength gate is conceptually different from canonical cross-sectional momentum as commonly defined in the literature.
- The production gate does not show strong descriptive overlap with the preregistered canonical 12-1 top-decile comparator.

## SUPPORTED BY EVIDENCE
- Among the predefined construct families tested in CI-001, the Trend family produced the highest descriptive agreement with the production gate.
- The next strongest descriptive matches in CI-001 were Benchmark-Relative Persistence and Breakout, both below Trend.

## SUPPORTED BY EVIDENCE
- The accumulated v2.0 evidence justifies treating the current production Relative Strength gate as an observable construct that still needs formal definition, rather than as a settled canonical momentum proxy.
