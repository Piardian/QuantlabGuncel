# Knowledge Gaps

## PLAUSIBLE BUT UNTESTED
- Whether the production Relative Strength gate maps more cleanly to a broader family of trend-following definitions than to a narrower canonical trend definition.
- Whether the gate is best understood as trend, persistence, or a hybrid construct.
- Whether the same descriptive pattern holds under alternative universes or alternative benchmark conventions.

## NOT YET ADDRESSED
- Predictive validation of any construct interpretation.
- Economic validation of any construct interpretation.
- Live or paper-trading reproducibility.
- Whether the observed construct relationship is stable in future unseen data.

## IMPORTANT LIMITATION
- The v2.0 studies were intentionally descriptive and comparator-limited. They answer “what does it resemble?” more reliably than “what does it cause?” or “what does it earn?”
