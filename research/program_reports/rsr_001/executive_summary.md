# RSR-001 Executive Summary

## Supported by multiple studies
- The production Relative Strength gate is not canonical cross-sectional momentum.
- The gate is descriptively closer to trend-like behavior than to the canonical momentum comparator tested in CV-001.

## Supported by evidence
- CI-001 ranked the Trend family highest by mean Jaccard similarity.
- Benchmark-Relative Persistence and Breakout were closer than Momentum but still below Trend.

## Remaining uncertainty
- The latent construct is still not fully identified.
- Predictive, economic, and live-trading implications remain unknown.

## Most justified next step
The next scientifically justified study is a targeted construct-mapping extension inside the Trend family, with preregistered comparators and no performance analysis.
