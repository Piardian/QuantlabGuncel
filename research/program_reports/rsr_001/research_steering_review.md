# RSR-001 Research Steering Review

## Purpose
This review synthesizes the completed v2.0 evidence without running new experiments. The objective is to identify the most scientifically justified next step.

## Evidence Base
- **LR-001** reviewed academic and practitioner literature on momentum and relative strength.
- **CV-001** compared the production Relative Strength gate against a preregistered canonical 12-1 cross-sectional momentum comparator.
- **CI-001** compared the production Relative Strength gate against multiple predefined construct families.

## What the evidence supports
- The production Relative Strength gate is **not the same construct** as canonical 12-1 cross-sectional momentum as operationalized in CV-001.
- The production Relative Strength gate is **more similar to trend-like descriptive behavior** than to the tested canonical momentum comparator.
- The accumulated evidence supports a move away from “does this filter work?” and toward “what construct does this filter actually represent?”

## What remains unknown
- The latent construct identified in CI-001 is still only descriptive and comparator-dependent.
- The evidence does not establish predictive power, trading edge, or economic value.
- The evidence does not yet identify whether the production rule is best described by a single construct or by a hybrid of several constructs.

## Most justified next step
The single most scientifically justified next step is a **targeted construct mapping study** focused on the trend-like construct family that emerged as the closest descriptive match in CI-001.

This recommendation follows from the accumulated evidence only. It does not imply economic superiority or production suitability.
