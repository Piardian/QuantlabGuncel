# Recommended Next Study

## Recommendation
The single most scientifically justified next step is:

**A focused construct-mapping study within the Trend family.**

## Why this is the best next step
- **SUPPORTED BY MULTIPLE STUDIES:** LR-001 and CV-001 already rule out treating the production gate as canonical 12-1 cross-sectional momentum.
- **SUPPORTED BY EVIDENCE:** CI-001 found the strongest descriptive agreement in the Trend family.
- **PLAUSIBLE BUT UNTESTED:** The current gate may be a trend-like construct with benchmark-relative persistence elements.

## What the next study should answer
- Whether the production gate is closer to trend distance, trend slope, persistence, or a blended trend descriptor.
- Which specific trend-like comparator family most cleanly reproduces the production gate’s observable selection behavior.

## What the next study should not do
- It should not evaluate returns, alpha, Sharpe, drawdown, or profitability.
- It should not recommend production changes.
- It should not infer predictive power or economic value.
