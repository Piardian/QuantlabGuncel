# CV-001 Construct Validation Summary

## Overall Assessment

MR-001 is a scientifically plausible two-state market regime implementation. The outputs are internally coherent, deterministic, and interpretable.

## Key Evidence

- Transition matrix shows strong persistence in both states.
- Posterior probabilities are sharply separated for most observations.
- State occupancy varies by historical period in a way that is consistent with major regime shifts.
- The model is reproducible and faithfully implements the frozen CD-001 construct.

## Classification

**Partially supported**

The construct is valid as a latent regime implementation, but stability is not perfect across all historical periods and the construct should still be treated as a validated regime representation rather than a universal market taxonomy.
