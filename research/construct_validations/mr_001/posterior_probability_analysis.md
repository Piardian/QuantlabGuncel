# Posterior Probability Analysis

## Summary Statistics

- Mean posterior max probability: 0.984134
- Median posterior max probability: 0.999942
- Share of observations with max posterior > 0.90: 95.01%
- Share of observations with max posterior > 0.99: 90.46%

## Interpretation

The posterior distribution is highly concentrated near 0 or 1 for most observations, indicating sharp state assignment and strong latent-state separation. A smaller set of observations lies near the transition boundary, which is expected in a regime-switching model.

## Conclusion

The posterior probabilities are interpretable and consistent with a two-state latent regime process.
