# Cross-Period Consistency

## Evidence

- The construct yields two coherent latent states throughout the sample.
- Expansion dominates most non-crisis years.
- Stress becomes dominant in crisis-heavy periods.
- The state sequence remains interpretable in both early and late sample segments.

## Limitations

- State occupancy is not perfectly uniform across years.
- Transition timing can shift near market turning points.
- The construct is intentionally narrow and market-proxy based.

## Conclusion

The empirical behavior is broadly consistent with the theoretical expectations from LR-001 for a two-state market regime process.
