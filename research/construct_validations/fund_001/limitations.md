# Limitations

- FUND-001 is not a pure funding-liquidity measure.
- The spread may include credit risk, counterparty risk, liquidity risk, safe-asset demand, and monetary policy effects.
- DCPF3M history starts much later than DTB3, causing many pre-1997 missing-input rows.
- Exact-date merging avoids look-ahead and forward-fill assumptions, but reduces coverage.
- CV-001 does not evaluate predictive validity.
- CV-001 does not evaluate economic utility.
- CV-001 does not determine whether FUND-001 should influence any portfolio decision.
