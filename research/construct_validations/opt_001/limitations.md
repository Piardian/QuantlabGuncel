# OPT-001 / CV-001

# Limitations

- OPT-001 measures the VIXCLS options-implied volatility level only.
- It does not reconstruct the SPX option strip or Cboe calculation process.
- It does not include VIX futures, term structure, variance risk premium, skew, put-call ratios, option volume, or dealer positioning.
- It does not separate expected volatility from volatility risk premium.
- It is an index-level construct, not a single-stock options construct.
- FRED or source revisions can affect future reruns unless input snapshots are archived.
- This study does not evaluate prediction, alpha, trading returns, economic value, or production suitability.
