# CV-001 Limitations

## Limited Historical Coverage

The current validation input covers 2023-07-31 through 2026-07-27. This limits validation across historical credit cycles.

## Source Dependency

The construct depends on the FRED-accessible ICE BofA high-yield OAS series. Source revisions may alter future reruns unless input snapshots are archived.

## Component Ambiguity

CRD-001 measures total high-yield OAS. It does not decompose default risk, liquidity premium, or excess credit premium.

## No Predictive or Economic Test

CV-001 does not test forecasting ability, trading performance, alpha generation, economic value, or production deployment.
