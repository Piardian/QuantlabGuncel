# OPT-001 / MI-001

# Mechanism Hypotheses For HV-001

## H1

HIGH_OPT_IMPLIED_VOL states are associated with materially higher contemporaneous and trailing realized market volatility than LOW_OPT_IMPLIED_VOL states.

Classification: Supported for formal HV-001 testing.

## H2

HIGH_OPT_IMPLIED_VOL states are associated with deeper contemporaneous market drawdown context than LOW_OPT_IMPLIED_VOL states.

Classification: Supported for formal HV-001 testing.

## H3

LOW_OPT_IMPLIED_VOL states are associated with calmer realized market conditions and lower recent turbulence.

Classification: Supported for formal HV-001 testing.

## H4

OPT-001 represents a pure expected-volatility mechanism separated from fear, risk aversion, volatility risk premium, or hedging demand.

Classification: Not supported by MI-001 alone. VIXCLS combines multiple option-pricing influences, so this narrower decomposition requires data not included in OPT-001.

## Boundary

These hypotheses concern explanatory validity only. They do not claim forecasting ability, trading edge, alpha, or economic value.
