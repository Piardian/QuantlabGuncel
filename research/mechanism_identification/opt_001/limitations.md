# OPT-001 / MI-001

# Limitations

- MI-001 is descriptive and contemporaneous; it does not evaluate forecasting ability.
- OPT-001 uses VIXCLS and does not decompose expected volatility from volatility risk premium, hedging demand, convexity demand, or risk aversion.
- SPY market profiling begins in 2008 in the available cached OHLCV file, while VIXCLS begins in 1990.
- State buckets use OPT-001 trailing percentile thresholds for descriptive profiling only; they are not optimized thresholds or trading rules.
- The mechanism label is an empirical interpretation of observed state profiles, not a causal claim.
- No prediction, alpha, trading performance, economic utility, or production suitability is evaluated.
