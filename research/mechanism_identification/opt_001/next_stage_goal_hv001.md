## BACKGROUND

OPT-001 has completed:

- RP-001
- LR-001
- CD-001
- IM-001
- CV-001
- MI-001

MI-001 identified the primary explanatory mechanism:

```text
Index option-implied uncertainty / expected volatility state
```

High OPT-001 states are descriptively associated with higher realized turbulence and deeper drawdown context.

--------------------------------------------------

## PURPOSE

Evaluate whether the mechanism hypotheses generated in MI-001 are consistently supported by empirical evidence.

This study evaluates explanatory validity only.

No predictive or economic conclusions are permitted.

--------------------------------------------------

## PRIMARY HYPOTHESES

H1

HIGH_OPT_IMPLIED_VOL states are associated with materially higher contemporaneous and trailing realized market volatility than LOW_OPT_IMPLIED_VOL states.

H2

HIGH_OPT_IMPLIED_VOL states are associated with deeper contemporaneous market drawdown context than LOW_OPT_IMPLIED_VOL states.

H3

LOW_OPT_IMPLIED_VOL states are associated with calmer realized market conditions and lower recent turbulence.

H4

OPT-001 cannot be interpreted as a pure expected-volatility mechanism separated from volatility risk premium, fear, risk aversion, or hedging demand using VIXCLS alone.
