# Executive Summary

MI-001 identifies the observable mechanism represented by MR-001.

## Main Result

The two latent states correspond to a **low-volatility expansion regime** and a **high-volatility stress regime**.

## What Differentiates the States

- EXPANSION has lower realized volatility, shallower drawdowns, and slightly positive mean return.
- STRESS has much higher realized volatility, deeper drawdowns, and a more adverse return environment.

## Transition Behavior

The regime is persistent and changes mainly around major market stress episodes such as 2008, 2020, and 2022.

## Conclusion

**Supported by evidence**

The MR-001 latent states are best understood as broad market risk states, consistent with the theoretical expectations documented in LR-001.
