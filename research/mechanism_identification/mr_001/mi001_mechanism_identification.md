# MI-001: MR-001 Mechanism Identification

## Objective

Identify the observable market mechanisms associated with the latent states generated by MR-001.

## Main Finding

MR-001 separates the SPY sample into two persistent latent states that map cleanly onto a **low-volatility expansion regime** and a **high-volatility stress regime**.

## State Interpretation

### EXPANSION

Supported by evidence:

- Lower realized volatility than STRESS
- More positive average daily return
- Much smaller average drawdown
- Strong posterior certainty

Interpretation:

This state is consistent with a calmer, upward-biased market environment in which market risk is lower and trend continuity is more common.

### STRESS

Supported by evidence:

- Much higher realized volatility
- More negative average daily return
- Deeper drawdowns
- Strong posterior certainty

Interpretation:

This state is consistent with a shock-prone or turbulence regime in which market risk is elevated and price paths are more unstable.

## Mechanism Summary

The latent states appear to represent a **market risk state mechanism** rather than a complex macro taxonomy:

- EXPANSION captures lower-risk, lower-volatility market conditions.
- STRESS captures higher-risk, high-volatility market conditions.

This is consistent with the dominant literature framing from LR-001, where regime models often separate persistent low-risk and high-risk market states.

## Conclusion

**Supported by evidence**

The empirical behavior of MR-001 is consistent with a two-state market regime mechanism driven primarily by realized risk and return environment.
