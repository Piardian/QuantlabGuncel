# Limitations

## Direct Limitations

- MR-001 is intentionally narrow: only SPY return and 20-day realized volatility are used.
- The construct distinguishes only two latent states.
- The analysis is descriptive and explanatory, not predictive.
- Some transitions cannot be tied to a single discrete event with certainty.

## Interpretation Limits

- EXPANSION and STRESS should not be over-interpreted as a complete macro taxonomy.
- The mechanism is best read as a broad risk-state separation.
- The evidence supports state characterisation, not forecasting power.
