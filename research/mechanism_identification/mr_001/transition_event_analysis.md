# Transition Event Analysis

## Observed Transition Behavior

The fitted MR-001 process is highly persistent:

- EXPANSION -> EXPANSION: 0.9593
- EXPANSION -> STRESS: 0.0407
- STRESS -> EXPANSION: 0.0150
- STRESS -> STRESS: 0.9850

## Historical Alignment

Observed state changes cluster around recognizable stress and recovery periods:

- 2008 financial crisis period
- 2010 flash-crash / sovereign stress period
- 2011 risk-off stress
- 2020 pandemic shock
- 2022 inflation / rate-hike stress regime

The transitions are not random day-to-day flips; they align with broader market shocks and recoveries.

## Interpretation

### Supported by evidence

- Regime shifts are sparse relative to total sample length.
- Transitions are more likely during historically stressful market episodes.

### Partially supported

- A specific causal event cannot be assigned to every transition day from the latent-state output alone.

### Inconclusive

- Whether every transition reflects a new macro regime versus a local volatility shock.

## Conclusion

The transition behavior is consistent with a persistent latent regime process that switches primarily during market stress episodes.
