# Market Condition Summary

## Consistent Market Mechanism

The MR-001 latent states behave like broad market-risk conditions:

- **EXPANSION:** lower risk, lower volatility, shallower drawdowns, more positive drift
- **STRESS:** higher risk, higher volatility, deeper drawdowns, less favorable drift

## Relation to LR-001

This is consistent with LR-001’s literature summary:

- market regimes are often expressed as persistent states
- returns and volatility are the most common observable inputs
- latent regime-switching models are the dominant framework

## What the States Do Not Appear to Be

Supported by evidence:

- The states are not arbitrary labels.
- The states are not unstable day-to-day noise.

Not supported by evidence:

- The states are not obviously a rich macro taxonomy in this implementation.

## Overall Assessment

MR-001 appears to capture a compact low-risk/high-risk market condition mechanism rather than a broader multi-factor regime decomposition.
