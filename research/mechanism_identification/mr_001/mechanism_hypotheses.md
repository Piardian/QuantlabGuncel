# Mechanism Hypotheses

## Supported by Evidence

1. **Volatility-state hypothesis**  
   MR-001 states primarily represent low-volatility versus high-volatility market conditions.

2. **Drawdown-state hypothesis**  
   STRESS captures periods of materially deeper drawdowns and more severe market turbulence.

3. **Risk-environment hypothesis**  
   The latent states track broad market risk conditions more than fine-grained thematic macro narratives.

## Partially Supported

1. **Bull / bear interpretation**  
   EXPANSION resembles bull-like behavior and STRESS resembles bear-like behavior, but the mapping is not exact enough to claim a strict bull/bear taxonomy.

2. **Return-sign hypothesis**  
   EXPANSION has positive mean return and STRESS has negative mean return, but both states contain mixed daily return signs.

## Not Supported

1. **Pure trend-only mechanism**  
   The evidence does not show that trend alone explains the states.

2. **Pure macro-event mechanism**  
   The model is market-data driven and does not directly estimate macro variables.

## Inconclusive

1. Whether the states should be interpreted as a complete market taxonomy.
2. Whether a richer observable set would split the same states into sub-regimes.
