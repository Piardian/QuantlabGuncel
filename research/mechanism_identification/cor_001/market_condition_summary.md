# Market Condition Summary

## Purpose

Summarize the observable market conditions that distinguish high and low COR-001 states.

## High Minus Low Differences

| Metric | High Minus Low |
| --- | ---: |
| Mean COR-001 raw correlation | 0.2399 |
| Mean SPY absolute return | 0.0075 |
| Mean SPY 20-day realized volatility | 0.1539 |
| Mean SPY 52-week drawdown | -0.0808 |
| Mean BRD-001 breadth | -0.2815 |

## Supported Interpretation

High-correlation states are not merely higher values of the COR-001 construct. They are contemporaneously associated with:

- higher daily absolute market movement
- higher realized volatility
- deeper drawdown from 52-week high
- lower market breadth

This supports the interpretation that high COR-001 states represent market-wide synchronization and common co-movement stress.

## Not Supported By MI-001

MI-001 does not show that high correlation predicts future volatility.

MI-001 does not show that high correlation predicts future returns.

MI-001 does not show economic value.
