# Mechanism Hypotheses

## Purpose

Translate MI-001 descriptive findings into preregistered hypotheses for HV-001.

These hypotheses are not validated in MI-001.

## Primary Mechanism

Supported by evidence:

COR-001 appears to represent market-wide synchronization / common co-movement stress.

## Hypothesis H1

High COR-001 states are associated with significantly higher contemporaneous realized market volatility than low COR-001 states.

Evidence status in MI-001:

Supported descriptively.

Requires HV-001 formal validation.

## Hypothesis H2

High COR-001 states are associated with deeper contemporaneous market drawdowns than low COR-001 states.

Evidence status in MI-001:

Supported descriptively.

Requires HV-001 formal validation.

## Hypothesis H3

High COR-001 states are associated with weaker contemporaneous market breadth than low COR-001 states.

Evidence status in MI-001:

Supported descriptively.

Requires HV-001 formal validation.

## Hypothesis H4

High COR-001 states represent a statistically distinct market synchronization state rather than random variation around normal correlation levels.

Evidence status in MI-001:

Plausible but untested.

Requires HV-001 formal validation.

## Alternative Explanations

Partially supported:

High COR-001 states may overlap with volatility stress.

Inconclusive:

Whether COR-001 provides information independent of VOL-001, MR-001, LIQ-001, or BRD-001.

Speculation:

High COR-001 states may represent common-factor dominance or deleveraging behavior. MI-001 cannot directly observe investor behavior or causal transmission.
