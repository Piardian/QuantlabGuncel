BACKGROUND

MI-001 identified the following primary explanatory mechanism:

COR-001 latent behavior primarily represents market-wide synchronization / common co-movement stress.

High COR-001 states were contemporaneously associated with higher realized volatility, deeper drawdowns, weaker breadth, and weaker market trend position.

These explanations now require formal empirical validation.

--------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE

--------------------------------------------------

Evaluate whether the mechanism hypotheses generated in MI-001 are consistently supported by empirical evidence.

This study evaluates explanatory validity only.

No predictive or economic conclusions are permitted.

--------------------------------------------------

PRIMARY HYPOTHESES

--------------------------------------------------

H1

High COR-001 states are consistently associated with significantly higher contemporaneous realized market volatility than low COR-001 states.

H2

High COR-001 states are consistently associated with deeper contemporaneous market drawdowns than low COR-001 states.

H3

High COR-001 states are consistently associated with weaker contemporaneous market breadth than low COR-001 states.

H4

High COR-001 states represent a statistically distinct market synchronization state rather than random variation around normal correlation levels.

--------------------------------------------------

- state characteristics remain stable across historical periods
- the identified mechanisms remain consistent under different market conditions
- alternative explanations better explain COR-001 behavior
