# BRD-001 / MI-001: Limitations

## Descriptive Scope

MI-001 is descriptive and explanatory.

It does not evaluate future outcomes.

## Bucket Limitation

LOW_BREADTH, MID_BREADTH, and HIGH_BREADTH are descriptive quintile-style buckets created for profiling.

They are not trading thresholds.

## Universe Limitation

BRD-001 uses a fixed current universe, not survivorship-free historical constituents.

## Variable Limitation

Market condition profiling used SPY same-day and trailing variables.

This does not cover every possible market-internal variable.

## Historical Event Limitation

Event alignment uses selected historical examples.

It is illustrative and does not establish a general statistical relationship.

## Interpretation Limitation

MI-001 can propose mechanism hypotheses.

It cannot validate them statistically.

Statistical explanatory validation belongs to HV-001.

## Forbidden Conclusions

MI-001 does not support claims about:

- prediction
- profitability
- alpha
- trading edge
- economic value
- production deployment
