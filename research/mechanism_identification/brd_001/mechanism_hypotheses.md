# BRD-001 / MI-001: Mechanism Hypotheses

## Purpose

Translate MI-001 descriptive findings into preregisterable hypotheses for HV-001.

These are not validated in MI-001.

## H1: Participation Mechanism

Hypothesis:

LOW_BREADTH states represent materially weaker long-term cross-sectional trend participation than HIGH_BREADTH states.

Expected observable evidence:

- lower percentage of securities above SMA200
- lower count above SMA200
- lower internal market confirmation

MI-001 status:

```text
Supported as a candidate mechanism
```

## H2: Index Trend Confirmation Mechanism

Hypothesis:

BRD-001 is associated with same-day broad index trend condition.

Expected observable evidence:

- higher BRD-001 when SPY is above SMA200
- higher BRD-001 when SPY distance from SMA200 is positive
- higher BRD-001 when SPY SMA50 is above SMA200

MI-001 status:

```text
Supported as a candidate mechanism
```

## H3: Stress Environment Mechanism

Hypothesis:

LOW_BREADTH states correspond to elevated contemporaneous market stress.

Expected observable evidence:

- higher same-day/trailing realized volatility
- deeper current drawdown from 52-week high
- weaker trailing SPY returns

MI-001 status:

```text
Supported as a candidate mechanism
```

## H4: Broad Participation Environment Mechanism

Hypothesis:

HIGH_BREADTH states correspond to broad, low-volatility expansion-like market conditions.

Expected observable evidence:

- high percentage of securities above SMA200
- SPY above SMA200
- low realized volatility
- shallow current drawdown

MI-001 status:

```text
Supported as a candidate mechanism
```

## HV-001 Boundary

HV-001 should validate these explanatory mechanisms statistically.

HV-001 must not evaluate predictive validity, trading performance, alpha, profitability, or economic value.
