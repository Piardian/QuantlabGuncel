## Identified Mechanism

BRD-001 primarily represents:

```text
Long-term cross-sectional trend participation and internal market confirmation.
```

Low BRD-001 values are associated with:

```text
Narrow participation, weak SPY trend condition, elevated realized volatility, and deeper contemporaneous drawdown.
```

High BRD-001 values are associated with:

```text
Broad participation, positive SPY trend condition, lower realized volatility, and shallow drawdown.
```
