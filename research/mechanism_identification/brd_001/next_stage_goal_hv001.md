MI-001 identified the primary explanatory mechanism:

BRD-001 represents long-term cross-sectional trend participation and internal market confirmation.

Low BRD-001 states are associated with narrow participation, weak SPY trend condition, elevated realized volatility and deeper contemporaneous drawdown.

High BRD-001 states are associated with broad participation, positive SPY trend condition, lower realized volatility and shallow contemporaneous drawdown.

These explanations now require formal empirical validation.

--------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE

--------------------------------------------------

Evaluate whether the mechanism hypotheses generated in MI-001 are consistently supported by empirical evidence.

This study evaluates explanatory validity only.

No predictive or economic conclusions are permitted.

--------------------------------------------------

PRIMARY HYPOTHESES

--------------------------------------------------

H1

LOW_BREADTH states are consistently associated with materially weaker long-term cross-sectional trend participation than HIGH_BREADTH states.

H2

LOW_BREADTH states are consistently associated with weaker same-day broad index trend condition than HIGH_BREADTH states.

H3

LOW_BREADTH states are consistently associated with higher same-day/trailing realized volatility than HIGH_BREADTH states.

H4

LOW_BREADTH states are consistently associated with deeper same-day drawdown from 52-week high than HIGH_BREADTH states.

H5

HIGH_BREADTH states represent a statistically distinct broad-participation market environment.
