# FUND-001 / MI-001

# Alternative Mechanisms

## Supported By Evidence

FUND-001 directly measures a short-term private financial funding spread over Treasury bills.

## Partially Supported

Elevated FUND-001 readings can be interpreted as funding stress when they coincide with commercial paper rate elevation relative to trailing context.

## Plausible Alternative Mechanisms

1. Counterparty credit concern embedded in commercial paper rates.
2. Safe-asset demand lowering Treasury bill rates.
3. Monetary policy or expected policy-rate movement affecting short-term rates.
4. Commercial paper market technicals unrelated to broad funding stress.
5. Treasury bill supply-demand distortions.

## Not Supported

FUND-001 alone does not isolate repo stress, margin haircut stress, dealer balance-sheet constraints, or central-bank liquidity demand.

## Implication For HV-001

HV-001 should test mechanism hypotheses carefully and separately distinguish:

- spread stress,
- commercial-paper funding-cost stress,
- Treasury-bill flight-to-quality contribution.

No predictive or economic conclusion is made here.
