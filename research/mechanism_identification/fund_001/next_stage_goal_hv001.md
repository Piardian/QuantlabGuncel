# RESEARCH PROGRAM

Market Signal Discovery Program

Version 3.0

Construct ID

FUND-001

Hypothesis Validation

HV-001

--------------------------------------------------

## BACKGROUND

FUND-001 has completed:

- RP-001
- LR-001
- CD-001
- IM-001
- CV-001
- MI-001

MI-001 identified FUND-001 as a short-term private financial funding spread stress sensor.

The mechanism is partially supported because high readings may arise from commercial paper rate elevation, Treasury bill rate decline, or both.

--------------------------------------------------

## PURPOSE

Formally validate whether the mechanism hypotheses generated in MI-001 are consistently supported by empirical evidence.

This stage evaluates explanatory validity only.

No predictive or economic conclusions are permitted.

--------------------------------------------------

## PRIMARY HYPOTHESES

H1

High FUND-001 states are associated with wider commercial-paper-to-Treasury-bill spreads.

H2

High FUND-001 states are associated with elevated commercial paper rates relative to trailing context.

H3

High FUND-001 states are associated with depressed Treasury bill rates relative to trailing context during some stress windows.

H4

FUND-001 represents a mixed funding-spread mechanism rather than a pure commercial-paper funding-cost mechanism.

--------------------------------------------------

## ALLOWED ANALYSIS

- distribution comparison,
- effect size estimation,
- confidence intervals,
- state comparison,
- cross-period validation,
- component contribution analysis,
- alternative mechanism comparison.

--------------------------------------------------

## FORBIDDEN

- evaluate prediction,
- run trading backtests,
- measure returns,
- evaluate alpha,
- evaluate economic value,
- change CD-001,
