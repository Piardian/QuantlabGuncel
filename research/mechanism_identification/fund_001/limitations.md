# FUND-001 / MI-001

# Limitations

- FUND-001 is a spread sensor, not a direct observation of funding liquidity.
- Component decomposition uses trailing context, not a structural model.
- Treasury bill movements can widen the spread even if commercial paper rates do not rise.
- Commercial paper rates may include credit and counterparty risk.
- Episode alignment is descriptive and does not establish prediction, causality, or economic value.
- MI-001 does not evaluate whether FUND-001 improves any decision process.
