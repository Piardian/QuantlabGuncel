# FUND-001 / MI-001

# Executive Summary

## Main Finding

FUND-001 is best interpreted as:

```text
Short-term private financial funding spread stress sensor
```

not as a pure funding-liquidity construct.

## Mechanism

High readings can arise from:

- higher commercial paper funding rates,
- lower Treasury bill rates,
- or both simultaneously.

This means FUND-001 partly reflects funding pressure, but can also reflect safe-asset demand, counterparty concern, credit risk, and short-rate context.

## Classification

Mechanism identification is:

```text
Partially supported
```
