# MI-001 Limitations

## Short Historical Coverage

The available CRD-001 validation sample begins in 2023. This limits mechanism identification across recessions, 2008, 2020, and other major credit-cycle stress periods.

## Internal Construct Profiling

State buckets are based on CRD-001's own percentile. This is appropriate for mechanism profiling but not sufficient to prove independence from other constructs.

## Component Ambiguity

High-yield OAS may reflect default expectations, liquidity stress, risk premium, and risk appetite. MI-001 does not decompose these components.

## No Predictive or Economic Claim

MI-001 does not test forecasting ability, trading performance, alpha generation, economic value, or production deployment.
