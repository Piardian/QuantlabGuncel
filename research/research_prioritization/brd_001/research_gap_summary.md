# Research Gap Summary

## Primary Gap

The project has no standalone market participation construct.

MR-001, LIQ-001, and VOL-001 describe regime, liquidity stress, and volatility state, but none directly measure whether market movement is broadly supported across securities.

## Scientific Gap

Without BRD-001, the platform cannot cleanly distinguish:

- index-level strength
- broad constituent participation
- narrow mega-cap leadership
- deterioration beneath an index
- sector- or universe-wide participation

## Measurement Gap

The project has not yet determined which breadth dimension should become the official construct:

- advance/decline
- moving-average breadth
- new-high/new-low breadth
- up/down volume breadth
- sector breadth
- equal-weight versus cap-weight confirmation

## Practical Gap

Risk and allocation workflows may benefit from knowing whether market participation is broad or narrow, but that possible utility must not be assumed before PV/EV stages.

## RP-001 Conclusion

The expected research contribution is high enough to justify a full lifecycle investigation.
