# Priority Assessment

## Final Priority

**High**

## Rationale

BRD-001 deserves high research priority because market breadth measures a market-internal dimension not covered directly by the completed MR-001, LIQ-001, or VOL-001 constructs.

The construct can potentially answer:

```text
Is market movement broadly supported across securities, or concentrated in a narrow subset?
```

This question is scientifically distinct from volatility state, liquidity stress, and index-level regime.
