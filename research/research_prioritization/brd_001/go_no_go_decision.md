# GO / NO GO Decision

## Final Decision

**GO**

## Decision Rationale

Market Breadth satisfies the minimum requirements for a standalone construct research program:

- It is recognized in market-internals analysis.
- It is conceptually distinct from regime, liquidity, volatility, trend and alpha.
- It has multiple reproducible measurement families.
- It can be measured from daily security-level panel data.
- It has practical relevance for participation, confirmation, concentration and market health.
- It may provide insight beyond completed MR-001, LIQ-001 and VOL-001 constructs.

## Authorized Next Stage

`BRD-001 / LR-001: Market Breadth Literature Review`

## Required Caution for LR-001

LR-001 must not prematurely select a breadth indicator.

It should first map the literature across:

- advance/decline measures
- moving-average breadth
- new-high/new-low measures
- up/down volume
- breadth thrust
- sector breadth
- equal-weight versus cap-weight confirmation
- ownership breadth as a related but distinct construct

No predictive, alpha, performance or economic claim may be made during LR-001.
