# Literature Overview

## Purpose

This is a high-level reconnaissance only. It is not the full BRD-001 literature review.

## Strongly Supported Themes

### Participation

Market breadth is commonly described as the number or share of securities participating in a market move.

### Advance / Decline Measures

Advance/decline lines and ratios are among the oldest and most widely recognized breadth measures.

### Moving-Average Breadth

Practitioner research frequently uses the percentage of securities above moving averages to assess broad trend participation.

### New High / New Low Measures

New highs and new lows represent participation at price extremes and are common breadth tools.

### Internal Confirmation

Breadth is often used to evaluate whether index-level movement is broadly confirmed by constituent-level participation.

## Representative Sources for LR-001

- Practitioner documentation from Fidelity and Schwab on advance/decline and breadth indicators.
- Academic work on market breadth and the cross-section of equity returns.
- Technical-analysis literature on advance/decline lines, new highs/lows, and moving-average breadth.
- Research on breadth of ownership and cross-sectional return implications, which is related but not identical and should be separated in LR-001.

## RP-001 Conclusion

The literature and practitioner foundation are sufficient to justify `BRD-001 / LR-001`.

However, LR-001 must clearly separate:

- market breadth as participation
- breadth of ownership
- cross-sectional dispersion
- momentum
- sector leadership
