# Executive Summary

BRD-001 / RP-001 evaluates whether a standalone **Market Breadth Construct** should enter the Market Signal Discovery Program v3.0 lifecycle.

## Decision

**GO**

## Why

Market Breadth is a recognized market-internals construct measuring the degree of participation across securities.

It is distinct from index trend, volatility, liquidity, regime and alpha.

## Priority

**High**

## Key Reason

The platform already has MR-001 for market regime, LIQ-001 for liquidity stress, and VOL-001 for volatility state.

It does not yet have a direct scientific sensor answering:

```text
Is market movement broadly supported across securities?
```

## Boundary

This stage does not choose a breadth indicator, define the construct, test prediction, evaluate economic utility, or modify any strategy.
