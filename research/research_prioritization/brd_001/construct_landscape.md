# Construct Landscape

## Construct Family

**Market Breadth**

## Scientific Meaning

Market breadth describes the extent of participation across securities within a market.

It asks whether market movement is broad and internally supported or narrow and concentrated.

## Relationship to Existing Constructs

### MR-001: Market Regime

MR-001 identifies latent market risk regimes from index-level return and volatility features. BRD-001 would study cross-security participation directly.

Relationship:

**Related but distinct.**

### LIQ-001: Liquidity Stress

LIQ-001 measures price-impact illiquidity. BRD-001 measures how many securities participate in market movement.

Relationship:

**Interacting but distinct.**

### VOL-001: Volatility State

VOL-001 measures realized market turbulence from SPY OHLC. BRD-001 measures participation breadth across securities.

Relationship:

**Related during stress, but distinct.**

### Momentum / Trend

Breadth can confirm or contradict index trend, but breadth itself is not simply trend. It measures cross-sectional participation in movement.

Relationship:

**Adjacent but distinct.**

## Major Breadth Dimensions

- advancing versus declining issues
- up-volume versus down-volume
- stocks making new highs or new lows
- stocks above moving averages
- broad participation versus narrow leadership
- sector-level participation
- index confirmation versus divergence

## Research Risk

The main risk is operational ambiguity. Breadth can mean many things.

CD-001 must freeze one narrow construct after LR-001.

## RP-001 Conclusion

The construct landscape supports a full research program, provided later stages avoid mixing incompatible breadth definitions.
