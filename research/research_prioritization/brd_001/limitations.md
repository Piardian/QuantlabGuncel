# Limitations

## RP-001 Scope Limitation

RP-001 is only a research-priority assessment. It does not define BRD-001.

## No Indicator Selection

No breadth indicator has been selected. That belongs to CD-001 after LR-001.

## No Predictive Validation

No claim is made that market breadth predicts returns, volatility, drawdowns, or future regime behavior.

## No Economic Validation

No claim is made that BRD-001 improves any portfolio workflow.

## Literature Reconnaissance Only

The references in RP-001 are high-level. A systematic literature review remains required in LR-001.

## Construct Ambiguity

Breadth is a broad family. Later stages must avoid mixing advance/decline, moving-average breadth, new-high/new-low breadth, ownership breadth, and sector breadth without explicit scientific justification.
