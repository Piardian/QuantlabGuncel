## PURPOSE

Conduct a comprehensive and unbiased literature review of Market Breadth as a financial construct.

The objective is to understand:

- how market breadth is defined
- how breadth is measured
- which breadth dimensions are most established
- where academic consensus exists
- where practitioner usage dominates
- where measurement disagreements remain
- which research gaps remain open

This study must remain descriptive.

--------------------------------------------------

## PRIMARY RESEARCH QUESTIONS

1.

How is Market Breadth defined throughout academic and practitioner literature?

2.

Which breadth dimensions are most commonly distinguished?

Examples:

- advance / decline participation
- up-volume / down-volume participation
- new highs / new lows
- moving-average breadth
- sector breadth
- breadth thrust
- equal-weight versus cap-weight confirmation
- narrow leadership versus broad participation

3.

Which breadth indicators are most established?

Examples:

- advance / decline line
- advance / decline ratio
- McClellan Oscillator
- percentage of stocks above moving averages
- new-high/new-low ratios
- up/down volume ratios

4.

What theoretical mechanisms explain breadth behavior?

5.

Which data inputs are required for each measurement family?

6.

What empirical evidence exists regarding breadth and market state behavior?

Only summarize reported findings.

Do NOT independently evaluate predictive validity.

7.

What limitations are consistently reported?

8.

Which research gaps remain open?

--------------------------------------------------

## INCLUSION CRITERIA

Prioritize:

- Peer-reviewed academic papers
- Journal articles
- Working papers from reputable institutions
- Established technical-analysis and market-internals literature

## EXCLUSION CRITERIA

- Evaluate trading performance
- Run backtests

- Draw predictive conclusions
- Draw economic conclusions
