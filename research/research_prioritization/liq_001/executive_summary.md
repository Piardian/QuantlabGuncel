# Executive Summary

LIQ-001 / RP-001 evaluates whether Market Liquidity should become a standalone construct in the Market Signal Discovery Program.

## Decision

**GO**

## Why

Market Liquidity is a fundamental financial construct with strong academic and professional recognition. It is distinct from volatility, market regime, and momentum because it describes trading friction, tradability, price impact, and market capacity.

## Priority

**High**

## Key Reason

MR-001 already covers market risk regime and volatility state. Liquidity may provide a different scientific channel: whether markets can absorb trades efficiently.

## Boundary

This stage does not define LIQ-001, select variables, test prediction, or evaluate economic utility.
