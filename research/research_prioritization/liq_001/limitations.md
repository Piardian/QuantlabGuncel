# Limitations

## RP-001 Limitations

- This is a prioritization study only.
- The literature review is high-level reconnaissance, not a full systematic review.
- No official LIQ-001 construct definition has been selected.
- No variable, proxy, model, frequency, or universe has been frozen.
- No empirical validation has been performed.
- No predictive or economic claim is made.

## Known Methodological Risks for Future Stages

- Liquidity is multidimensional.
- Measures may require different data frequencies.
- Daily proxies can be convenient but incomplete.
- High-frequency or order-book data may not be available.
- Liquidity can be confounded with volatility and stress regimes.
- Transaction-cost usefulness may depend heavily on execution assumptions.
