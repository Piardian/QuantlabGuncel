## Priority Rationale

Market Liquidity deserves a dedicated research program because it is both scientifically mature and practically important.

Unlike pure price-trend constructs, liquidity describes market trading conditions: how difficult, costly, or disruptive it is to transact. This makes it relevant to execution quality, portfolio capacity, slippage, risk management, and market stress.
