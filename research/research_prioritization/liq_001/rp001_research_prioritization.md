# LIQ-001 / RP-001: Research Prioritization

## Purpose

Determine whether Market Liquidity should become a standalone research construct within the Market Signal Discovery Program.

This stage evaluates research priority only. It does not define the final construct, select variables, evaluate prediction, or test economic utility.

## Primary Decision

**GO**

Market Liquidity should proceed to `LIQ-001 / LR-001`.

## Evidence Summary

Market Liquidity is strongly recognized in academic and professional finance as a fundamental market construct. It appears in market microstructure, transaction-cost analysis, asset pricing, financial stability, and risk-management literature.

The construct is distinct from volatility, momentum, and market regime. Liquidity can interact with those constructs, especially during stress periods, but it is not reducible to them. Liquidity describes the ability to transact with limited cost, price impact, delay, and market disruption.

The literature is mature enough to justify a dedicated research program. Important foundational work includes market microstructure dimensions such as tightness, depth, immediacy, breadth, and resiliency, as well as asset-pricing research on liquidity premia and systematic liquidity risk.

## Evaluation Dimensions

| Dimension | Assessment | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scientific relevance | High | Liquidity is a central construct in market microstructure, asset pricing, and financial stability. |
| Theoretical foundation | High | Established theories connect liquidity to transaction costs, market maker inventory risk, asymmetric information, funding constraints, and asset pricing. |
| Construct independence | High | Liquidity is related to volatility and stress but conceptually distinct from both. |
| Literature maturity | High | Decades of academic research exist, including foundational and survey-level work. |
| Data availability | Medium | Daily proxies are broadly available; direct order book or bid-ask data may require richer datasets. |
| Measurability | Medium to High | Multiple reproducible measures exist, but no single universal measure dominates all contexts. |
| Practical importance | High | Liquidity affects execution cost, capacity, slippage, portfolio risk, and market stability. |
| Expected research contribution | High | LIQ-001 may add information not captured by MR-001 because liquidity describes trading frictions rather than only realized return/volatility states. |

## Supported Claims

- Market Liquidity is a recognized financial construct.
- Liquidity has a mature academic literature.
- Liquidity is conceptually distinct from volatility and market regime.
- Liquidity can be operationalized reproducibly, though measurement choice will require careful literature review.
- A dedicated research program is scientifically justified.

## Not Supported at RP Stage

- Liquidity predicts future returns.
- Liquidity improves trading performance.
- Liquidity has economic value in this project.
- Any specific liquidity measure is superior.

LIQ-001 receives a **GO** decision for the next stage.
