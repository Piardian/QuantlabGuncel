# GO / NO GO Decision

## Final Decision

**GO**

## Decision Rationale

Market Liquidity satisfies the minimum requirements for a standalone construct research program:

- It is scientifically recognized.
- It has a mature literature.
- It is theoretically distinct from existing validated constructs.
- It has multiple reproducible measurement families.
- It has practical importance for execution, risk, capacity, and market stability.
- It may provide insight beyond MR-001 because it targets trading friction rather than only return/volatility regime state.

## Authorized Next Stage

`LIQ-001 / LR-001: Market Liquidity Literature Review`

## Required Caution for LR-001

LR-001 must not prematurely choose a liquidity measure. It should first map the literature across:

- spread-based measures
- volume-based measures
- price-impact measures
- resiliency measures
- turnover measures
- aggregate market liquidity measures
- high-frequency versus daily-data approaches
