# Research Gap Summary

## Main Gaps

1. Liquidity is multidimensional, and no single measure captures all aspects.
2. Daily proxies are easier to reproduce but may miss order-book depth, resiliency, and intraday execution friction.
3. High-quality microstructure data can be expensive or unavailable.
4. Liquidity often interacts with volatility, market stress, and funding conditions, making independence testing important.
5. The practical role of liquidity may differ by use case: alpha signal, risk warning, execution filter, capacity control, or market-stability indicator.

## Project-Specific Gaps

- The current project has a validated market regime construct, but it does not yet have a dedicated trading-friction or market-quality construct.
- It is unknown whether a liquidity construct would add information beyond MR-001.
- It is unknown whether daily-data liquidity proxies are sufficient for the project scope.

## Research Opportunity

LIQ-001 could fill a distinct gap by evaluating market tradability and friction, which MR-001 does not directly measure.
