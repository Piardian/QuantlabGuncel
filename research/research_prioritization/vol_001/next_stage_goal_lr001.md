## PURPOSE

Conduct a comprehensive and unbiased literature review of Market Volatility as a financial construct.

The objective is to understand:

- how volatility is defined
- how volatility is measured
- which volatility dimensions are most established
- where academic consensus exists
- where measurement disagreements remain
- which research gaps remain open

This study must remain descriptive.

--------------------------------------------------

## PRIMARY RESEARCH QUESTIONS

1.

How is Market Volatility defined throughout academic and practitioner literature?

2.

Which volatility dimensions are most commonly distinguished?

Examples:

- historical realized volatility
- high-frequency realized volatility
- range-based volatility
- conditional volatility
- implied volatility
- cross-sectional volatility
- volatility clustering
- volatility persistence

3.

Which volatility estimators are most established?

Examples:

- close-to-close realized volatility
- Parkinson
- Garman-Klass
- Rogers-Satchell
- Yang-Zhang
- ATR-derived volatility
- ARCH/GARCH
- VIX-style implied volatility

4.

What theoretical mechanisms explain volatility-state behavior?

5.

Which data inputs are required for each measurement family?

6.

What empirical evidence exists regarding volatility persistence and volatility-state behavior?

Only summarize reported findings.

Do NOT independently evaluate predictive validity.

7.

What limitations are consistently reported?

8.

Which research gaps remain open?
