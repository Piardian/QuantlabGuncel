# Executive Summary

VOL-001 / RP-001 evaluates whether a standalone **Volatility State Construct** should enter the Market Signal Discovery Program v3.0 lifecycle.

## Decision

**GO**

## Why

Market volatility is a foundational financial construct with strong academic and practitioner recognition. It is distinct from direction, alpha, liquidity and market regime, although it interacts with each.

## Priority

**High**

## Key Reason

The platform already has MR-001 for market regime and LIQ-001 for liquidity stress. It does not yet have a direct scientific sensor answering:

```text
What is the current volatility state of the market?
```

## Boundary

This stage does not choose a volatility estimator, define the construct, test prediction, evaluate economic utility, or modify any strategy.
