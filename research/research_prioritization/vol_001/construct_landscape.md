# Construct Landscape

## Construct Family

**Market Volatility**

## Scientific Meaning

Market volatility describes the magnitude, variability and dispersion of market price changes over time.

It is not a directional construct. High volatility can occur during falling, rising or sideways markets.

## Relationship to Existing Constructs

### MR-001: Market Regime

MR-001 uses volatility as one input into a latent market regime model. VOL-001 would instead study volatility state directly.

Relationship:

**Related but distinct.**

### LIQ-001: Liquidity Stress

LIQ-001 measures aggregate price-impact illiquidity. Volatility often rises during liquidity stress, but volatility and liquidity are not identical.

Relationship:

**Interacting but distinct.**

### Momentum / Trend

Momentum and trend describe directional persistence. Volatility describes magnitude of variation.

Relationship:

**Conceptually distinct.**

## Major Volatility Dimensions

- Realized variation from returns
- Intraday or range-based dispersion
- Conditional expected volatility
- Option-implied volatility
- Cross-sectional dispersion
- Volatility persistence
- Volatility clustering
- Volatility-of-volatility

## Research Risk

The primary research risk is construct ambiguity. Volatility can be measured many ways, and different estimators represent different dimensions.

Therefore LR-001 must map the literature carefully before CD-001 freezes one narrow construct.

## RP-001 Conclusion

The construct landscape supports a full research program, provided that later stages avoid mixing incompatible volatility definitions.
