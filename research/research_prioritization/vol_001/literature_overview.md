# Literature Overview

## Purpose

This is a high-level reconnaissance only. It is not the full VOL-001 literature review.

## Strongly Supported Literature Themes

### Volatility Clustering

Financial returns exhibit periods of persistently high and low volatility. ARCH and GARCH models were developed to model this time-varying conditional volatility.

### Realized Volatility

Realized volatility and realized variance provide nonparametric ex-post measures of return variation. This literature is central to volatility measurement and forecasting.

### Range-Based Volatility

OHLC-based estimators such as Parkinson, Garman-Klass, Rogers-Satchell and Yang-Zhang use intraday range information and can estimate volatility more efficiently than close-to-close returns under specific assumptions.

### Implied Volatility

Option-implied volatility, especially VIX-style measures, represents market expectations of future volatility inferred from option prices.

### Portfolio Risk Use

Volatility is foundational for portfolio risk management, volatility targeting, risk budgeting, option pricing and risk-aware exposure decisions.

## Representative Sources for LR-001

- Engle's ARCH work and Bollerslev's GARCH extension for conditional volatility modeling.
- Andersen, Bollerslev, Diebold and Labys for realized volatility measurement and forecasting.
- Parkinson for high-low range volatility estimation.
- Rogers-Satchell and Yang-Zhang for OHLC volatility estimators.
- Cboe VIX methodology for implied volatility construction.
