# GO / NO GO Decision

## Final Decision

**GO**

## Decision Rationale

Market Volatility satisfies the minimum requirements for a standalone construct research program:

- It is scientifically recognized.
- It has mature theoretical and empirical literature.
- It is distinct from market direction, alpha, liquidity and regime.
- It has multiple reproducible measurement families.
- It has direct practical relevance for risk management and portfolio control.
- It may provide scientific insight beyond MR-001 and LIQ-001.

## Authorized Next Stage

`VOL-001 / LR-001: Market Volatility Literature Review`

## Required Caution for LR-001

LR-001 must not prematurely select a volatility estimator.

It should first map the literature across:

- realized volatility
- range-based volatility
- conditional volatility models
- implied volatility
- ATR-derived volatility
- cross-sectional volatility
- volatility persistence and clustering

No predictive, alpha, performance or economic claim may be made during LR-001.
