# Limitations

## RP-001 Scope Limitation

RP-001 is only a research-priority assessment. It does not define VOL-001.

## No Estimator Selection

No volatility estimator has been selected. That belongs to CD-001 after LR-001.

## No Predictive Validation

No claim is made that volatility state predicts returns, drawdowns, realized volatility or portfolio outcomes.

## No Economic Validation

No claim is made that VOL-001 improves any portfolio workflow.

## Literature Reconnaissance Only

The literature references in RP-001 are high-level. A systematic literature review remains required in LR-001.

## Construct Ambiguity

Volatility is a broad family. Later stages must avoid mixing realized, implied, conditional, range-based and cross-sectional definitions without explicit scientific justification.
