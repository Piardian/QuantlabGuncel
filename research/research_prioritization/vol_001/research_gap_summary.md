# Research Gap Summary

## Primary Gap

The project already has a market regime construct and a liquidity stress construct, but it does not yet have a standalone volatility-state construct.

## Scientific Gap

Without VOL-001, the platform cannot cleanly distinguish:

- volatility as a direct observable market state
- volatility as an input to latent regime classification
- volatility as a consequence of liquidity stress
- volatility as a risk-management signal

## Measurement Gap

There are many valid volatility estimators. The project has not yet determined which dimension is most appropriate for a standalone scientific sensor:

- close-to-close realized volatility
- range-based volatility
- implied volatility
- cross-sectional volatility
- conditional model-based volatility

## Practical Gap

Portfolio risk workflows require current volatility-state awareness. MR-001 and LIQ-001 may help, but neither replaces a direct volatility-state sensor.

## RP-001 Conclusion

The expected research contribution is high enough to justify a full lifecycle investigation.
