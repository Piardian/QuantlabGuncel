# Priority Assessment

## Final Priority

**High**

## Rationale

VOL-001 deserves high research priority because market volatility is central to risk measurement, financial econometrics, derivatives, portfolio construction and risk-control workflows.

The construct is also strategically important for the existing research platform because MR-001 and LIQ-001 already address market regime and liquidity stress. A standalone volatility-state construct can clarify whether volatility should be treated as an independent scientific sensor rather than only as an input to other constructs.
