## Scientific Relevance

Assessment: High.

Options markets encode forward-looking risk-neutral information. This makes OPT-001 scientifically distinct from realized-price constructs already studied in the program.

## Theoretical Foundation

Assessment: High.

The literature links options prices to:

- expected volatility,
- risk-neutral density,
- variance risk premium,
- skewness and tail risk,
- hedging demand,
- investor risk aversion,
- information asymmetry.

## Construct Independence

Assessment: High.

Options-implied information is not identical to realized volatility, realized correlation, market regime, credit spread stress, or funding spread stress.

However, overlap is expected and must be studied later.

## Literature Maturity

Assessment: High.

Major bodies of literature exist on VIX, variance risk premium, risk-neutral moments, option-implied skewness, put-call parity deviations, and option-implied risk.

## Data Availability

Assessment: Moderate.

Index-level options-implied data such as VIX-related series are widely available. Full cross-sectional option surface data is more expensive and difficult.

## Measurability

Assessment: Moderate.

Several measurements are reproducible if the scope is narrow. Broad option surface constructs require careful filtering, maturity selection, interpolation, liquidity controls, and data-quality rules.

## Practical Importance

Assessment: High.

Options-implied information is widely used by practitioners for risk monitoring, volatility forecasting, hedging, sentiment, and tail-risk assessment.

## Expected Research Contribution

Assessment: High.

OPT-001 can test whether option-implied information provides scientific information beyond realized-market sensors already completed.

## Priority Verdict

OPT-001 should proceed to LR-001.
