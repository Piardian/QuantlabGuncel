# OPT-001 / RP-001

# Literature Overview

## Purpose

This is a high-level literature reconnaissance for research prioritization only.

It is not the full LR-001 systematic literature review.

## Major Evidence Areas

## 1. VIX And Implied Volatility

Cboe methodology defines VIX as an options-based volatility index using S&P 500 option prices. It is designed to represent market expectations of near-term volatility.

Evidence status: Strong literature and practitioner recognition.

## 2. Variance Risk Premium

Bollerslev, Tauchen, and Zhou study the difference between option-implied and realized variation and relate it to expected stock returns and priced uncertainty.

Evidence status: Strong academic foundation, but measurement choices matter.

## 3. Risk-Neutral Moments

Bakshi, Kapadia, and Madan derive and study risk-neutral skewness and higher moments from option prices.

Evidence status: Strong theoretical foundation.

## 4. Option-Implied Skew / Smirk

Xing, Zhang, and Zhao report that individual option volatility smirks contain information about future equity returns and negative information flow.

Evidence status: Strong cited evidence, but requires detailed option surface data.

## 5. Put-Call Parity Deviations

Cremers and Weinbaum study deviations from put-call parity and report information about future stock returns.

Evidence status: Strong cited evidence, but sensitive to microstructure, liquidity, and implementation details.

## 6. Option-Implied Risk Measures

Option-implied volatility, skewness, and related moments have been used to estimate future risk, beta, tail risk, and market conditions.

Evidence status: Moderate to strong, depending on the specific measure.

## Literature Implication

The literature strongly justifies OPT-001 as a research family, but does not justify treating all option-implied measures as a single construct.

LR-001 must separate subdomains before CD-001 selects one exact construct.
