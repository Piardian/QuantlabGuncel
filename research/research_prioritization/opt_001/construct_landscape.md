# OPT-001 / RP-001

# Construct Landscape

## Candidate Construct Family

```text
Options-Implied Market Information
```

## Conceptual Domain

Options-implied market information refers to information inferred from option prices, option surfaces, or option-market trading behavior.

It may represent:

- forward-looking volatility expectations,
- priced uncertainty,
- risk-neutral tail probabilities,
- downside protection demand,
- crash risk pricing,
- variance risk compensation,
- informed trading in derivatives,
- hedging pressure.

## Major Subdomains

### Implied Volatility Level

Examples:

- VIX,
- at-the-money implied volatility,
- implied volatility indexes.

Potential construct meaning:

```text
market-priced expected volatility
```

### Implied Volatility Term Structure

Examples:

- VIX futures curve,
- short-term vs medium-term implied volatility,
- contango/backwardation.

Potential construct meaning:

```text
term structure of priced uncertainty
```

### Volatility Risk Premium

Examples:

- implied variance minus expected or realized variance,
- variance swap premium,
- VIX-related variance premium measures.

Potential construct meaning:

```text
compensation for bearing volatility risk
```

### Risk-Neutral Skewness And Tail Risk

Examples:

- implied skew,
- option-implied tail loss probability,
- put-wing steepness,
- risk-neutral moments.

Potential construct meaning:

```text
priced downside asymmetry and tail risk
```

### Option-Implied Correlation

Examples:

- index implied variance vs component implied variances,
- dispersion-implied correlation.

Potential construct meaning:

```text
priced systemic co-movement
```

### Put-Call Parity Deviations

Examples:

- call-put implied volatility spread,
- deviations from arbitrage parity after frictions.

Potential construct meaning:

```text
option-market informed demand imbalance or constraints
```

### Option Flow And Positioning

Examples:

- volume,
- open interest,
- put-call ratio,
- dealer gamma estimates.

Potential construct meaning:

```text
derivatives-market positioning pressure
```

## Key Landscape Risk

OPT-001 is not one construct yet. It is a construct family.

LR-001 must map the literature before CD-001 freezes one narrow operational definition.
