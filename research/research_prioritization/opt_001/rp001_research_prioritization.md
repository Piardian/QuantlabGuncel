# OPT-001 / RP-001: Research Prioritization

## Construct Candidate

```text
Options-Implied Market Information
```

## Purpose

Determine whether options-implied market information should become a standalone research construct within the Market Signal Discovery Program v3.0.

This stage evaluates research priority only. It does not define the construct, select variables, implement indicators, test prediction, or evaluate economic value.

## Primary Research Questions

1. Is options-implied information recognized as a fundamental market construct in academic and professional literature?
2. Does it represent a distinct concept rather than a subset of volatility, market regime, credit stress, or liquidity?
3. Is there sufficient theoretical and empirical literature to justify a dedicated research program?
4. Can options-implied information be operationally defined in a reproducible way?
5. Could investigation provide scientific insight beyond existing validated constructs?

## Executive Answer

OPT-001 should proceed to a full scientific research program.

Decision:

```text
GO
```

```text
9.0 / 10
```

## Rationale

Options markets are widely studied because option prices contain forward-looking, risk-neutral information about volatility, tail risk, skewness, variance compensation, hedging demand, and investor risk aversion.

The construct is distinct from existing realized-market sensors:

- VOL-001 measures realized volatility state.
- MR-001 measures latent market regime from realized returns and realized volatility.
- COR-001 measures realized co-movement.
- CRD-001 measures credit spread stress.
- FUND-001 measures a funding spread.

OPT-001 would represent information embedded in option prices rather than information observed only from realized asset prices or spreads.

## Main Scientific Opportunity

OPT-001 can become the program's first construct family focused on risk-neutral, forward-looking market information.

This is scientifically important because realized constructs and options-implied constructs answer different questions.

Realized constructs ask:

```text
What has the market done?
```

Options-implied constructs ask:

```text
What risk distribution is currently priced by the options market?
```

## Main Risk

The options domain is broad. If LR/CD do not narrow the construct carefully, OPT-001 can become too vague.

Potential sub-constructs include:

- implied volatility level,
- implied volatility term structure,
- volatility risk premium,
- risk-neutral skewness,
- implied tail risk,
- volatility smirk/smile,
- option-implied correlation,
- put-call parity deviations,
- option volume/open-interest behavior.

These should not be mixed without explicit construct definition.

## RP-001 Decision

OPT-001 is approved for LR-001.

The next stage must perform a systematic literature review and separate option-implied subdomains before any construct is selected.
