# OPT-001 / RP-001

# Limitations

## No Construct Definition

RP-001 does not define OPT-001. It only determines whether the construct family merits research.

## Broad Domain Risk

Options-implied information includes many possible constructs. Treating them as one undifferentiated object would be scientifically weak.

## Data Limitations

Index-level data may be available, but full option surface data can be expensive, incomplete, or complex to process.

## Microstructure Sensitivity

Options data can be affected by bid-ask spreads, stale quotes, liquidity, moneyness, maturity, exercise style, dividends, and corporate events.

## No Predictive Claims

RP-001 does not evaluate forecast power.

## No Economic Claims

RP-001 does not evaluate profitability, risk-management value, portfolio utility, or trading performance.

## No Production Claims

RP-001 does not imply that any option-based signal should be used in production.
