# Construct Landscape

## Candidate Construct

Funding Stress / Funding Liquidity Conditions.

## Conceptual Domain

Funding Stress describes the state in which market participants face increased difficulty or cost in obtaining short-term funding, rolling liabilities, financing positions, or maintaining leverage.

## Related But Distinct Constructs

| Construct | Relationship | Difference |
|---|---|---|
| LIQ-001 Market Liquidity | Closely related | LIQ measures trading frictions; FUND measures financing constraints. |
| CRD-001 Credit Stress | Related | CRD measures credit risk compensation; FUND measures funding availability/cost. |
| VOL-001 Volatility State | Related | Volatility can trigger margins; funding stress concerns financing capacity. |
| COR-001 Correlation State | Related | Common shocks can worsen funding pressure; correlation is not funding availability. |
| MR-001 Market Regime | Related | Market regimes can include stress; FUND is narrower. |

## Candidate Measurement Families

- Unsecured funding spreads.
- Secured funding spreads.
- Reference-rate basis measures.
- Funding components of financial stress indexes.
- Dealer balance-sheet or leverage proxies.
- Margin and collateral stress proxies.
