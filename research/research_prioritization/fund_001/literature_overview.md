# Literature Overview

## High-Level Literature Signals

Funding Stress is supported by several mature research streams:

- Funding liquidity theory.
- Market liquidity and funding liquidity interaction.
- Margin spirals and deleveraging.
- Interbank funding stress.
- Financial stress index construction.
- Reference-rate and wholesale funding market research.

## Representative Sources

- Brunnermeier and Pedersen, "Market Liquidity and Funding Liquidity."
- ECB work on funding liquidity risk definition and measurement.
- Federal Reserve and New York Fed work on interbank funding markets and LIBOR-related funding costs.
- OFR Financial Stress Index methodology, which separates funding as a stress category.
- Chicago Fed NFCI documentation, where risk indicators include volatility and funding risk.

## Preliminary Literature Assessment

The literature is mature enough to justify LR-001. However, it also shows that funding stress is measurement-sensitive. Some proxies capture bank funding, some capture secured funding, some capture interbank risk, and some are composite stress categories.
