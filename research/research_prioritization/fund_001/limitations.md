# RP-001 Limitations

## High-Level Review Only

RP-001 is a prioritization stage, not a full literature review.

## No Construct Definition

No official FUND-001 definition is selected here.

## Data Proxy Risk

Funding stress may be difficult to represent with one public series, especially after LIBOR transition.

## Construct Overlap

Funding stress overlaps with liquidity, credit, volatility, leverage, and financial conditions constructs.

## No Predictive Or Economic Claim

RP-001 does not evaluate prediction, trading performance, alpha, economic value, or production readiness.
