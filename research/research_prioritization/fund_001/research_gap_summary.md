# Research Gap Summary

## Gap 1: Funding Stress Definition

The term can refer to unsecured funding stress, secured funding stress, margin stress, dealer balance-sheet stress, or composite funding conditions.

## Gap 2: Post-LIBOR Measurement

Traditional LIBOR-based proxies may be less reliable or structurally changed after the LIBOR transition.

## Gap 3: Independence From Other Constructs

Funding Stress overlaps with LIQ, CRD, VOL, COR, and MR. Later stages must clarify whether FUND-001 represents a distinct observable construct.

## Gap 4: Public Reproducibility

Some high-quality funding measures require proprietary data or institutional methodology.

## Gap 5: Economic Role

Funding Stress may be most useful for risk management rather than return prediction. This cannot be evaluated at RP-001.
