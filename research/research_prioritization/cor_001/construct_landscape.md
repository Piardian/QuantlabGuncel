# Construct Landscape

## Candidate Construct

COR-001: Market Correlation / Correlation State Construct

## What The Construct May Represent

At a high level, a correlation construct measures the degree to which assets move together.

Potential observable dimensions include:

- Average pairwise equity correlation
- Rolling correlation matrix behavior
- Cross-asset correlation, such as stock-bond correlation
- Sector-level co-movement
- Realized correlation
- Implied correlation, if options data is available
- Correlation concentration, such as first principal component share
- Dynamic conditional correlation
- Tail dependence and crisis co-movement
- Network concentration of dependence

## Distinction From Existing Constructs

MR-001 measures latent market regime from SPY returns and volatility.

VOL-001 measures volatility state.

LIQ-001 measures liquidity stress.

BRD-001 measures market breadth and participation.

COR-001 would measure co-movement and diversification structure. This is not fully captured by any completed construct.

## Key Design Tensions For Future CD-001

- Pearson correlation versus nonlinear dependence
- Rolling window length versus estimator stability
- Equal-weighted versus capitalization-weighted universe treatment
- Equity-only versus cross-asset correlation
- Current constituents versus historical constituents
- Realized correlation versus implied correlation
- Correlation level versus correlation change
- Broad market state versus sector-specific state

The construct landscape is broad enough to justify a full literature review, but too broad to define during RP-001.
