# Executive Summary

COR-001 was evaluated as a candidate Market Correlation / Correlation State Construct.

RP-001 concludes GO.

The construct is scientifically important because correlation describes asset co-movement, diversification effectiveness, common risk exposure, and potential stress concentration. These concepts are related to but distinct from existing constructs such as market regime, volatility state, liquidity, and breadth.

The strongest reason to proceed is that correlation may capture a risk dimension not directly measured by the current construct library: whether assets are moving independently or together.

The main caution is methodological. Correlation estimation is sensitive to volatility, window length, universe definition, missing data, and nonlinear dependence. These issues must be handled in LR-001 and CD-001 before implementation or validation.

Final decision: GO to COR-001 / LR-001.
