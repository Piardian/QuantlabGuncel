# Literature Overview

## Purpose

This file provides high-level reconnaissance only. It is not a systematic literature review.

## Established Themes

### Portfolio Diversification

Correlation is central to portfolio risk because diversification depends not only on individual asset volatility but also on co-movement between holdings.

### Time-Varying Correlation

Financial correlations are not constant. Literature and practitioner research commonly report that correlations can rise during stress periods, reducing diversification effectiveness.

### Contagion And Interdependence

Correlation increases during crises are difficult to interpret. Forbes and Rigobon emphasize that heteroskedasticity can bias simple correlation-based contagion tests.

### Systemic Risk

High co-movement can indicate concentration of common risk exposure. Correlation structures are therefore relevant to systemic risk and portfolio vulnerability.

### Cross-Asset Allocation

Changes in stock-bond or cross-asset correlations affect portfolio construction, risk parity, and diversification assumptions.
