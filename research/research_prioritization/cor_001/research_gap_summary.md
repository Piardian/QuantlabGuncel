# Research Gap Summary

## Program Gap

The Market Signal Discovery Program has completed or advanced constructs for market regime, volatility state, liquidity, and breadth. None of these directly measures cross-asset co-movement or diversification breakdown.

## Scientific Gap

Correlation is widely recognized, but implementation choices can materially affect interpretation. A dedicated research program is justified to avoid treating correlation as an informal supporting statistic rather than a rigorously defined construct.

## Key Open Questions For Future Stages

- Which correlation definition is most scientifically appropriate for a market state construct?
- Should COR-001 represent equity-only correlation or cross-asset correlation?
- Should the construct measure correlation level, correlation change, or correlation concentration?
- How should heteroskedasticity bias be handled?
- Can the construct be implemented deterministically with available data?
- Does the construct remain stable across historical periods?
- Does it provide predictive information for future risk variables?
- Does it provide economic utility in risk management workflows?

## Boundary

These questions are not answered in RP-001. They define the expected research value of proceeding.
