# Priority Assessment

## Construct Candidate

COR-001: Market Correlation / Correlation State Construct

## Final Priority

High

## Evaluation Dimensions

| Dimension | Assessment | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Scientific relevance | High | Correlation is central to portfolio theory, diversification, systemic risk, and contagion/interdependence research. |
| Theoretical foundation | High | Co-movement, common factors, diversification failure, and dependence structures are established finance concepts. |
| Construct independence | High | Correlation measures dependence among assets; it is related to but distinct from volatility, liquidity, breadth, and regime. |
| Literature maturity | High | A large academic and practitioner literature exists across portfolio theory, covariance modeling, DCC models, contagion, and systemic risk. |
| Data availability | Medium-High | Daily return panels are available; broader measures may need clean universe design and robust missing-data handling. |
| Measurability | Medium-High | Multiple estimators exist, but measurement is sensitive to window length, universe, heteroskedasticity, and nonlinear dependence. |
| Practical importance | High | Correlation affects diversification, portfolio risk, hedging, risk budgeting, and stress monitoring. |
| Expected research contribution | High | COR-001 can fill a distinct gap not covered by existing validated risk-state constructs. |

## Priority Score

8.6 / 10

## Decision

GO

## Decision Rationale

Market Correlation is a mature, independent, and practically important construct. The main research risk is not whether the concept matters, but whether a narrow, reproducible, scientifically justified operational definition can be frozen in CD-001.
