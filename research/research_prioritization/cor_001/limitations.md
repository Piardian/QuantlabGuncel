# RP-001 Limitations

## Scope Limitations

RP-001 is a prioritization review, not a literature review or empirical study.

No systematic citation scoring was performed.

No construct definition was selected.

No data was analyzed.

## Scientific Limitations

Correlation can be unstable and sensitive to estimation window.

Simple correlations can be biased during high-volatility periods.

Pearson correlation may miss nonlinear dependence and tail dependence.

Correlation and volatility often interact, making construct separation important.

Universe selection can materially affect any future correlation measure.

Survivorship-free historical constituent data may be difficult to obtain.

## Interpretation Limitations

GO means the construct deserves a full scientific research lifecycle.

GO does not imply predictive value, economic value, profitability, or production suitability.
