# Research Gap Summary

## Program Gap

The Market Signal Discovery Program has completed constructs for market regime, volatility, liquidity, breadth, and correlation.

None directly measures credit-market financing stress.

## Scientific Gap

Credit stress is widely recognized, but it can be represented by many different proxies. A dedicated research program is justified to avoid treating credit stress as a vague macro overlay.

## Key Open Questions For Future Stages

- Should CRD-001 use high-yield spreads, investment-grade spreads, Baa/Treasury spreads, Baa/Aaa spreads, or excess bond premium?
- Should the construct measure spread level, spread change, or normalized stress?
- Should it prioritize long historical availability or richer credit-market specificity?
- Can it be implemented deterministically from public data?
- Does it remain stable across historical periods?
- Does it represent default-risk repricing, liquidity stress, risk-premium stress, or a mixture?
- Does it contain predictive information for future market risk variables?
- Does it provide economic utility in risk-management workflows?

## Boundary

These questions are not answered in RP-001. They define the expected research value of proceeding.
