# Literature Overview

## Purpose

This file provides high-level reconnaissance only. It is not a systematic literature review.

## Established Themes

### Credit Spreads

Credit spreads measure the yield compensation investors require for holding risky debt relative to safer reference rates.

### Default Risk And Risk Premia

Credit spreads embed expected default losses, risk premia, recovery expectations, tax effects, liquidity, and market-risk components.

### Excess Bond Premium

Gilchrist and Zakrajsek separate credit spreads into expected-default components and a residual excess bond premium, which is widely discussed as a financing-risk or risk-premium indicator.

### Financial Stress Indexes

Federal Reserve and OFR-style financial stress indexes commonly include credit spread variables as core components.

### Business Cycle And Monetary Transmission

Credit stress is frequently studied as a channel linking financial conditions, borrowing costs, investment, economic activity, and asset prices.
