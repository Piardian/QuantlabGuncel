# Construct Landscape

## Candidate Construct

CRD-001: Credit Stress Construct

## What The Construct May Represent

At a high level, credit stress measures debt-market pressure, default-risk compensation, risk-premium repricing, or deterioration in financing conditions.

Potential observable dimensions include:

- investment-grade credit spreads
- high-yield credit spreads
- Baa versus Treasury spread
- Baa versus Aaa spread
- option-adjusted spreads
- credit default swap indexes
- excess bond premium
- leveraged-loan spreads
- distressed-credit spreads
- credit components of financial stress indexes
- default probability or expected default frequency proxies

## Distinction From Existing Constructs

MR-001 measures latent market regime from SPY returns and volatility.

VOL-001 measures realized volatility state.

LIQ-001 measures liquidity stress.

BRD-001 measures market breadth and participation.

COR-001 measures equity co-movement and synchronization.

CRD-001 would measure financing-risk and debt-market stress. This is not fully captured by any completed construct.

## Key Design Tensions For Future CD-001

- investment-grade versus high-yield stress
- spread level versus spread change
- Treasury-relative versus rating-relative spreads
- OAS versus simple yield spread
- realized credit stress versus expected/default-risk model
- public bond-market data versus CDS/loan/private-credit data
- broad market stress versus sector-specific credit stress
- long history versus richer modern data
