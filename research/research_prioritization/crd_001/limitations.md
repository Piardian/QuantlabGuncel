# RP-001 Limitations

## Scope Limitations

RP-001 is a prioritization review, not a systematic literature review or empirical study.

No construct definition was selected.

No data was analyzed.

No predictive or economic evidence was evaluated.

## Scientific Limitations

Credit spreads may reflect multiple forces:

- expected default losses
- risk premia
- liquidity
- taxes
- recovery assumptions
- duration effects
- monetary policy
- market-wide risk appetite

These components must not be mixed casually in CD-001.

## Data Limitations

Some rich credit datasets require paid or institutional access.

Public data exists, but series availability, revisions, start dates, and methodology changes must be reviewed.

## Interpretation Limitations

GO means the construct deserves a full scientific research lifecycle.

GO does not imply predictive value, economic value, profitability, alpha, or production suitability.
