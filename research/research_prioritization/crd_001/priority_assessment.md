# Priority Assessment

## Construct Candidate

CRD-001: Credit Stress Construct

## Final Priority

High

## Evaluation Dimensions

| Dimension | Assessment | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Scientific relevance | High | Credit spreads are central to corporate finance, macro-finance, business-cycle analysis, and financial stability. |
| Theoretical foundation | High | Credit stress is connected to default risk, risk premia, financing constraints, monetary transmission, liquidity, and balance-sheet stress. |
| Construct independence | High | Credit stress is related to volatility and liquidity but measures a distinct debt-market financing-risk dimension. |
| Literature maturity | High | Decades of academic and institutional literature exist on credit spreads, default risk, excess bond premium, and financial stress. |
| Data availability | High | Public series exist via FRED and institutional sources, including Baa spreads, high-yield OAS, investment-grade OAS, and financial stress components. |
| Measurability | High | Multiple reproducible proxies exist, though CD-001 must select one narrow definition. |
| Practical importance | High | Credit stress affects risk appetite, financing conditions, default risk, monetary transmission, and portfolio risk. |
| Expected research contribution | High | CRD-001 may add a financing-stress dimension not captured by volatility, breadth, liquidity, correlation, or market regime constructs. |

## Priority Score

9.1 / 10

## Decision

GO

## Decision Rationale

Credit Stress is a mature, independent, and practically important construct. The main research risk is not whether the concept matters, but whether CD-001 can select a narrow, reproducible, scientifically justified operational definition without mixing multiple credit-risk dimensions.
