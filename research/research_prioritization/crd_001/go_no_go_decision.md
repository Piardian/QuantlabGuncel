# GO / NO GO Decision

## Decision

GO

## Basis For Decision

Credit Stress is:

- scientifically recognized
- theoretically grounded
- distinct from existing completed constructs
- operationally measurable in principle
- practically important for financial conditions and risk management
- sufficiently mature in the literature to justify a full LR stage

## Conditions

The GO decision authorizes only CRD-001 / LR-001.

CD-001 must not begin until LR-001 identifies candidate definitions, measurement approaches, limitations, and research gaps.

## Explicit Non-Claims

This decision does not claim that credit stress predicts returns.

This decision does not claim that credit stress has economic value.

This decision does not select any credit-spread series.

This decision does not approve any production use.
