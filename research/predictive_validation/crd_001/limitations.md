# PV-001 Limitations

## Short Effective Sample

The effective CRD-001 predictive sample is limited to the available 2024-2026 normalized observations. This restricts cross-period and long-horizon validation.

## Source Coverage

CRD-001 currently uses the available FRED graph output for BAMLH0A0HYM2. Longer historical validation would require a broader reliable source snapshot.

## No Model Fitting

PV-001 intentionally does not fit predictive models or optimize thresholds.

## No Economic Test

PV-001 does not evaluate trading profitability, alpha generation, portfolio performance, Sharpe ratio, CAGR, economic value, or production deployment.
