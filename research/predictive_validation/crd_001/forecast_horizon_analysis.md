# Forecast Horizon Analysis

## Horizons

- 5 trading days
- 20 trading days
- 60 trading days

## Output

Detailed horizon metrics are written to:

```text
forecast_horizon_metrics.csv
```

## Assessment

The horizon analysis evaluates whether predictive relationships are directionally consistent across short and medium horizons. Because the available sample is short, horizon stability is considered limited evidence rather than broad historical validation.
