# BRD-001 / PV-001: Cross-Period Validation

## Purpose

Evaluate whether predictive associations remain directionally stable across broad historical periods.

## Findings

Future realized volatility:

- Direction was consistently negative across all four period blocks at both horizons.
- Lower breadth was associated with higher future realized volatility.

Future trend deterioration:

- Direction was consistently negative across all four period blocks at both horizons.
- Lower breadth was associated with higher future trend deterioration risk.

Future drawdown risk:

- Direction was mixed across period blocks.
- Pooled evidence is supportive, but cross-period stability is weaker.

Future returns:

- Direction was consistently negative or weakly negative.
- This does not support the preregistered hypothesis that higher breadth predicts higher future returns.

## Classification

| Hypothesis | Cross-Period Classification |
| --- | --- |
| Future realized volatility | Supported by evidence |
| Future trend deterioration | Supported by evidence |
| Future drawdown risk | Partially supported |
| Future returns | Not supported |

## Boundary

Cross-period validation is predictive-statistical only.

It does not evaluate trading profitability or economic utility.
