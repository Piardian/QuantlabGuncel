# Executive Summary

COR-001 / PV-001 evaluated whether the Market Correlation construct contains predictive information about future market risk variables.

Overall result:

```text
Partially supported
```

Supported by evidence:

- future realized market volatility
- future market breadth deterioration
- future market correlation persistence

Partially supported:

- future drawdown risk

The strongest result was future correlation persistence. High COR-001 states were followed by higher future average COR-001 values at both 20-day and 60-day horizons.

Future realized volatility and breadth deterioration were also supported across both horizons and historical periods.

Future drawdown risk showed expected pooled direction, but rank correlation and cross-period stability were weak.

No trading, alpha, profitability, portfolio-performance, economic value, or production-deployment claim is made.

COR-001 is ready for EV-001 after human approval.
