# PV-001 Limitations

## Predictive Validity Only

PV-001 evaluates statistical predictive information.

It does not evaluate trading profitability, alpha, portfolio performance, Sharpe ratio, CAGR, or economic value.

## No Strategy

No trading strategy, portfolio simulation, hedge rule, sizing rule, or execution model was tested.

## Fixed Current Universe

COR-001 and BRD-001 use a fixed current universe, which may introduce survivorship bias.

## Data Source

Yahoo Finance data limitations remain.

## Overlap With Other Constructs

COR-001 may overlap with VOL-001, BRD-001, LIQ-001, or MR-001.

PV-001 does not estimate independent incremental predictive contribution versus those constructs.

## Drawdown Limitation

Future drawdown risk evidence is weaker and period-unstable.

## No Causality

Predictive association does not establish causality.
