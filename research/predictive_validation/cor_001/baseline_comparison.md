# Baseline Comparison

## Purpose

Compare COR-001 predictive evidence against a simple no-association baseline.

## Null Model

The null hypothesis is that COR-001 has no relationship with the future risk outcome.

The study used:

- fixed-seed permutation tests for rank correlation
- high-versus-low state mean differences
- event classification AUC against unconditional risk-event labels

## Results

Supported by evidence:

COR-001 rejected the no-association null for:

- future realized volatility at 20d and 60d
- future breadth deterioration at 20d and 60d
- future correlation persistence at 20d and 60d

Partially supported:

Future drawdown risk showed expected high-minus-low differences, but rank-correlation and period-stability evidence were weak.

## Boundary

This baseline comparison is statistical only.

It does not compare investment returns, portfolio performance, or economic outcomes.
