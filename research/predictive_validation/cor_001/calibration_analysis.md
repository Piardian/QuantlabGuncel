# Calibration Analysis

## Purpose

Evaluate whether future risk-event rates change monotonically or directionally across COR-001 percentile quintiles.

## Main Observations

Supported by evidence:

Future realized volatility event rates were higher in the top COR-001 quintile than in the bottom quintile at both horizons.

Supported by evidence:

Future breadth deterioration event rates were higher in the top COR-001 quintile than in the bottom quintile at both horizons.

Supported by evidence:

Future correlation persistence event rates rose strongly across COR-001 quintiles.

Partially supported:

Future drawdown risk event rates were directionally higher in the top COR-001 quintile in pooled data, but calibration was less stable than the other targets.
