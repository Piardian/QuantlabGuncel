# Forecast Horizon Analysis

## Purpose

Compare COR-001 predictive evidence across the 20-day and 60-day forecast horizons.

## Horizon Summary

| Hypothesis | 20d Classification | 60d Classification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 Future realized volatility | Supported by evidence | Supported by evidence |
| H2 Future drawdown risk | Partially supported | Partially supported |
| H3 Future breadth deterioration | Supported by evidence | Supported by evidence |
| H4 Future correlation persistence | Supported by evidence | Supported by evidence |

## Interpretation

Supported by evidence:

The strongest and most stable signal is future correlation persistence.

Supported by evidence:

Future realized volatility and future breadth deterioration remain supported at both horizons.

Partially supported:

Future drawdown risk preserves the expected pooled direction but lacks stable rank-correlation and cross-period support.

## Boundary

Forecast horizon analysis evaluates statistical predictive information only.

It does not evaluate trading performance or economic value.
