# Testing & Research Validation

QuantLab validation is intended to make research behavior reproducible and assumptions visible.

## Validation areas

- Configuration parsing
- Data-layer behavior
- Strategy execution
- Portfolio/execution simulation
- Metrics calculation
- Output generation

## Research evidence

Backtest metrics should always be interpreted together with the data source, date range, configuration, strategy version, and execution assumptions used to generate them.

A successful backtest is evidence about a historical experiment. It is not evidence of guaranteed future performance.
